## THROUGHOUT VIDEO

**FIX THE AUDIO — THIS IS THE #1 ISSUE**

- The talking-head audio has an echo / hollow sound. The cause: the camera files' left and right channels are two DIFFERENT microphones. The RIGHT channel is the lav mic on Dan's chest; the LEFT is a room mic about 9 feet away. Mixing both together creates the echo (comb filter), and no EQ or plugin can fix it after they're mixed. (Measured on this export: echo peak at ~7.0–7.3 ms in every speech section; both output channels identical, so the editor summed the two mics before export.)
- The fix: re-import every talking-head camera file and use the RIGHT channel only, as mono. In Premiere: right-click clip → Modify → Audio Channels → Mono, from the Right channel only.
- Do NOT try to EQ or denoise the current mix — the voice must be rebuilt from the source files.
- Master to −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1.5 dB.

**ADD A MUSIC BED AND SOUND EFFECTS**
- Music bed under the entire video, ~20 dB under the voice.
- Whoosh/pop SFX on text-graphic entrances/exits.

**TIGHTEN THE PACING**
- Remove every pause longer than ~0.3s. Zero dead air (reference edit had none).

**ADD TEXT GRAPHICS IN THE BRAND COLOR SCHEME**
- Current cut has zero text graphics. Brand scheme (see YouTube Shorts covers):
  - Background: black (or very dark brand green #162118)
  - Header text: large, dark green, ALL CAPS (brand olive-green #8C9858 so it reads on black)
  - Body text: off-white #E9EEDE
  - Highlight boxes/bars: green; red #E22222 reserved for attention accents only
  - Font: Manrope (Bold for headers)
- Title Capitalization on all text graphics.

**AI DISCLOSURE LABELS**
- "*AI Generated" on EVERY AI image/clip, full duration.
- Fix the typo: several labels read "AI GENERATE D" (space before D), e.g. 0:24–0:30.
- Full-screen AI clips: label UPPER-LEFT, ~50% larger. Small panel inserts keep centered bottom tag.

**COMPLIANCE HARD RULES (Google Ads)**
- Never side-by-side before/after (AI or not). Before → footage → after separately is OK, disclosed.
- No morph/transformation shots (same body changing in one continuous shot).
- Never negatively portray fat/out-of-shape people; no belly-fat zooms.
- Never show an email-capture form.

**VERTICAL (9:16) PHOTOS AND CLIPS**
- No plain black pillarboxing (currently 0:00–0:13, 1:06–1:08, 2:38–2:44). Brand-style framed card or blurred-fill background.

**PUNCH-INS ON THE TALKING HEAD**
- Alternate ~50% and ~70% closer zooms, cutting on phrase boundaries.

**CASTING RULE FOR ANY "GOAL BODY" PERSON**
- White or Asian man, 30–50, shredded-not-bulky. No mass monsters, no unmanly outfits.
