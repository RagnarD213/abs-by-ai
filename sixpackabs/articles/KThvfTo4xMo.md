Spiderman planks train your core and obliques, and all you need is the floor. Start in a plank on your elbows, push up into a push-up position, come back down, and stay perfectly straight the whole time.

Listen: this exercise lives or dies on staying straight. Here's how to do it right, and how to make every rep harder once you've got it down.

## How to do a Spiderman plank

1. **Get into a plank on your elbows.** Don't put your butt way up in the air, and don't let it sag. Like the name says, you're a plank: perfectly straight.
2. **Push up into a push-up position.** Keep staying perfectly straight as you go up.
3. **Come back down to your elbows.** Hold the tension in your transverse abdominis and keep staying perfectly straight.
4. **Repeat.** In my workout, I do 10 reps.

## Make it harder: add a vacuum

If you're a little more advanced, add a vacuuming motion. As you plank, keep your stomach sucked in to focus on your transverse abdominis. Suck it in, go up keeping it sucked in, and go down keeping it sucked in.

From the outside, you can't really see any difference. But it makes each rep more challenging. If you've never done one, here's [how to do a stomach vacuum](/videos/how-to-do-a-stomach-vacuum-to-shrink-your-waist/).

## What Spiderman planks work

Spiderman planks target your core and your obliques. The core muscle doing most of the work is your transverse abdominis: the sleeve of muscle beneath your six pack that holds everything together. When you do a plank, it's what enables you to hold it. When you do a squat and need to stay stable, it's what holds it all together.

Your obliques are the muscles on your sides that handle twisting and bending. Most ab workouts I used to do only worked the six pack in front. Spiderman planks train the muscles it misses.

## Where Spiderman planks fit in my workout

Spiderman planks are the third and last exercise in my [1 minute ab workout](/videos/1-minute-ab-workout-that-hits-all-4-ab-muscle-groups-at-home/), after toe touches and V-sit twists. I do 10 reps of each exercise, one time through the series. You can do it at home, it's super quick, and most of you won't even need any equipment. Between the three exercises, it hits all the major muscle groups in your abs.

Stay perfectly straight, keep your stomach sucked in, and you'll feel your whole core working on every rep.
