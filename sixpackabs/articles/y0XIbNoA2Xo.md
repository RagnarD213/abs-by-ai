The trick that finally stopped my late-night snacking is sugar-free gum. I set an alarm to stop eating an hour before bed, and when it goes off I start chewing gum. After half an hour of chewing my jaw is too tired to want food, and my whole mouth tastes like mint.

## My weak spot: salty snacks late at night

My biggest diet problem has always been salty snacks late at night, especially after a couple of drinks. That's when the willpower goes.

So I told my AI nutritionist about it in detail: what I eat, when, and why. It gave me strategies built for that exact problem, and a few of them helped me more than anything a human nutritionist ever told me.

## The alarm alone didn't work

I'd tried a stop-eating time before. It didn't stick. An alarm tells you to stop, but it does nothing about the craving that shows up twenty minutes later.

## The gum is what made it work

AI suggested chewing sugar-free gum as soon as the alarm goes off. That's what made the difference, for two reasons:

- **Your jaw gets tired.** After chewing for half an hour or 45 minutes, I don't even feel like eating.
- **Your mouth tastes like mint.** Doritos on top of that minty flavor would taste kind of gross.

## How to set it up

1. **Keep the snacks out of the house.** That was the first thing AI told me. Make them something you only eat outside the house, on special occasions.
2. **Set a stop-eating alarm about an hour before bed.** Mine goes off at 11 p.m.
3. **Start chewing sugar-free gum the moment it goes off,** and keep going for 30 to 45 minutes.

That's why I chew gum every night, and why you should too if night is where your diet falls apart. If you also [skip breakfast and fast until 2 p.m.](/videos/why-i-skip-breakfast-every-single-day/), the alarm is what closes your eating window at night.
