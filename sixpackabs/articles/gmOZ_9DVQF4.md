Here are three ways to cut your alcohol down. If you're serious about getting lean, this is the fastest thing that you can fix. Talk to your doctor about a GLP-1 like Zepbound, never drink liquor, and when you do drink, only drink zero sugar drinks that taste bad.

## 1. Zepbound

I did not decide to drink less. The wanting to drink just left. On a GLP-1 medication like Zepbound, my desire for alcohol faded the same way my desire for junk food did.

Zepbound is a prescription drug, so it's a decision to make with your doctor, and I can't promise it works that way for everyone. But for me, I didn't have to fight myself to drink less, because I just didn't want it the way I used to. I put more of what I've learned on it in my [top 5 Zepbound tips to lose fat and keep your muscle](/videos/top-5-zepbound-tips-to-lose-fat-and-keep-your-muscle/).

## 2. Never drink liquor

When I say liquor, I mean shots and nights where you're drinking it straight. Liquor is how a two-drink night turns into a six-drink night. And it is the only alcohol you can put down fast enough to get drunk before your body warns you.

## 3. Only drink zero sugar drinks that taste bad

When you do drink, only drink zero sugar alcohol drinks that taste bad. Plain hard seltzer, vodka and soda, or clear rum and Diet Coke.

Yes, vodka and rum are liquor. But here it's mixed into one tall drink, not thrown back as shots, and that's the difference between this and rule number two.

Not only are you consuming less calories and sugar, but you'll consume less alcohol too if you hate the taste. You're not going to sit there and keep ordering a drink you don't even like.

## What it did for me

Cutting down alcohol made a huge difference for me. And I can still enjoy a drink every now and then, I just drink far less than I used to.
