Do three sets of the standing stomach vacuum, 20 seconds each, with 30 seconds of rest between sets. Use a phone timer, keep taking short breaths through your nose during the hold, and take deep belly breaths during the rest.

This is the follow-along version. If you want the full explanation of why I recommend the vacuum, watch my video on the best ab exercise for belly fat first, then come back here and do the sets with me.

## The vacuum workout

1. Stand in front of a mirror and set your phone timer for 20 seconds.
2. Start the timer and hold the vacuum for the full 20 seconds.
3. Rest for 30 seconds.
4. Repeat for a second set, rest again, then do a third set.

In the video I do all three sets live, so you can see what a set looks like and follow along with the timer.

## How to breathe during a set

You do not hold your breath. You will not be able to breathe deep while your stomach is pulled in, but you can take short, little breaths through your nose. I can still talk and still breathe normally while I hold a vacuum. That is what gives you the endurance to hold it for a long time, so keep breathing and do not run out of oxygen.

## How to breathe between sets

Your breathing is restricted during a set, so use the rest to recover. Take deep belly breaths and fully oxygenate yourself before you start the next set.

## When to do it

First thing in the morning is my recommended time. You are totally fasted, even if you do not do intermittent fasting, and that makes it the easiest time to do a vacuum.

## If you are a beginner

If you have never done a vacuum before, start with one set. When you wake up, look in the mirror and do a single 20 second timed vacuum. That one set alone makes a big difference.
