You are eating healthy. You are working out, but the scale will not move. And I will tell you right now what it is not. It is almost certainly not your metabolism. It is not your hormones, and it is not your genetics. You are eating more calories than you think. Below are the four ways you're doing it, the four hidden reasons why, and the simple system that fixes all of it.

## It's not your metabolism

People underestimate what they eat, often dramatically, sometimes by 40% or more. And I'm not calling you a liar. Untracked eating is invisible, even to the person doing it.

It was invisible to me too. I was 200 pounds at 5'7", and I would have told you with a straight face that I ate pretty well. You are not lying. You just cannot see it yet. So let me show it to you.

## The 4 calorie leaks

There are four leaks. Almost everybody reading this has at least two of them.

### 1. You are not tracking your calories

Eyeballing your portions fails for almost everybody. And the excuse used to be fair because tracking meant weighing your food on a scale and typing it into an app every meal. Normal guys with jobs and kids were never going to do that.

That excuse is dead now. With AI, you take a photo of your plate and it gives you the calories and the macros. It takes two seconds. There is no longer any reason not to know. I go into this more in [you don't need a food scale anymore, just a photo](/videos/you-dont-need-a-food-scale-anymore-just-a-photo/).

### 2. Weekend blowouts

Five disciplined weekdays build you a deficit of maybe 2,500 calories. One big Friday dinner with drinks plus a lazy Saturday erases all of it.

This is why Monday to Friday dieters stay exactly the same weight for years. They are not failing. They are averaging out to zero. Every week they build the deficit, and every weekend they spend it.

### 3. Liquid calories

The coffee drinks, the juice, the smoothies, the protein shakes, the alcohol. You know my rule: if it has calories in it, do not drink it.

Liquid calories do not fill you up at all, so they are pure addition on top of everything else you eat that day. Plenty of people who feel like they eat very little are drinking a thousand calories a day, and they think those calories don't matter. When you're losing fat, you need every calorie you consume to fill you up.

### 4. Invisible calories

The cooking oil in the pan, the salad dressing, the sauces, the bites of your kids' food. The three chicken nuggets you eat standing over the trash can because you're just finishing the plate. None of it ever gets counted, and it adds up to hundreds of calories a day, every single day.

Cooking oil alone will get you. A tablespoon of olive oil is 120 calories, and nobody uses one tablespoon. You pour it straight from the bottle into the pan. That is a few hundred calories that never once crossed your mind, in a meal you would describe as chicken and vegetables.

400 invisible calories a day is 40 pounds in a year. That is the entire difference between the guy you are and the guy you want to be, and it is hiding in the stuff you do not think counts as eating.

## The 4 hidden reasons the leaks are there

Now let me tell you the real reasons the leaks are there. This is the part that nobody else will say out loud.

### 1. You have a fat girlfriend or a fat wife

If your partner is not dedicated to fitness herself, it is far, far harder for you to get in shape. She orders a dessert. She wants the pizza. She is the one who says you look fine and you do not need to do that. And eating down to your girlfriend's standards is one of the most common ways that otherwise dedicated men fail.

So you have to decide. Do you want the body and the health that you want, or are you going to sacrifice that?

You will literally die sooner, and your life will be massively worse in many ways, if you accept a partner who does not take care of her health. You will be less attracted to her. She will have less energy. You will spend your 50s and 60s managing her health problems instead of living. And since she's the single biggest influence on you, you'll probably develop obesity-related health problems yourself.

Because of that, I do not think it is ever worth settling for a girlfriend or a wife who does not care about her health.

### 2. You have fat friends

Same problem. Resisting their pressure to overeat is extremely difficult, and they will absolutely apply that pressure, because your discipline makes them feel bad about themselves. The guy ordering a salad at the table ruins the night for everyone who wanted to pretend it was a normal Tuesday.

So decide. Separate from them, or sacrifice your fitness goals. Those are the two options. There is not a third one where you get to keep going out four nights a week with guys who drink 12 beers and you also get abs.

### 3. You are not on a GLP-1

If you have plateaued for months and you are not happy with where you are at, you have two choices. You keep doing exactly what you are doing, and you probably never get there. Or you take tirzepatide or another GLP-1.

I'm not a doctor, and you do need to talk to a doctor before making the final decision. But Zepbound has helped me get abs, and it is the single most impactful thing you can do to lose fat faster.

### 4. Bad sleep

Poor sleep drains the exact willpower you need to say no. If you sleep five hours, your diet is fighting uphill from the moment you wake up. You cannot out-discipline four hours of sleep.

And I'm not going to sit here and pretend sleep is some magic fat loss secret, because it is not. It's not going to melt your belly. What it does is decide how much fight you have when the moment comes.

On 7 hours, you say no to the donut without really thinking about it. On 5 hours, you stand there and negotiate with yourself, and eventually you lose, because you are exhausted and you have been losing arguments with yourself all day.

## My simple system

All right, so here is my simple system, and it fixes almost all of this.

1. **Fast until 2 PM.** The deficit gets built into your day automatically, before you have to make a single decision. And drink 2 to 4 cups of black coffee during your fast to kill your appetite. I explain how I do it in [why I skip breakfast every single day](/videos/why-i-skip-breakfast-every-single-day/).
2. **Track everything with a photo.** Every meal, every drink, no exceptions. Especially on the weekend. The weekend is the whole ballgame.
3. **Weigh yourself daily.** Watch the 7-day trend and ignore single-day readings. One day means nothing. The line over a week means everything. More on that in [weigh yourself every single day, here's why](/videos/weigh-yourself-every-single-day-heres-why/).
4. **Spend less time with the people pulling you down.** You do not have to announce it. Just go less.
5. **Protect your sleep like it is part of your diet, because it is.** Pick a bedtime and defend it the same way you defend your gym time.

Notice that not one of them is a new workout program, and not one of them is a supplement. Because your problem is not that you do not know enough. You have watched 100 hours or more of fitness content. Your problem is that nothing in your day is measured, so nothing in your day is real.
