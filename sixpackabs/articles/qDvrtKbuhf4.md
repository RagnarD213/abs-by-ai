If you want to get a pump before you take your shirt off, all you need is two minutes and a pair of dumbbells. Do 30 seconds of bicep curls, 30 seconds of side lateral raises, and then 30 seconds of overhead tricep extensions. Just take two minutes, do that, and you'll look visibly pumped and better when you take your shirt off.

## When I use it

This workout is great to do before a photo shoot or before an event. This is what I personally do before I shoot photos, and before I go to something like a pool party.

## The 2 minute round

So here's what I recommend. You're going to do three exercises back to back, and then you're going to take a break.

1. Bicep curls, 30 seconds
2. Side lateral raises, 30 seconds
3. Overhead tricep extensions, 30 seconds
4. Rest, 30 seconds

That's one round. That's the whole two minutes.

## Why a timer and not reps

Now, you'll notice I didn't give you a number of reps. Like with most home workouts, I recommend timed sets rather than counting reps. So you're not counting anything here. You're just going for the full 30 seconds on each exercise.

The reason for that is timed sets are going to encourage greater time under tension. And time under tension is a more important factor than reps in building muscle.

## How many rounds

So after you take your break for 30 seconds, if you're feeling up to it, do two or three more rounds. It depends on what you're looking for.

- If you just want a quick pump, one round is good.
- If you're looking for a quick home workout, then do three or four rounds.
