## The catch with the ab shock belt

Shocking yourself hard enough to build your abs hurts more than training does. That's the catch. A light buzz on your stomach isn't the thing that develops your abs. To actually get results, you have to turn it up to the point where it's painful.

So the belt doesn't save you the work. And saving the work is the whole reason anyone buys it in the first place. If you're buying an ab belt, it's because you don't want to do ab exercises. But you're still putting in the work. It just feels worse.

## It does nothing about belly fat

The bigger issue for most guys isn't their ab muscles. It's the belly fat sitting on top of them. And the belt does nothing about that.

Fat does not come off one spot of your body. You can't shock the fat off your stomach, the same way you can't crunch it off your stomach. So if there's fat over your abs, you won't see them with a shock belt or without one.

That's the real issue for most guys. Building your abs is great. But if they're covered up, nobody is going to see them, including you.
