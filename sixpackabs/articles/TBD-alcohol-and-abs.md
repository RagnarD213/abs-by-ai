Can you drink alcohol and still have six pack abs? Yes, but only if you put rules and restrictions on your drinking. Break those rules and alcohol will quietly destroy your progress, even if everything else in your life is perfect. Your training can be perfect, your macros can be perfect, and you can still be standing in the mirror in six months wondering why nothing changed.

Here is what alcohol actually does to your body, my 6 rules for drinking without losing your abs, and the honest truth about where I landed myself.

## What alcohol really does

Most guys think alcohol is just some extra calories. It is a lot worse than that.

1. **It is pure liquid calories.** Alcohol has 7 calories per gram, which is almost as dense as straight fat, with zero nutrition and zero fullness. My rule on liquid calories is simple: if it has calories, do not drink it. Alcohol is the worst offender in that whole category, because at least soda is only sugar.
2. **It demolishes your sleep.** Alcohol knocks you out faster, but falling asleep quickly is not high quality sleep. I can see it on my Oura ring every single time I drink: deep sleep collapses and my heart rate stays elevated all night. Bad sleep means worse recovery, more hunger the next day and less willpower to say no to anything.
3. **It lowers your inhibitions.** Nobody has ever broken their diet on a salad. The late night drunk eating does more damage than the drinks themselves. You did not eat 2,000 calories at midnight because you were hungry. You ate it because the part of your brain that says no was turned off four drinks ago.
4. **It suppresses your recovery and your testosterone.** Your body treats alcohol as a poison, and everything else gets put on hold until it is gone. That includes building the muscle you just trained for. A heavy weekend of drinking can wipe out an entire week of hard training and dieting.

## My 6 rules for drinking and keeping your abs

### Rule 1: Eat first

Never drink during your fasting window, and never with your first meal of the day. Wait at least 30 minutes after you break your fast. Alcohol on an empty stomach absorbs faster and hits harder, so you get drunker on less and do more damage doing it. Eat first, wait, then drink. If you are new to fasting, here is [why I skip breakfast every single day](/videos/why-i-skip-breakfast-every-single-day/).

### Rule 2: Count the calories

A real night out is 1,000 calories or more, easily. That is an entire day's deficit gone in one evening. A lot of guys fail to lose their belly fat because they only count what they eat and not the beers, cocktails and liquor they drank. Those calories count whether you write them down or not. This is where an app does the work for you: take a photo of your drink and you are done. If you want the method, I covered it in [you don't need a food scale anymore, just a photo](/videos/you-dont-need-a-food-scale-anymore-just-a-photo/).

### Rule 3: Kill the sugary mixers

Cocktails and sugary mixed drinks are a double hit: alcohol and liquid sugar at the same time. Go for dry wine or spirits with soda water and lime. My test is that when you drink alcohol, it should taste bad. If it tastes bad, it is not sugary, and bad tasting drinks have a second benefit: you drink less. Most guys do not have six margaritas because they wanted to be six drinks drunk. They had six because they tasted like dessert.

### Rule 4: Cap your nights

One drinking occasion a week, two at the absolute maximum. Not a nightly habit, and not a glass of wine every evening to take the edge off. The nightly glass is the one I want to call out, because people defend it the hardest. Two glasses a night is 14 glasses a week, somewhere around 1,700 calories, plus seven nights of degraded sleep, every week, forever. There is no version of that where you get abs. And be honest about what counts as an occasion. If you are skipping the Wednesday beers because there were only two, you are doing what you do with food: only counting the times you noticed.

### Rule 5: Stop 3 hours before bed

Your last drink should be at least three hours before you go to bed. This is the sleep protection rule. The further the alcohol is from bedtime, the more of it your body has already cleared and the less deep sleep it takes from you. It sounds annoying, but it is the single highest return rule on the list. Same number of drinks, same night out, you just front-load it and stop early. Drink water in between too. It does not cancel anything out, but it slows you down and keeps you from drinking six when you meant to drink three.

### Rule 6: Run your normal fast the next morning

This is where most guys actually lose, and it is not at the bar. The next morning you wake up feeling terrible, decide last night was a write-off and go get a big greasy breakfast. Then the whole day is gone, and then the whole weekend. One bad night is completely recoverable. A bad weekend is not. Wake up, drink your coffee and fast until 2 like normal, and the damage stays contained to one night.

## The honest truth

If you are actively cutting right now, or trying to get abs for the first time in your life, the real answer is to drink as little as possible until you get there. Every single drink slows you down, and there is no version of this where drinking makes the cut faster.

Once you are maintaining and you already have the body, moderate drinking inside these rules is completely compatible with keeping your abs. I drink myself, but far more moderately than I used to. I only drink one or two days a week, I do not drink liquor, and I follow all six rules.

## How I drink now

I used to drink 5 to 10 drinks a week, and most weeks it was closer to 10: one or two on a date, then a blowout party night once or twice a week with four to six. Those blowout nights are gone. I did not force myself, they just stopped appealing to me. Now when I go out I have one or two and I am done, and I genuinely do not feel like I am missing anything.

Taking Zepbound massively reduced my desire to drink alcohol. Realistically, I probably would not have had the willpower to cut down if I had not gone on it. If you are struggling to get your drinking under control, talk to your doctor about whether it makes sense for you.

## So can you drink and still have abs?

Yes. Fast first, count it, drink things that taste bad and are low in sugar, cap it at one or two nights a week and stop three hours before bed. And do not let one bad night become a weekend.
