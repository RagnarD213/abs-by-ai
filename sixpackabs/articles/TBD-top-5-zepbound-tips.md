Zepbound is the most powerful fat loss tool that has ever existed for normal people. But it is a tool, and it only gets you results if you use it the right way.

The short version: use a vial and a needle instead of a pen, inject into the outer front of your thigh, take your shot on Thursday evening, ramp on slowly and taper off even slower, and guard your protein. I have been on Zepbound for about two months and it has changed my life. I also screwed some things up when I started, and a lot of other people screw up the same things. Here is what I'd do from day one.

## Tip 1: Use needles and vials, not pens

There are two ways to inject Zepbound: fill a needle from a vial, or use a pen or auto-injector. Almost everyone starts with the pen, because that is what gets handed to you.

The problem with the pen is that it's harder to get a dose outside the standard doses. With a vial and a needle, adjusting your dose precisely is much easier. That matters. I have found that a dose as small as 1 milligram can be beneficial for people who are already in excellent shape, and that is less than half of what the pen gives you.

What I did was start at 1 milligram, then step up to 2, then to 2 and a half. That gradual ramp avoids nearly all of the side effects that come from stepping up your dose: the nausea, the fatigue, the days where you feel like garbage. Most of that is the jump, not the drug. Once you've lost the weight, you can dial it down to maintain. I'm currently taking 1.5 milligrams a week, and it's a great long-term maintenance dose for me.

Here's the part nobody believes until they do it: once you get good with a needle, it is less painful than the pen. The pen fires the drug at its own speed. You control the needle.

This is also the part that scares people off, so let me be practical. You are not doing surgery. You get a vial and insulin syringes, which are the tiny ones. Your dose is measured in units on the side of the barrel. Your doctor or your pharmacy will tell you exactly how many units equal a milligram for the concentration you have, and you pull back to that line. That is the entire skill. If you can read a measuring cup, you can do this.

## Tip 2: How to actually inject

This takes about 10 seconds once you know what you're doing.

1. Pick the spot: your thigh, on the outer front part, right around the middle. Not the inside of your leg, and not down by your knee.
2. Pinch the fat. You want a good fold between your fingers, because this is a subcutaneous injection. It goes into fat, not into muscle.
3. Stick the needle in, not too deep. This is a tiny, short needle, nothing like the ones used to draw blood.
4. Inject slowly. Do not slam it in.
5. Wait one second after it is all gone, then withdraw.

Here's how you grade yourself. A good stick is nearly painless and there is no blood. If you bled, or it actually hurt, change something next time: you went too deep, you were in the wrong spot, or you pushed the plunger too fast.

Two more things. Rotate your sites: left thigh one week, right thigh the next. If you hit the same square inch every week, you will eventually get a lump of hardened tissue there, absorption gets unpredictable, and you start wondering why the dose stopped working. And if you're nervous the first couple of times, hold an ice cube on the spot for 30 seconds to numb it. You won't need it after the second or third time.

## Tip 3: Inject on Thursday evening

This one is free, and almost no one thinks about it. The medication is strongest in the first few days after your shot and fades toward the end of the week. So the question is: which days need the most help?

For me, and for basically every guy I've talked to about this, the answer is the weekend. Monday through Thursday, I am dialed in. Friday night, Saturday and Sunday are where diets go to die. That is the dinner out, the drinks, the pizza with the family, the afternoon with nothing to do when you eat out of boredom.

If you inject Thursday evening, your appetite suppression is at full strength Friday night through Sunday. The drug is working hardest exactly when your willpower is working worst. If you inject on a Monday, you spend your strongest days on the days you were going to win anyway.

Whatever day you pick, keep it the same every week. The level in your body is supposed to stay steady. And if you're switching to Thursday from another day, do not double up. Just take your next shot on Thursday, even if that means waiting a few extra days one time.

## Tip 4: Ramp on slowly, and taper off even slower

Coming on slowly is the ramp from tip 1. Coming off is where I see people destroy everything they built. I have watched multiple people lose a huge amount of fat on Zepbound, quit cold, and regain almost all of it within a few months. Then they think the medication failed them. It did not. They just stopped the wrong way.

Here is the process I use instead:

- Lower your dose from 2.5 milligrams to 2. If you don't gain any weight, go down to 1.5.
- Keep lowering by half a milligram per week as long as you stay at or below your goal weight.
- If you go above your goal weight, even by a tenth of a pound, stay at your current dose that week.
- If you gain more than three pounds above your goal weight, go back up half a milligram.

That is it. You walk the dose down and let the scale tell you when to stop. Done this way, you can taper all the way off over a couple of months and hold your new body the entire time. Walk down the stairs instead of jumping off the roof. ## Tip 5: Focus on protein

Zepbound reduces your appetite, so eating fewer calories gets easier. But studies have shown the average person loses a lot of muscle on this medication. You can avoid that by eating enough protein and weight training regularly. I have actually gained muscle and strength on Zepbound because of this.

Hitting your protein is harder on this medication, because you're not hungry and food stops being interesting. If you're winging it, you might not get enough. So track your macros and hit your protein every day. Aim for at least 0.8 grams of protein per pound of lean body mass. In real life that is a serious serving of protein with lunch and dinner, around 8 ounces each, plus protein-focused snacks in between: rotisserie chicken, sardines, jerky, eggs. Your snacks should be protein, not carbs. Tracking is easy now: [you don't need a food scale anymore, just a photo](/videos/you-dont-need-a-food-scale-anymore-just-a-photo/).

The failure here is not obvious. If you under-eat protein, the scale still goes down and it feels like it's working. What you don't see until the end is that a big piece of what you lost was muscle. You hit your goal weight, take your shirt off, and you are smaller but soft. That is the real risk of this medication for a guy who has spent years building muscle.

Needles instead of pens. Inject correctly into the outer thigh. Inject Thursday evening. Ramp on slow and taper off slower. Guard your protein. If you take this medication, eat badly and never lift, you end up skinny, weak and still unhappy with the mirror.
