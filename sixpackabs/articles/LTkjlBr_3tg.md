Jump rope is the best cardio you can do at home, but most people are doing it wrong. Stop hopping over the rope with both feet. Skip over it one foot at a time, like a boxer, starting slow and building up to full speed.

Listen: this takes a few minutes to learn, not weeks. Here's exactly how to go from tripping over the rope to skipping rope like a boxer.

## Why you keep tripping over the rope

Most people jump rope by hopping over it with two feet on every turn. That's more impact on your joints, and it makes it harder to push your cardio without catching the rope.

The other mistake is going for full speed on day one. Skipping one foot at a time is hard to do fast when you first start, so you trip over the rope. Slow it down first, and the speed comes with practice.

## How to skip rope like a boxer

1. **Don't hop with two feet.** Skip over the rope one foot at a time.
2. **Start slow.** Your first time, turn the rope slowly and skip over it one foot at a time.
3. **Speed up bit by bit.** Accelerate the rope gradually, a little at a time.
4. **Build to full speed.** Keep going until you're skipping over the rope one foot at a time at full speed.

Skipping one foot at a time is easier on your joints, and once you have the rhythm, it's much easier to push your cardio hard without tripping on the rope.

## How to use jump rope once you've got it

Once you're good at jump roping, there are two easy ways to fit it into your day:

- **Two minutes every morning** when you wake up, to get your blood pumping.
- **Two minutes before your workout** for a quick cardio warm-up.
