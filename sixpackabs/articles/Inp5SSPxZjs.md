Stop cooking your own meals and use a meal prep service instead. A meal prep service saves you 2 to 3 hours a week, it puts the calories right on the label, and it costs about $8 a meal, which is less than Chipotle. Then take those hours and put them into working out.

The problem with cooking isn't the food. The problem is that cooking eats up the time you could be training.

## The hidden cost of cooking

When most people think about cooking, they only think about the cooking part. But that's not the whole thing. You have to drive to the store, shop, drive home, cook, and then wash the dishes. That whole chain takes a lot of time.

Survey data puts the average American at around 5 hours a week on all of that. That's 5 hours a week you're not working out.

## How a meal prep service works

With a meal prep service, your meals show up already cooked and already portioned. All you do is this:

1. **Heat it.** 2 minutes in the microwave.
2. **Eat it.**
3. **Done.** Throw the container away.

No cooking. No dishes. That's it.

## Tracking calories gets easier

Every meal comes with the calories and the macros (protein, carbs and fat) on the label. So there's no guessing and no weighing your food. You just read the label.

This matters a lot, because losing belly fat comes down to calories. If tracking your calories is easy, you're actually going to do it.

## What it costs

A meal prep service runs about $8 a meal, and a lot of these services deliver right to you. That's less than ordering Chipotle. And it's barely more than cooking yourself, once you count your time.
