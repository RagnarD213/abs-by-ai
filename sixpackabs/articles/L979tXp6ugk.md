Hamstring curls are the key to building well-balanced, aesthetic legs. Line your knees up with the machine's hinge, pull your toes toward you, curl the weight as far as it goes and squeeze for a second at the top. Then take two to three seconds on the way down and explode back up.

## Set up the machine first

Before you do a single rep, line your knees up with the hinge of the machine. That's the first thing you want to get right.

Then set the range of motion so you start with the stretch. You want to feel a stretch in your hamstrings at the bottom of the rep. But not so much of a stretch that your legs hyperextend. So set it where you feel the stretch in your hamstrings, and no further than that.

## How to do a hamstring curl

1. **Pull your toes toward you** to flex your hamstrings. Do this on every rep, not just the first few.
2. **Curl the weight as far as it goes.**
3. **Squeeze for a second at the top.**
4. **Lower it slowly**, taking two to three seconds on the way down.
5. **Explode back up.**

So pull your toes in, squeeze at the top, and take your time on the way down. Do them this way and you get a lot more out of every set.

## Sets and reps

1. **One really easy warm-up set** of 10 to 15 reps.
2. **Two working sets** of 8 to 12 reps.

The warm-up set should be really easy. The two working sets are where you push it.

Every working rep should be strict. Do as much weight as you can for as many reps as you can, and then stop once you can't do another clean rep. Don't keep grinding out reps once your form starts to go. Stop before your form breaks.
