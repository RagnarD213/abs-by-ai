The fastest way to lose your love handles is to lower your total body fat. Side bends and twists cannot burn the fat on your sides.

I know that's not the immediate solution people want. But if you're still trying to work off your love handles with oblique exercises, you need to understand why that approach isn't working.

## Why your love handles go last

When you lose weight, the deep fat around your organs comes off first. In a review of 89 weight loss studies, that deep belly fat shrank faster than the fat you can pinch under your skin. And love handles are fat you can pinch.

Researchers have also found that the fat under the skin of an overweight person's belly gets less blood flow. That makes it slower to release its fat for you to burn.

For most men, the belly and the sides are the last places to lean out. Your face and your arms lean out first. That's why you can lose 20 pounds, see a thinner face in the mirror, and still have the same love handles.

So don't quit just because they're still there. This is the part where a lot of guys quit.

## Why side bends won't burn your love handles

I've repeated this many times, but I'm repeating it again because people are still using this failed approach. You can't spot reduce fat. Working the muscle under the fat doesn't burn the fat on top of it.

In one study, people did ab exercises five days a week for six weeks. Their belly fat didn't change, and neither did their waist size.

You're working the muscle. But the fat covering that muscle is still there. The way to lose that fat is to lower your body fat percentage across your entire body.

## 1. Track your calories and cut liquid calories

Snap a photo of your meal and let AI count the calories for you. That's what makes AI calorie tracking so useful. You can see how much you're eating.

And cut every liquid calorie. Soda, juice, sweet coffee, and alcohol. Those calories don't fill you up, so you end up eating the same amount of food on top of them.

## 3. Lift every day so you keep your muscle

Number three, lift every day. When you lose weight, you can lose muscle along with the fat. And if you lose muscle, you can end up skinny fat. You're smaller, but you're still soft and you still have the same love handles.

That's not the result you want. You want to lose the fat and keep the muscle. Lifting every day tells your body to keep that muscle while the weight comes off.

Otherwise, you lose the muscle, then you have to bulk up to get the muscle back. And bulking adds fat, so now you're right back where you started. You're trying to lose the same fat again. So lift every day and avoid that whole cycle.

## 4. Weigh yourself every morning through the slow part

Number four, weigh yourself every morning. The last stretch of fat loss is slow, and it's the part where most guys quit.

You look in the mirror and your love handles look the same. You think what you're doing isn't working. But when you weigh yourself every morning, you can see the trend going down even on the weeks the mirror looks the same. That keeps you motivated through the slow part.

Pick one of these four things you're not doing yet and start it this week. You already know what you need to do. Now you need to actually start doing it.
