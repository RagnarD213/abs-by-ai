These are the five muscles that are the most important for attracting women: your shoulders, your upper chest, your arms, your back and your abs. And none of these five muscles needs a barbell. You can build every one of them on a machine or a cable, and I'm going to show you exactly which one builds each muscle.

## Why looks come first

Looks are the foundation of attraction. How you look shirtless decides how women see you. On the beach, on Instagram, and most importantly, on dating apps.

She might tell you she wants a guy who's funny and nice and who treats her well. But on a dating app, she decides in a couple of seconds off your pictures, before she ever finds out if you're funny or if you have a good personality. So here's exactly which muscles are important.

## 1. Your shoulders (side delts)

Number one is your shoulders, specifically your side delts. These are the muscles on the outside of your shoulders. When your side delts are wide, your waist looks smaller, and that's what gives you the V shape. So if you want that V shape, a V-taper physique, then go ahead and start with your side delts.

The best way to build your side delts is with the side lateral machine and [dumbbell side laterals](/videos/make-your-waist-look-smaller-with-side-lateral-raises/). On both of those, you're going to raise your arms to about shoulder height and no higher. Shoulder height, and that's it.

And go slow on the way down. Don't just let the weight drop. Lower it slowly every rep.

So that's number one. Side lateral machine, dumbbell side laterals, up to shoulder height and slow on the way down.

## 2. Your upper chest

Your upper chest is what fills out a T-shirt. If your upper chest is flat, then your T-shirt is going to hang off you. If your upper chest is built up, your T-shirt is going to look like it was made for you.

The best way to build your upper chest is with the incline dumbbell press and the chest fly machine.

On the incline dumbbell press, lower the dumbbells to just about chest level, then press them back up.

On the chest fly machine, set the seat so the handles are at chest height. Then bring the handles together and squeeze in front of your chest. That squeeze is what makes the exercise work. So don't rush it.

For your upper chest, it's the incline dumbbell press and the chest fly machine. Do those two and your T-shirts are going to fit a whole lot better.

## 3. Your arms

When you're wearing a T-shirt, your arms are the first thing she's going to see. Your shirt is covering everything else, but your arms are always right there. So if you want her to notice you in a T-shirt, you need to be building your arms.

The best way to build your arms is with cable curls, rope tricep pushdowns and preacher curls with the EZ bar.

On the cable curls and the rope pushdowns, keep your elbows pinned at your side. [Don't let them swing forward](/videos/stop-swinging-your-bicep-curls/). Elbows pinned the entire set.

And don't skip your triceps. A lot of guys only train their biceps, because that's the muscle they think about when they think about big arms. But your triceps are the bigger muscle, and they're most of your arm. So if you want big arms, you need to be training your triceps just as hard as your biceps.

## 4. Your back

A wide back is the V-taper. Your shoulders start the V shape, and your back finishes it.

The best way to build a wide back is with pull-ups. If you can't do a pull-up yet, that's fine. Do lat pulldowns instead.

The other exercise you need to be doing is a chest supported T-bar row. A wide grip is what makes your back wide.

So, pull-ups or lat pulldowns, and chest supported T-bar rows. That's your back.

## 5. Your abs

Your abs are the ones she notices most, and they're also the hardest to get. The other four muscles on this list, you can build in the gym and they'll show. Your abs are different.

To build your abs, do cable crunches and leg raises on the captain's chair. On the cable crunch, kneel facing the stack, then stick your butt out like you're twerking. I know that sounds weird, but trust me on this one. It'll help you get a better contraction.

And here's the thing about your abs. This is the part most guys get wrong. Cable crunches and leg raises build them, but losing your belly fat is what shows them. [Nutrition is the most important factor](/videos/nutrition-matters-more-than-your-workouts/) for revealing your abs. You can have the strongest abs in the gym and nobody will ever see them under a layer of fat.

So if you want her to notice your abs, then you have to do both. Build your abs in the gym with cable crunches and leg raises, and lose the belly fat that's hiding them with nutrition.

## The bottom line

None of these five muscles need a barbell. Not one of them. You can build every one of them on machines and cables.

Train all five every week, and get lean enough to show them, because building the muscle is only half of it. If you've got belly fat covering your abs, nobody is going to see them. So you have to do both.

## How to fit them in

Here's how you fit all five in:

- For every exercise, do one easy warm-up set of 10 to 15 reps.
- Then do two working sets of 8 to 12 reps. That's it. One easy set and two hard working sets.
- The warm-up set should feel easy. The working sets should be as much weight as you can do for 8 to 12 reps.
- Do them on the machines and the cables.
- Train total body every day.

So every week you're hitting your shoulders, your upper chest, your arms, your back and your abs. That might sound like a lot to you, but it's only two working sets per exercise, so you can do it. And if you're training total body every day, you never skip a muscle.

## The 5 muscles, one more time

1. Your shoulders: the side lateral machine and dumbbell side laterals.
2. Your upper chest: the incline dumbbell press and the chest fly machine.
3. Your arms: cable curls, rope tricep pushdowns and EZ bar preacher curls.
4. Your back: pull-ups or lat pulldowns, and the chest supported T-bar row.
5. Your abs: cable crunches and captain's chair leg raises. These are the ones women notice most.

Build these five muscles and get lean. If you do that, you're going to see the difference in how women look at you. On the beach, on Instagram, and on the dating apps. You're going to see it in your matches.
