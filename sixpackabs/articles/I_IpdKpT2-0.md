The toe touch is a killer six pack exercise you can do at home, and one mental trick makes it work much better. Lie flat, jackknife up, lift your head and butt off the ground, and picture kneeing yourself in the face on every rep.

Listen: I did this exercise for years before I made this breakthrough. It sounds a little weird, but it gets me a better contraction and better work on my rectus abdominis, the six pack muscle.

## How to do a toe touch

1. **Lie out flat on the mat.** Your feet and your toes are on the ground.
2. **Jackknife up.** At the end, lift your head and your butt off the ground.
3. **Picture kneeing yourself in the face,** forcefully, as you come up.
4. **Go back down to the bottom.** Each time you do that is one rep.
5. **Keep it slow and controlled.** In my workout, I do 10 reps.

## The trick: picture kneeing yourself in the face

Don't worry about actually kneeing yourself in the face. It's not possible. Nobody's really that flexible. But if you visualize kneeing yourself in the face forcefully on each rep, you make the right kind of contraction.

Down at the bottom, visualize it and get way up. Down to the bottom, visualize it, and get way up again. That's how you get a good contraction on every single rep.

## Start with no weight

For most of you, I recommend doing toe touches without any weight. Instead of adding weight, just use really good form. It should be hard enough for most people with no weight at all.

I use a 4-pound medicine ball because I've been doing this for a while. If you want to start with a little resistance, use 2 pounds, and if you want to go heavier than me, 6 or 8 pounds. I wouldn't go beyond 10 pounds, even if you're a bodybuilder, because your form suffers.

## Where toe touches fit in my workout

Toe touches target your lower abs and your rectus abdominis. They're the first exercise in my [1 minute ab workout](/videos/1-minute-ab-workout-that-hits-all-4-ab-muscle-groups-at-home/), followed by [V-sit twists](/videos/work-your-obliques-with-v-sit-twists/) and Spiderman planks, 10 reps of each. Doing toe touches first burns out your six pack muscle, so the V-sit twists focus more on your obliques.

Keep it slow, keep it controlled, and that's how you'll get the most out of this exercise.
