Cortisol belly is real, but the TikTok fixes for it, like the cortisol cocktail, won't take an inch off your waist. Stress and high cortisol are linked to belly fat, that part is true. But it's the extra food you eat because you're stressed that puts the fat on your belly, not the cortisol by itself.

So I'm going to show you what cortisol actually does to your belly and the six things that actually get rid of it. If you've been told that stress is the reason you can't lose your belly fat, you need to hear this.

## What TikTok gets right

Cortisol is your main stress hormone, and stress and high cortisol are linked to belly fat. That part is true. In a Yale study, women with more belly fat released more cortisol under stress than women without it.

So TikTok has that part right. But then TikTok tells you the fix is a drink.

## What TikTok gets wrong

Cortisol doesn't make belly fat out of nothing. Stress makes you eat more, and the extra calories land on your belly.

Let's say you were in a calorie deficit and super stressed out. You would still get leaner. And let's say you were eating in a calorie surplus, but you felt totally zen and mellow. You would still gain weight.

The problem is that in real life, stress makes you eat more calories, and this has been proven. In another Yale study, the people whose cortisol jumped the most under stress ate more calories after a stressful day than the people whose cortisol stayed calm, and they went for more sweets. On a calm day, the two groups ate the same.

So the stress isn't putting fat on your belly directly. It's the extra food you eat because you're stressed. If you're under massive stress but you're disciplined enough to stay in a calorie deficit, you will stay lean. If you have zero stress but you overeat anyways, you will gain fat.

## What's really in the cortisol cocktail

It's orange juice and coconut water with some salt. That's it. A typical one has about 80 calories and most of that is sugar, so you're adding calories when extra calories are the real cause of your belly fat. And sucking down a sugar drink like this won't do anything to reduce your cortisol either.

So skip the cortisol cocktail, and skip the adrenal supplements too. In my opinion they're both a waste of money.

## The 6 things that actually fix cortisol belly

So here's what actually fixes stress and stress related eating. There are six things.

### 1. Sleep

When you don't sleep, your cortisol goes up, you get hungrier and you make worse food choices. All three of those at the same time.

In a University of Chicago study, dieters who slept five and a half hours a night lost 55% less fat than when they slept eight and a half hours, on the exact same diet. Same food, same everything, and they lost a lot less fat just because they slept less.

So get at least seven hours a night in a cold room. I go deeper on this in [8 hours in bed is not 8 hours of sleep](/videos/8-hours-in-bed-is-not-8-hours-of-sleep/). When you sleep well, it's a lot easier to say no to junk food the next day.

### 2. Train every day, first thing in the morning

Training is one of the best ways there is to burn off stress. I work out first thing in the morning, seven days a week.

When I started working out first thing in the morning, I felt much less stress for the rest of the day. Because of that, I ate less junk food and fewer calories. I also did a lot less late night eating than when I used to work out in the evening.

That's why I train first thing every day, and why you should too.

### 3. Cut way back on alcohol

A lot of guys drink to deal with stress. But alcohol wrecks your sleep and adds calories at the same time, so it makes cortisol belly worse in two different ways.

I used to be a heavy drinker. I haven't quit totally, but I've cut way back. And since reducing my drinking, my sleep and my stress levels have massively improved.

This is the one that nobody else on YouTube will tell you. If your wife stresses you out all the time, divorce her. If your job stresses you out all the time, quit. If a family member stresses you out, cut them off until they change.

Now I know a lot of you guys are saying, oh, Dan, that's crazy, I can't divorce her just because she stresses me out. But what's truly crazy is sticking with a relationship or a job that is literally killing you. If you keep going down this path of stress and stress related overeating, you are going to die years earlier.

Hit your calories and your protein every day, no matter how you feel. Even if you had a bad day at work, or a fight with your girlfriend, hit your calories.
