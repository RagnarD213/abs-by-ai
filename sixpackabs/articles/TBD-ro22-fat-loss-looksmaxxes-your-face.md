Getting lean is by far the most powerful way to looksmax your face. If you want a sharper jawline, the first thing I would look at is how much body fat you're carrying. That was what made the difference for me. I didn't do a single face exercise. When the fat came off my belly, it came off my face too.

In the video, I show you what changed in my face after losing the fat. I also talk about the smaller things I do now. Those things matter, but getting lean comes first.

## What changed in my face

At 200 pounds, my face was puffy and bloated and I looked awful. I didn't have any jawline, just a sloppy double chin. You can see it in the real comparison photos in the video.

Today my face is a lot less puffy, and I have a defined jawline where there wasn't one before. I'm not the most looksmaxed guy in the world. But looking at those pictures, I think you can agree that my face looks much better after getting leaner.

And this is the part I want you to understand. I didn't make that transformation by spending all day working on my face. It came from losing body fat. I wanted to get leaner, and my face changed along with the rest of me.

If you're carrying extra fat and wondering why you don't have a defined jawline, look at that first.

## Face fat versus face hacks

There are a lot of things guys spend time on when they get interested in looksmaxxing. Jaw exercises, mewing, chewing gum, bone smashing. But none of these things burn face fat.

You can't spot reduce your face any more than you can spot reduce your belly. Doing something with your jaw doesn't mean the fat covering it is going to go away. For most guys carrying extra fat, that's the issue they need to deal with first.

I think it's very easy to get focused on the smaller details because you're thinking about your face. But if the main thing making your face look puffy is body fat, you need to put that time into your diet.

Once you're lean, then you can worry about the smaller details. I do some of those things myself now. I just wouldn't put them ahead of losing the fat.

## Your face matters, and your body matters too

Clavicular talks a lot about how much your face matters, and I partially agree with him. Most men totally neglect their face. I was one of them.

For most of my life, I didn't think about my face at all. I didn't start a skincare routine until a few years ago, and I didn't start getting my eyebrows done until a few years ago either. That was a big mistake.

Your face is the first thing people see. It's the first thing she sees on a dating app. Of course your face matters.

But I also think your body matters a lot more than Clavicular gives it credit for. Building muscle matters too. I don't think you need to choose between caring about your face and caring about your body. Getting leaner helped both for me. The fat came off my stomach, and my face looked better too.

## My three-step guide to a leaner-looking face

### Step one: Get lean enough to see your abs

This is the step that makes the biggest difference by far. Your face leans out with the rest of you. That's what happened to me, and it's where I would put my attention first.

Don't get so caught up in face hacks that you neglect the thing that made my transformation happen. Work on losing the body fat. You can see the change in my real photos in the video.

Read more about [why ab exercises do not burn belly fat](/videos/stop-doing-ab-exercises-until-you-can-see-your-abs/).

And remember that getting lean and building muscle both matter. I still care about my body. Taking care of your face doesn't mean you need to stop caring about the rest of you.

For one of my nutrition habits, see [how I make my daily salad](/videos/how-i-make-my-daily-salad-700-calories-4-a-bowl-fresh-for-7-days/).

### Step two: Cut back on alcohol and salty restaurant meals

The morning after a few drinks or a big salty restaurant meal, my face looks a lot puffier. Both can make your body hold water, and that can show up in your face.

So this is another thing I pay attention to. If you're looking at yourself the next morning and wondering why your face looks puffier, think about what you had the night before.

This is separate from losing the body fat, but it's part of what I talk about in the video because I notice it in my own face.

### Step three: Add the smaller extras

I use a 3% caffeine eye cream for puffiness under my eyes. I get my eyebrows threaded, and I get a good haircut.

None of these replace losing the fat. But once the fat is gone, each one makes my face look a little sharper. These are things I neglected for a long time, and now I pay attention to them.

If you want to looksmax your face, start with getting lean. That's what changed my face, without a single face exercise. Then worry about the smaller details.
