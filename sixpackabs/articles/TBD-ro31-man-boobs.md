## There are two kinds of man boobs

First, you need to know there are two kinds of man boobs, and they're not the same thing at all.

The first kind is chest fat. It's soft and it's spread across your whole chest. It comes from having too much body fat, the same as your belly. There's nothing special about it. It's just fat, and it's sitting on your chest instead of your stomach.

The second kind is true gynecomastia. That's a firm, rubbery disc of gland tissue right under your nipple. It's breast tissue, not fat.

Now, some men do truly suffer from this medical condition. I'm not saying it doesn't exist. But for the majority of men who think that they have man boobs, the issue is fat and not gland tissue. And that's good news, because fat comes off the same way your belly fat does.

## How to tell which one you have

So how do you tell which one you have? Feel around your nipple.

- If it's soft and spread out, it's fat.
- If you feel a firm disc right under the nipple, that's gland.

If you're not sure, it's probably fat. If you do truly have gynecomastia, you will feel a firm gland and you will definitely know that it's there. It's not something you're going to be wondering about.

If you want a diagnosis, a doctor can tell you in about a minute. So check yourself today so you know which fix you need, because the fix for fat and the fix for gland are not the same.

Let's start with the fix for chest fat, because that's what most of you guys have.

The first step is to lose your body fat. Your chest leans out with the rest of you. You can't spot reduce it any more than you can spot reduce your belly. So you're not going after your chest. You're going after your body fat, and your chest comes down with it.

## Step 2: build your chest

The second step is to build your chest. Push-ups and chest flies build the muscle under the fat, so the area turns into a ripped pec instead of a saggy man boob.

So you're doing two things at the same time. You're [losing the fat and building the muscle](/videos/how-to-keep-your-muscle-while-you-lose-fat/) that's underneath it. And as that fat comes off, what's left looks like a man's chest.

So add push-ups and chest flies to your workouts this week. Not next month. This week.

Pair this with Zepbound and reducing your calories, and you'll be shocked at how different your chest looks in a t-shirt within a few months.

## The fix for true gynecomastia

Now, let's talk about the fix for true gynecomastia. See a doctor first. True gynecomastia has a cause, and you want to know what it is.

Here's where it comes from:

- In about a quarter of cases, it's left over from puberty.
- In another quarter, nobody can find a cause.
- Up to a quarter, it comes from drugs or other substances. Anabolic steroids are one of the big ones, and the gynecomastia they cause often never goes away.
- Less often, it comes from low testosterone, a liver or thyroid problem, or very rarely, it can come from a tumor.

That's why you should see a doctor before you do anything else. You want to know what's causing it.

Now, diet won't remove gland tissue. You can get ripped and the disc will still be there. That's the big difference between this and chest fat. Chest fat comes off when you lose your body fat. Gland tissue doesn't. If it's been there a long time, surgery is the fix.

So if it's a firm disc, book the doctor instead of starting another diet.

But again, I want to remind you that most men who think they have man boobs don't have true gynecomastia. So don't assume the issue is gland tissue until you get a doctor's diagnosis. If you're not sure, it's probably fat, and you already know how to fix fat.

## Both fixes, one more time

So let's review both fixes.

If you have chest fat:

1. Lose your body fat with Zepbound, AI calorie tracking and cutting liquid calories.
2. Build your chest with push-ups and chest flies.

This is the fix for the vast majority of you guys.

If it's true gynecomastia:

1. See a doctor.
2. Find the cause.
3. If it's been there a long time, surgery is the fix.

Either way, you don't have to hide your chest under a loose shirt for the rest of your life.
