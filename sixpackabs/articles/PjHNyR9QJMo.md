There's a couple different ways you can do bicep curls, and both ways work. One arm at a time gives you a little more range of motion, and it's easier to keep good form. Both arms together is faster, and it keeps your two sides even.

## One arm at a time

First, you can do them arm by arm. Hold the dumbbells by your sides and curl one up. Then curl the other one up.

You're twisting your wrist as you curl. You start with your palm facing your side at the bottom. You end with your palm facing up at the top. That's the first way.

## Both arms at the same time

There's a second way that you can do it as well. I do it this way too, at the gym when I'm counting reps. Just hold both of them and curl both at the same time.

The advantage of this is that you'll be getting the work done quicker. You'll also be less vulnerable to one side being done differently than the other side, which is what might happen when you're doing it one arm at a time. So it's quicker and your two sides stay even.

The disadvantage of this is you'll get a little bit less of a range of motion. And some people who are just starting off find it hard to keep good form this way.

## Which one should you do

Try it out both ways. Whichever way works the best for you is perfectly valid. You're not doing it wrong if you pick one over the other.
