If you want to make your waist look smaller, you need bigger shoulders. And the way you build bigger shoulders is with side lateral raises.

## Why your shoulders change how your waist looks

You want to be hitting your deltoids on a regular basis. This is one of the muscle groups that men neglect the most in my opinion. The reason why you need to be hitting your deltoids extensively and growing large shoulders is that large shoulders are going to make your waist look smaller.

For me, for example, I have a pretty thick waist. It's very thick relative to my height. And if I didn't have bigger shoulders, I wouldn't look that good. But because I have bigger shoulders, it makes my waist look smaller than it really is.

So shoulders are important, not just because they look good directly, but because they make your abs and your waist look better.

## How to do side lateral raises

All you need for this is a pair of light dumbbells. I'm doing 15 pounds. Most of you guys should be doing 5 or 10 pounds for this. You don't need to go heavy on this one.

1. Keep a bend in your arms and raise the dumbbells out to your sides.
2. Get your elbows high at the top. If your elbow is not high, then your deltoid is not being worked.
3. Don't swing the weight. If you have to swing it, the weight is too heavy for you.

I do these as a 30 second timed set. It is the second exercise in my [home arm workout](/videos/home-arm-workout-shoulders-biceps-triceps-with-just-dumbbells-do-it-with-me/).
