I started taking my nutrition seriously in my 20s, and it changed a lot more than how I looked.

## What it looks like for me today

Here's what that looks like for me today:

- I [skip breakfast](/videos/why-i-skip-breakfast-every-single-day/) and don't eat anything until about 2 p.m.
- Then I start with a big salad.
- Every meal has a large serving of meat, fish or eggs.
- I [meal prep](/videos/stop-cooking-your-meals-use-a-meal-prep-service/), which costs me about $8 or $9 a meal. That's less than ordering Chipotle.

I want to point out that last one, because a lot of guys tell themselves that eating right is expensive. That excuse is gone. About $8 or $9 a meal is less than ordering Chipotle, so if you can pay for Chipotle, you can't tell me eating right costs too much.
