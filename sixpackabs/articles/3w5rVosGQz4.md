If you're on Adderall or another ADHD stimulant for productivity, my advice is to get off it. I'm not a doctor, so talk to yours about the safest way to come off it, and don't put that conversation off. Here's the short answer. It makes you more productive for a few hours, but over the long run it costs you more productivity than it ever gave you, because it wrecks your sleep and it wrecks your nutrition.

## My experience

I took Vyvanse, Adderall and Adderall XR for about 5 years, in my late 20s and early 30s. At first it felt like it turned me into Superman. I was more productive than I'd ever been in my life, and I made more money than I ever had.

But long term, it had a huge, permanent negative effect on my health and my productivity. Now that's my own experience. I'm not telling you it's a medical fact for everyone. But I lived it, and this is how it played out for me.

## The cycle

Here's how it goes:

1. You take it and you get more productive for a few hours.
2. You sleep worse that night and you don't eat properly, so the next day you're less productive.
3. So you take more, and you get above normal again, but not as high as the first time.
4. The longer you use it, the bigger the hit to your sleep and your nutrition.
5. Eventually you need it just to function the way you used to function without it.
6. In the end, even on a high dose, you're less productive than you were before you ever took it. And the long-term effects get so bad that you have to quit.

## What actually works long term

Look at what the drug takes from you. Sleep and nutrition. Those two things are what actually drive your energy and your productivity. So when you give them up for a few good hours, you lose more than you got.
