Your body fat percentage tells you how close you are to abs. The scale doesn't. The scale can't tell fat from muscle, it just tells you how heavy you are. To see your upper abs, you need to get down to about 15%. When the government measured thousands of American men with body scans, the average man was around 28% body fat, and that number is from 20 years ago, so today it's probably even higher. So the average guy is a long way from abs.

Here's what every body fat percentage looks like on the same guy, and exactly what to do at each level to get to the next one.

In the video I show you AI-generated images of me at every body fat level, from 35% all the way down to 8%. Find the one that looks the most like you. That's your starting point. Then do the action step for your level, plus every step below it. Each step builds on the one before it, so keep doing the earlier ones as you go.

## 35% body fat

At 35%, your belly sticks out past your chest. You have man boobs and a double chin. If this is you, you have a belly fat emergency, and you need to treat it like one.

Your action step is to start Zepbound, start doing a five-minute workout every morning and start fasting until 2 pm every day. Focus mainly on your nutrition at this stage, because it's going to be the main factor in how fast you lose fat. Zepbound turns down your appetite, so it makes fasting much easier for you.

Now, I'm not a doctor, so talk to yours before you start any medication. But in my opinion, at 35% body fat, the benefits of Zepbound far, far outweigh the risks, and it's something you should be seriously thinking about.

Five minutes for a workout might not sound like much, but at this level your goal is to make working out a habit that you never skip. Do squats, push-ups and planks every morning with no equipment. Do those three things every day and the first 20 pounds will come off faster than you expect.

## 30% body fat

At 30%, your belly hangs over your belt. You have love handles and a puffy face. And don't let anybody tell you that this is healthy at any size. In my opinion, that advice is bullshit. You cannot be healthy at any size, and this is the reason why a lot of guys stay fat.

Your action step is to cut every liquid calorie. That means soda, juice, sweet coffee, milk and even coconut water. I drank large amounts of coconut water for years because I thought I needed it to stay hydrated. Now I use a zero calorie electrolyte powder instead.

And [weigh yourself every morning](/videos/weigh-yourself-every-single-day-heres-why/). If you don't weigh yourself every day, you can gain weight for weeks before you notice. But if you catch a couple of pounds the next morning, they're easy to lose.

## 25% body fat

At 25%, your belly still sticks out in a shirt. You don't have muscle definition anywhere.

Your action step is to step it up to 10-minute workouts with a kettlebell every day, and to put protein in every meal. [Kettlebell deadlifts](/videos/how-to-do-kettlebell-deadlifts-the-right-way-at-home/) are the best place to start. They hit your legs, your back and your grip. You can do them in your living room, and you just need a kettlebell and a mat.

Putting protein in every meal means every meal you eat has a large serving of meat, fish or eggs. Protein fills you up more than anything else you can eat. It's also going to protect your muscle when the fat comes off, and that's a bigger concern now that you're getting leaner.

## 20% body fat

At 20%, you look flat in a shirt, but you're soft without one. And you still have love handles.

Your action step is to join a real gym, train total body every day, and swap chips and crackers for sardines or hard-boiled eggs. My workout is 15 minutes of cardio, then one leg exercise, three upper body exercises and one ab exercise, three sets for each exercise.

Chips and crackers are the easiest calories in the world to overeat. Sardines and eggs are packed with protein, so they actually fill you up. I buy the prepackaged hard-boiled eggs, so there's nothing to cook, and you can eat sardines right out of the can. Super convenient and easy.

## 15% body fat

At 15%, you can see the outline of your upper abs in good light. Your shoulders and your arms start to show.

Your action step is to cut way back on alcohol and restaurant meals, and to order from a meal prep service instead. I didn't quit drinking, but I cut way back to get to a lower body fat percentage than this. And instead of eating out, I order meal prep for about $8 to $9 per meal. That's less than ordering Chipotle.

This is the level where most guys stall, because they're happy with how they look in a shirt. Push through it, because the next level is where your upper abs show all day.

## 12% body fat

At 12%, your upper abs show all day, your obliques start to show and your jawline comes back.

Your action step is to eat at least 0.8 grams of protein per pound of lean body mass, and to sleep in a cold room. So let's say you're 180 pounds and you're 12% body fat. You have about 158 pounds of lean mass, so you need at least 127 grams of protein a day. That's a large serving of meat, fish or eggs at every meal, plus a protein snack once or twice a day.

And sleep matters more than most guys think. I sleep best at 63 degrees, so I keep my room as cold as I can get it. In a University of Chicago study, dieters who slept five and a half hours a night lost 55% less fat than when they slept eight and a half hours on the same exact diet.

## 10% body fat

At 10%, you've got a clear four pack. Your lower abs are just starting to show.

Your action step is to track every calorie with AI. These last few pounds aren't going to come off by guessing. You take a picture of your food and AI estimates the calories for you. You can [try Abs By AI free for 7 days]({CTA}), or use any other AI calorie tracking app you like. Once you start tracking, you'll be shocked at how many calories were hiding in the food you thought was healthy.

## 8% body fat

At 8%, you have the full six pack, with veins in your arms and on your lower abs.

Your action step is to [train abs every day](/videos/why-i-train-abs-every-single-day/). Cable crunches, leg raises and my three exercise ab workout. Notice this is the first level where I'm telling you to train your abs hard.

Ab exercises don't burn the fat that covers your abs. But once the fat is gone, putting weight on your abs makes them bigger so they show more. When you're this lean, even building a millimeter of muscle in your abs will make a huge visual difference. That's why for shredded guys, focusing on ab training really pays off. And that's why I train abs every day now, and why you should too once you get here.

## Find your level

Find the level that looks the most like you, then start on its action step today.
