Use a pair of dumbbells for three 30-second exercises, then rest for 30 seconds and replay the round as directed. That's how to use my Arms & Shoulders Dumbbell Workout: Follow Along (3 Exercises). You do bicep curls first, side lateral raises second, and overhead triceps extensions third.

Now, this video is a follow-along. After the short opening, you're following the exercise demonstrations, the labels, and the countdown. So before you start, let me walk you through how the round works and what the replay instructions mean.

## Get ready before you press play

Have your pair of dumbbells ready, and make sure you've got enough room for the side lateral raises and the overhead triceps extensions. Those movements are coming up in the same round, so you want to have room for all three exercises before you start the video.

The opening and countdown begin at 0:00, and the curls start at about 0:06. There isn't a long introduction where I explain each exercise. Once that first exercise starts, you're into the timed workout.

If you want me to explain the exercises before you follow along, watch [the full home arm workout explanation](/videos/home-arm-workout-shoulders-biceps-triceps-with-just-dumbbells-do-it-with-me/). That's the related narrated video, Home Arm Workout: Shoulders, Biceps & Triceps With Just Dumbbells (Do It With Me), which is already available. You can use that video to learn the exercises, then come back here for the timed round.

## Follow the three exercises in order

The round is simple: 30 seconds of curls, 30 seconds of side laterals, and 30 seconds of overhead triceps extensions. You're working from the timer, so there isn't a prescribed number of reps you have to finish in each block.

1. **Bicep curls, about 0:06.** This is the biceps exercise. Follow the filmed movement and the visible countdown for the first 30-second block. When that block ends, the next exercise is side lateral raises.

2. **Side lateral raises, about 0:36.** This is the shoulders exercise. Again, follow the movement shown in the video and use the countdown to follow the 30-second block. After that, you're moving to the overhead triceps extensions.

3. **Overhead triceps extensions, about 1:06.** This is the triceps exercise and the last exercise of the round. Follow along until the exercise block finishes at about 1:36. That's when you reach the completion message and rest.

So you've got one exercise for biceps, one for shoulders, and one for triceps. Keep that order when you replay. Each replay is the same sequence you just completed, with the same three timed exercise blocks.

## Use the screen to follow along

The finished video is mainly music and on-screen labels. You can see the exercise being demonstrated and watch the countdown. You don't need to wait for me to tell you verbally when to change exercises, because there aren't narrated directions through the workout.

This is why the two videos serve different purposes. In the narrated explainer, I talk you through the exercises. Here, you can follow the movements and timing without going through that explanation again each round. If you need more detailed form instruction, go through the narrated explainer first.

You may also be wondering about doing curls with one arm at a time or both arms together. Both versions work. One arm at a time permits more range of motion, while both arms together is faster and helps keep the sides even. ## Rest, then replay the round

At the end of the exercise sequence, the video gives you these instructions:

For beginners replay 2 more rounds.

For advanced guys replay 4 more rounds.

Now, the important word there is “more.” You've already completed one round when you see that message. So for beginners, two more rounds means three rounds total. For advanced guys, four more rounds means five rounds total.

Rest for 30 seconds at the end of the round before you replay. That rest comes after the three exercises. The workout doesn't call for a 30-second rest between every exercise.

One full round gives you 90 seconds of exercise plus 30 seconds of rest. The opening adds a few seconds, which is why the full video runs about two minutes and six seconds. The exercise sequence itself is still three 30-second blocks.

When you're ready to replay, start the video again and follow the same order: curls, side laterals, overhead triceps extensions, then rest. Count the round you've already done toward your total. In other words, finishing the curls doesn't finish a round. You're counting a full round after you've gone through all three exercises.
