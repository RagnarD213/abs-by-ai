## 7 reasons your belly fat is an emergency

Some of these sting. They're supposed to.

### 1. It is killing your dating life

If you're single, modern dating is winner take all, and your looks are the biggest factor in winning. More and more relationships start on dating apps, and on the apps a small share of the men get most of the matches. With belly fat, most attractive women swipe left on you in seconds. If you're ripped, you get abundant matches and your choice of attractive women.

### 2. It is killing your relationship

Attraction cannot be negotiated. Your wife or girlfriend can love you and still not feel attracted to you. If you have a belly, that's probably what's happening. She won't say it. She'll tell you that you look fine, but her attraction responds to what she sees, not to what she says. She didn't stop noticing lean, fit men the day she met you, and she never will.

Think about it. Aren't you much more attracted to fit women than overweight women? Women feel the exact same way. When you get lean, you feel the difference fast: more affection, more desire, more respect. Your body is the one part of this you completely control.

### 3. It is hurting your health

Belly fat is the most dangerous fat on your body because a lot of it is visceral fat, the kind that wraps around your organs. Visceral fat is linked to a higher risk of heart disease, type 2 diabetes and some cancers. If your doctor told you that you had a condition raising the risk of all three, you would treat it as an emergency. That is what belly fat is.

### 4. It makes you less productive at work

Your energy crashes after every meal. Your sleep is worse. You're dragging through the afternoon on caffeine, working at 70%.

Lean, fit people have more usable hours in the day. That's a real competitive advantage.

### 5. It is costing you money

It's called the halo effect. People automatically assume fit, good-looking people are more disciplined, more competent, more of a leader. Fit people tend to get hired more, promoted more, paid more.

Your belly walks into every interview and meeting before you do. If you don't have the discipline to eat healthy and work out, employers and business partners assume you don't have it for the job either. That's how I think, and how most successful people think too.

### 6. It is stealing your fun

Vacations, the beach, the pool, hiking, playing with your kids. With a belly you're the guy keeping his shirt on at the beach, never in the pictures.

I have a daughter. Being the dad in the water, in the pictures, keeping up all day, that alone is worth everything in the plan below.

### 7. It compounds

This is the big one. The more belly fat you carry, the harder exercise feels, so you exercise less. Energy drops, you feel worse, you comfort yourself with food. More food, less movement, more belly fat.

It's a downward spiral. Every month you stay casual the hole gets deeper.

The compounding works both ways. The moment you start losing, everything gets easier and motivation feeds on results. You have to force the spiral the other way.
