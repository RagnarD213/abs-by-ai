This is my 1 minute ab workout with no talking, just the workout. Press play and do it with me: three rounds of toe touches, V-sit twists and Spiderman planks, 10 reps each, with a 30 second jog in place between rounds. The whole thing takes under five minutes.

## The exact sequence

Every round is the same three exercises, 10 reps each, back to back.

1. **Toe touches**, 10 reps
2. **V-sit twists**, 10 reps
3. **Spiderman planks**, 10 reps
4. **Jog in place**, 30 seconds

Here's how it runs in the video:

- **Round 1** starts at 0:00, and the first jog in place starts at 1:17.
- **Round 2** starts at 1:49, and the second jog in place starts at 2:56.
- **Round 3** starts at 3:26 and finishes at 4:32. There's no jog after the last round. You're done.

The rounds run on reps, not a clock. Each exercise takes me roughly 20 to 30 seconds, and the video moves on when I finish my 10. If you need more time, pause it and finish your reps. Clean reps beat keeping up.

## How many rounds to do

- **Starting out:** do one round, the way I run it in the full video, and do your toe touches with no weight.
- **Once one round feels easy:** do all three rounds with me, start to finish.
- **Want more resistance:** add a light medicine ball. I use a 4 pound ball. Most people should start with no weight on the toe touches, 2 pounds is a good first step up, and 6 or 8 pounds is more than I use. I wouldn't go past 10 pounds, even if you're a bodybuilder, because your form starts to suffer.

## Tips for following along

### Toe touches: knee yourself in the face

Lie flat, jackknife up and get your head and your butt off the ground at the top. On every rep, picture kneeing yourself in the face. You can't actually do it, but aiming for that motion gives you the right contraction. Keep it slow and controlled.

### V-sit twists: move your legs

Balance on your butt with your knees in the air, then move your legs to one side while you bounce the ball on the other side. Both sides is one rep. Your legs moving side to side is what makes the exercise work, so don't just tap the ball back and forth. Because they come right after the toe touches, your six pack is already tired and your obliques do more of the work.

### Spiderman planks: stay perfectly straight

Start on your elbows in a plank, push up into a push-up position, then come back down. No butt in the air, no sagging. If you're more advanced, keep your stomach sucked in the whole set to make every rep harder.

### Jog in place: keep moving

The 30 second jog is your only break between rounds. Keep your feet moving and get ready for the next round of toe touches.
