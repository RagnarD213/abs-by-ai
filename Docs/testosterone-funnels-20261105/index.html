Your question first

Is tongkat ali a problem on Google?

No. The ingredient is fine. The wording is the risk.

  • Google's banned supplement list doesn't name tongkat ali (also called Eurycoma longifolia or longjack). It also doesn't name tribulus, orchic, maca, ginseng, boron or arginine. The list is mostly spiked sex pills and prohormones by brand name.
  • V Shred's Test Boost Max contains 200 mg of tongkat ali, per the NIH label database. V Shred has spent about $6M on YouTube ads for it and still runs them. Nugenix Ultimate (150 mg), Gorilla Mind Sigma (200 mg) and Prime Labs Prime Test also contain tongkat.
  • What gets ads rejected is the claim. Avoid sexual enhancement, libido, "like TRT," "steroid-like," and anything comparing it to a prescription drug. Keep the ads to energy, strength, belly fat and "supports healthy testosterone levels."
  • Two things to watch with the supplier. Some imported tongkat products have been recalled for hidden ED drugs or heavy metals, so ask Supliful for a certificate of analysis on the tongkat lot. Your 400 mg dose is also double the 200 mg that the main safety review covered.

What they all do

Patterns to copy

Lead with food, not the pill

The two biggest spenders both open with foods that kill testosterone. The product is the "and here's the shortcut" at the end.

Estrogen is the villain

Stubborn belly and chest fat get blamed on estrogen. That ties testosterone to fat loss, which is your lane.

Men over 40 or 50

Every winner names an age. TestoGreens and Nugenix both target over 50.

A small yes before the pitch

A quiz or "see if you qualify" step comes before the video or the offer.

1/3/6 bottle ladder

$49 for one, then a lower price per bottle and free shipping at 3 and 6, plus a long guarantee.

Bonuses and guarantees

Two free e-books on the 3 and 6 packs. Guarantees run from 60 days to a lifetime and usually refund the product price only, not shipping.

Billing is where they get hurt

Every brand's complaints are about auto-ship, extra shipments and hard cancellation. A clear subscription with easy cancelling sets you apart.

An authority on camera

A doctor (TestoGreens) or a fit founder (V Shred). You fit the founder role: 41, with abs.

Recommendation

What Great Again T's funnel should be

Model V Shred's structure and use TestoGreens' angle.

  • Ad: you on camera, "the foods quietly lowering testosterone in men over 40 and parking fat on your belly."
  • Step 1: a two-to-four-question quiz (age, belly fat, energy).
  • Step 2: your VSL. Cover the foods, the estrogen and belly-fat link, what you do at 41, then Great Again T.
  • Offer: 1/3/6 bottles at about $59 / $147 / $234 (the market range), two bonus guides on the bundles, a subscription with Menerals-style perks, and a long guarantee. The Eternal Burn AM + PM stack is the upsell.
  • Hook only you can use: "400 mg of tongkat. Most boosters have none." Back it with your own body at 41.
  • Wording: energy, strength, belly fat, "supports healthy testosterone." No libido, ED, TRT or steroid claims.
