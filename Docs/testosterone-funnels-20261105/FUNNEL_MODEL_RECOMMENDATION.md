### Your read on V Shred vs TestoGreens

You are partly right. V Shred has a big brand and an existing customer list, so its funnel can work without being optimal. But the evidence cuts both ways:

- TestoGreens is the one scaling **right now**, so its funnel is proven in current conditions. V Shred's Test Boost spend peaked earlier.
- But TestoGreens' edge is **ad volume**, not page cleverness: 556 ads into one page. The lesson from TestoGreens is "test a lot of long ads into one strong VSL", which you can copy without copying its page.
- V Shred's drop is company-wide (all products), so it doesn't tell us the Test Boost funnel stopped working.
- V Shred ran both a quiz and a no-quiz page, and the quiz version is the one still running. That is the only head-to-head evidence on quizzes in this category, and it leans toward the quiz.

## Why the structure should be V Shred's

1. **You are a Vince, not a doctor.** TestoGreens' VSL is built on "Dr. A, 33-year career, reviewed blood work from thousands of patients." You can't claim that. V Shred's structure runs entirely on a fit founder who has coached a lot of men, which is your position.
2. **The formula matches V Shred's story.** Great Again T has tongkat 400 mg, zinc 50 mg, maca, eleuthero/ginseng, nettle, boron, tribulus. V Shred's mechanism is cortisol + SHBG ("testosterone locked up") + zinc. Tongkat, nettle and boron all fit the "free up the testosterone you already make" story. TestoGreens' mechanism is an aromatase blocker (pomegranate peel, cocoa, DIM). **We have none of those**, so its central promise would not match our label.
3. **Mobile.** Both of V Shred's VSL versions are vertical. A vertical founder video fills a phone. TestoGreens is horizontal and still scales, but its viewers skew 50+ on YouTube, more of them on TV and desktop.
4. **Length.** 28 minutes is a realistic watch for a busy 40-year-old. TestoGreens runs 52 minutes, of which the last 11 are FAQ.

## What to take from each

### From V Shred
- **Hook**: "Are healthy foods destroying your testosterone?" plus one shocking proof (atrazine frogs). Both funnels open on foods, so the foods hook is the most proven element in the category.
- **"It's not your fault, the rules changed" reframe** (02:03): "You're not losing your edge because you stopped trying."
- **Four-stat montage** (06:13): testosterone halved since the 1980s, etc. Fast and credible.
- **A named system**: "the male hormone matrix" (LH, SHBG, cortisol). A name makes the mechanism memorable. Make our own.
- **One ingredient per failure point**: three hero ingredients, each fixing one named problem, then a short list of the rest.
- **Day-in-the-life future pacing** (23:28): snooze button, 2 p.m. crash, meetings, gym. Strong for 35 to 55 year old men.
- **Close**: "the only risk is doing nothing", then option one / option two.
- **Quiz**: two yes questions, not a real quiz.

### From TestoGreens
- **Origin story with real numbers** (03:10 to 06:18): failed life insurance exam, did everything right, still fell apart, tested at 216, now 931 at 56, stepped on stage. This is the strongest part of either VSL. Your version needs your own true story. Your own real blood work, if you have it, would be the most powerful proof in the video.
- **TRT as the enemy and the price anchor**: "$600 to $1,000 a month", side effects, "fighting the symptom, not the cause."
- **"Why other boosters fail"**: they push testosterone up but ignore the real problem. We frame it with SHBG instead of aromatase.
- **Price-drop sequence**: $219.95 retail, not $159, not $129, maybe $99 soon, today $69, "$2.30 a day."
- **Bonuses only on the 3 and 6 bottle packages**: 97 testosterone foods guide, 1-day estrogen detox, dosing protocol. A foods guide is a natural fit for you; you can write these.
- **Scarcity reasons**: batches take months, ingredients are hard to source.
- **FAQ after the close** (40:52 to 51:50): "You still there?" then the main objections answered one by one. It catches people who didn't buy.
- **Long pre-selling ads (3.5 to 5 minutes)** and a lot of them.

### From neither (don't copy)
- **Explicit bedroom lines** ("soldier at full salute", "toe-curling performance"). TestoGreens runs them, but they are what Google restricts, and they don't fit your brand.
- **V Shred's chelated zinc argument.** V Shred says zinc oxide is cheap and barely absorbed. **Great Again T uses zinc oxide.** Copying that section would argue against our own label.
- **Nugenix-style free sample with hidden auto-ship.** No sales video, and the billing is what drives complaints.

## Our differentiator to build the mechanism around

**400 mg tongkat per serving, double V Shred's 200 mg.** It fits V Shred's own framing ("tongkat is the testosterone liberator") and lets us say ours has twice the dose. Hero ingredients: tongkat (frees testosterone, lowers cortisol), zinc (the testosterone factory needs it), maca or the nettle and boron pair (keeps testosterone free).

## The quiz decision

**Launch with the quiz, test against no quiz.**

- For it: V Shred's surviving version uses it; "Take the quiz" is a better ad call to action than "Watch this video"; two yes answers make a small commitment; the quiz answers give us data on the audience (age, goal) that we don't have yet.
- Against it: one extra step, so some people drop off. TestoGreens scales without one. V Shred's bigger Google spend ($4.0M vs $2.2M) went to the no-quiz page.
- The test costs almost nothing to build: one quiz page in front of the same VSL page. Ads differ only in the last line ("Take the 30-second quiz" vs "Watch the free video").

### How much a fair test costs (estimate, not from VidTao)

Judge on cost per purchase. To tell two pages apart you need about 30 to 50 purchases on each. If a purchase costs roughly $60 to $120 of ad spend on cold YouTube traffic (my assumption for a new brand at these prices, to be replaced by our real number within the first week), that's about **$4,000 to $10,000 total** for a clear answer. An early read at 15 to 20 purchases each (about $2,000 to $4,000) only catches a big difference.

## Vertical or horizontal

**Vertical 9:16 for the VSL.** Both of V Shred's versions are vertical; most of our traffic will be phones; a founder talking to camera works natively vertical. On desktop it shows as a centered column, which is fine. The ads are a separate choice per placement (YouTube in-stream is mostly horizontal); that doesn't change the VSL.
