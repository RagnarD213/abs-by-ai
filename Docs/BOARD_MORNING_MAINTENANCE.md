# Morning brief: board maintenance

Run during STEP 1 — GATHER of `abs-by-ai-morning-brief`, every morning in America/Chicago.

1. Read `AI_COORDINATION.md`, recent Git changes, `AI_COORDINATION_ARCHIVE.md` and the previous brief. Report newly finished work in the brief (a short completed-work section, once per completion) and in the finishing session's chat. Never park finished work as FYI on the board. Retrieve completions from Git, the archive and available session reports; do not depend on a board entry surviving until morning.
2. Flag every open entry with **no substantive change for 7+ days**. Compare its text and history; formatting, date-only edits, relocation and routine “still waiting” checks do not reset the clock. Show days unchanged and the next action/owner. Use `Updated: YYYY-MM-DD` for new substantive updates.
3. List every unresolved decision **older than 14 days** under **“decide or drop”**, with its age, consequence and concrete choices. Preserve `Decision since: YYYY-MM-DD` when updating it; updating wording does not make the decision younger. This list and the stale-entry flags must not be silently hidden by the brief's normal row caps. Keep the existing special choices for the two held long-forms.
4. Legacy entries may have only MM-DD dates or `Baseline 2026-09-15; age unknown`. Establish actual last change and decision origin from Git history, older briefs or the archive. The baseline is the day tracking was introduced, NOT a new task or decision date. If evidence is unavailable, label **age unknown — verify**; never invent an age or quietly call it fresh. Resolve MM-DD years from the source history.
5. **Once a week, Monday**, archive any remaining finished/approved entries to `AI_COORDINATION_ARCHIVE.md`, then remove only those entries from the board. Catch up on the next successful morning run if Monday was missed: inspect the latest `## Weekly board cleanup — YYYY-MM-DD` marker and run if none exists or it is 7+ days old. Append that dated marker after the sweep, even when zero entries were eligible. Preserve entry text and dates in the archive; verify the append before removing anything. Work awaiting approval, verification or an external dependency is still open. Re-read the board immediately before saving and preserve concurrent changes.
6. Run `scripts/board-check.sh` after any board edit and before publishing the brief. Fix missing dates and compress entries if it fails; route durable details to their appropriate files. Never discard open decisions or blockers just to pass. Include maintenance changes in the morning brief's normal task-scoped commit/push.

The weekly sweep is a backstop. Finishing sessions remove finished/approved entries immediately after preserving durable information; they do not wait for Monday. Report completion in chat and the morning brief, not as FYI on the board.
