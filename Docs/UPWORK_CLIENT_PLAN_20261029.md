Written by Claude Opus 5.5, 2026-10-29, for Dan Rose. Status: plan approved in principle by Dan; paid-options budget and Codex's review still open. Execution brief: `Handoffs/handoff-20261029-upwork-client-acquisition.md`. Profile copy and packages: `Handoffs/upwork-profile-draft.md`.

## 1. Summary

Dan wants two things from this business:

1. **Money as fast as possible, even small amounts.** He has paid for AI subscriptions for a long time without making money from them and needs proof that AI work can earn. This is a cash business, not a long-term brand like Abs By AI or SixPackAbs.com.
2. **Eventually about 95% run by AI.** AI finds clients daily, decides what to pitch, writes pitches, makes samples and does the work. Dan is willing to work extensively by hand first to train the system.

The plan:
- Sell Dan's approved direct-response video ad packages, leading with start-to-finish ads, and take single-part jobs (scripts, edits, thumbnails, AI footage) as first wins.
- Two client engines: **Upwork bids** through Upwork's official AI connector (Dan confirms each proposal), and **direct outreach to brands** off Upwork (fastest cash, no review problem).
- Replace Dan's "$1 bids for reviews" idea with real paid launch prices that are never tied to reviews.
- Train the AI on every proposal and job from day one, so that by month two Dan works about 2 hours a day approving a queue the AI prepared.

## 5. Upwork rules that shape the plan (verified on Upwork's own pages, 2026-10-29)

- **Bots are banned.** A bot is "any script, program, browser extension, or third-party service that automatically sends requests to Upwork, collects data, or performs actions faster or more frequently than a human could." Job watchers, auto-refresh, page monitors, macros and scripts using session cookies get accounts flagged. Penalty: restriction up to permanent block. https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/43342677368467-Use-bots-and-other-automation-properly
- **The official route is Upwork's MCP connector** (`https://mcp.upwork.com/mcp`, launched August 2026, free, works with Claude Code, Codex, ChatGPT). It can search jobs, read details, draft and submit proposals, read and send messages, submit milestone work and manage the Availability badge. "Every write action is a draft you confirm separately." Accepting offers and money happen on upwork.com. It cannot create Project Catalog listings. https://www.upwork.com/ai/mcp and https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/55446516654611-How-to-use-Upwork-with-AI-agents-through-MCP
- **API and MCP Terms (effective 2026-10-05):** searching must serve "a specific, documented, user-directed task" and may not "continuously monitor" job listings. Scraping, headless browsing and screen reading are banned. If a client "would reasonably believe" content came from a human, AI origin must be disclosed. https://www.upwork.com/legal#apimcpterms
- **Rate limits:** searches 15/min, 150/hour, 750/day; proposals and jobs 20/min, 60/hour, 150/day. "Do not poll." https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/56577997707923-Rate-limits-for-Upwork-MCP
- **Public API key** needs $25,000+ lifetime earnings and 90%+ Job Success Score, so it is not available to Dan. Job RSS feeds ended in August 2024.
- **Feedback building is banned.** Upwork names "ask for or offer free work to 'earn' a good review", paying or refunding for feedback, and fake jobs "even with someone you know". First offense: warning or suspension; serious or repeat: permanent block. Upwork also deletes "insider" feedback between closely connected accounts. https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/17989850384531--What-is-feedback-building
- **Price floors and costs:** minimum $5 fixed price, $3/hour. Service fee 0 to 15% per contract. Connects $0.15 each; Connects per proposal vary by job (third parties say about 16 to 32, unconfirmed). Basic plan now gives 0 Connects a month. Freelancer Plus $19.99/month: 100 Connects, personalised job alerts, proposal insights, full Uma (seen in Dan's account).
- **Badges:** Rising Talent needs a 4.8+ rating and $250 earned in 12 months (or an Upwork invite). Paid Consultations require a badge. Availability badge is paid weekly in Connects; Upwork claims up to 70% more invites.

## 6. What we sell first, and pricing at zero reviews

- **Main recurring product (from Greg Isenberg's "always-on ad studio" idea):** a paid trial on one product, then a monthly pack of new ads every week. Brands' ads wear out faster than their teams can replace them.

## 7. Engine 1: Upwork bids

- **Search:** Claude through the official connector, 3 times a day as a defined task (not a polling loop), plus Freelancer Plus job alerts as a trigger. Bid-tool data says reply rates roughly halve after the first 30 minutes, so speed matters.
- **Targets:** direct-response ad scripts, video ads, YouTube ads, VSLs, ad editing, ad thumbnails, AI video ads, YouTube/Google Ads management. Skip vlogs, weddings and general channel editing (the profile excludes them).
- **Score each job (record the reasons):** fit 30, clear brief and usable assets 20, budget 20, client signals (payment verified, spend, hire rate) 15, repeat potential 15. Shortlist at 75+. Missing data counts as unknown, not zero.
- **Proposal formula:**
  1. Name their product and one specific observation about their current ad or page. Their live ads are public in Google's Ads Transparency Center and Meta's Ad Library.
  2. Three new hook ideas for their product.
  3. The most relevant portfolio piece, with its number.
  4. Price and timing.
  5. One question.

  Lead with money results, never "AI". Answer screening questions directly. Keep it short.
- **Volume:** in month one, 3 to 5 excellent proposals a day. Boost only the top-scored jobs. Dan confirms every proposal in Upwork; Claude reopens it to confirm it is live.

## 8. Engine 2: direct outreach to brands (off Upwork)

Why: Dan has no warm leads; zero reviews hurt on Upwork but not in direct outreach; no platform fee; free case-study work is allowed off Upwork.

- Each week, AI builds a list of fitness, supplement, health and info-product brands, plus ad-buying agencies, that clearly spend on YouTube or Meta ads but whose ads all make the same argument.
- For the best 5 to 10 a week: a 2-line teardown of their ads plus 3 watermarked sample hooks or concepts built from their own customer reviews (Greg Isenberg's method). For the top 2 or 3, Dan records a personal video.
- Offer: a paid trial on one product, then the weekly ad pack. One or two early prospects may get free or cheap work in exchange for a case study and testimonial. Those go into the Upwork portfolio and Upwork's client-testimonial feature (confirm the feature still exists).
- Pitch agencies too: one agency can bring several brands.
- Send from a separate domain with warmed-up mailboxes, never absbyai.com or Dan's main Gmail. Dan approves the first batch (list and text) once; later batches follow his batch-approval rule.

## 9. Personal video method

- **Desk webcam, not the studio set.** For a pitch video, being specific beats picture quality; buyers want proof Dan looked at their ad.
- **Format:** Loom free plan (25 videos, 5 minutes each) or Mac screen recording uploaded unlisted. The screen shows the client's current ad, then Claude's 3 sample hooks, with Dan's face in the corner. 60 to 90 seconds, one take, no editing.
- **Script card from Claude for each video:**
  1. "Hi [name], I watched your [ad]" plus the one problem.
  2. The 3 new angles on screen.
  3. One next step.

  About 5 minutes per video including prep.
- No spliced generic intro (the profile video already does that job, and a reused opener reads as a template). No AI clone of Dan's face or voice presented as personal (disclosure rule; buyers notice).

## 11. Phases and the training loop toward 95% AI

1. **Week 1:**
   - Dan verifies his ID and the profile-finish task completes.
   - Buy Plus and Connects.
   - Connect Claude Code to the Upwork connector and confirm a test search.
   - Set up the outreach domain and start mailbox warm-up.
   - Build the tracker.
   - First Upwork proposals, each approved by Dan.
2. **Weeks 2 to 4 (Dan full time):**
   - Upwork: 3 to 5 proposals a day.
   - Outreach: 10 to 20 personalised emails a day once the mailboxes are warm.
   - Personal videos: 2 to 3 a week.
   - Deliver with the existing pipelines: scripts (`/scriptwriting`, `dan-voice-writer`), edits (`/ad-edit`), thumbnails (`/youtube-packaging`, Codex images), AI footage (`_shared/veo`), Google Ads (`scripts/ads/api/`). Client videos are categorised AD before editing.
3. **Training loop (from job one):** after every proposal and job, record what Dan changed and why, every missing input, every revision and where time went. Turn these into rules in a new skill (`.claude/skills/upwork-pitch/`) covering scoring, proposal templates, sample recipes and intake questions. Corey Ganim (Isenberg guest) reported drafts going from 60 to 70% right to near copy-paste by about job 4 to 6 this way.
4. **Month two (Dan 2 hours a day):**
   - A scheduled Claude routine searches 3 times a day, drafts proposals and samples, and leaves an approval queue that Dan clears in one sitting.
   - Outreach lists, teardowns and follow-ups run through the same queue.
   - Alert Dan only for replies, offers, approval-ready top jobs and failures.
5. **Realistic ceiling:** AI does nearly all the labour. Dan's floor is confirming each Upwork proposal and message and accepting offers, which Upwork requires today. Measured in hours, that is about 95%.

## 12. Tracking and targets

- One record per Upwork job ID or prospect:
  - Job: URL, time found, budget, scope, client signals.
  - Proposal: Connects, score and reasons, proposal version, sample, whether a video was sent, approval, submission receipt.
  - Outcome: reply, interview, hire, revenue, costs, Dan's minutes.
- Weekly: proposals sent, reply rate, hire rate, Connects spent, cost per hire, revenue, Dan's hours.
- Rough benchmark: about 7 replies per 100 proposals (GigRadar data: 8.13% for hand-written templates vs 7.13% for its auto-bidder) and about 1 hire per 50 proposals. That puts the Connects cost per Upwork hire at roughly $100 to $150, fine on $350+ jobs and painful on $25 jobs. These numbers are a guess to check against our own data.
