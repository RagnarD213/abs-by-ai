# Multi-AI design workflow

Dan adopted this process on November 3, 2026 after the SixPackAbs.com packaging contest. Apply to future substantial design contests, with explicit authorization before messaging or launching external AI tasks.

For website contests, all entrants follow the mobile-first rule in AGENTS.md. Specify the phone viewport and review order in every handoff.

1. One coordinator collects the owner brief, facts, assets, real manufacturing or platform limits and common output requirements once.
2. Multiple AIs develop independent proposals against the same brief. For a five-choice round, require five different composition ideas, not color swaps.
3. Present all complete entries together at matching sizes. Judge visible output; tooling, code volume and confident explanations do not establish design quality.
4. Ask Dan what earns trust, feels premium, improves clarity and feels wrong. Attribute his preferences accurately.
5. Share the strongest actual examples across entrants. Explicitly borrow the better ideas and explain what each adds. Do not average everything into an indistinct compromise.
6. Converge through a bounded new round. Inspect mobile, desktop and real production constraints before review. For websites, design and review the phone layout first (AGENTS.md, "Website design: phone first").
7. Lock the exact owner-approved file with a hash and a short description of what must stay. Separate visual approval from technical proof, supplier acceptance and launch.
8. Assign final implementation ownership after comparing independent production proofs. Record candid feedback and specific improvements for every entrant.
