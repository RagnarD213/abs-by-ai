Standing checklist for Abs By AI paid ads, written after the 2026-08-09 Google Ads suspension (Unacceptable Business Practices; reactivated 2026-08-11). Google named no cause. The registered-trademark symbol was NOT the cause (that claim was withdrawn 2026-08-11). The most probable cause was automated account flagging on a new account in a health vertical. Result-promising headlines were a real but secondary contributor.

**Never in ad copy:**
- **Never promise a physical result.** "Get Real Abs Using AI Tools", "Get Sixpack Abs Using AI Tools", "make them real" all read as offering a product we don't deliver. Sell the visualization and the plan, never the body.
- **No before/after body imagery framed as real**, no disease names, no "humiliating", and no drug or medication names in ADS (organic content may name the drug: [[organic-drug-names-allowed]]).
- The Demand Gen video creative was separately flagged under YouTube's Negative Events and Imagery (body-image) policy, and that limit survives reinstatement. Avoid creative that dwells on the heavier "before" state or negative self-perception.

**On the landing page:**
- Keep the AI-disclosure line above the fold, in the hero.
- The disclosure must say plainly that images are AI-generated and it's a visualization and motivation tool, not real results.

**Account hygiene:**
- Baseline scrutiny on this account is elevated, so the margin comes from boring creative and slow budget ramps.
- Keep identity and billing answers consistent and true.
- **Never open a second Ads account.** Google suspends new ones on sight.
- Expect a possible re-review. Plan for a pause rather than treating it as a shock.
- A suspended account cannot be edited. Copy fixes are described in the appeal and made after reinstatement.
