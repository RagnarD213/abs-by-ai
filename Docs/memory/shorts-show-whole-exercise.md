A short cut from a long-form must make complete sense to a viewer who never watched the long-form. When it tells the viewer to do an exercise, show the whole exercise briefly (a few complete reps), never only a detail of it (arm angle, grip, thumbs) that needs the full video for context.

**Why:** SL-04 short 1 round 2 ended on the side-lateral arm-angle set-up with no exercise shown; Dan: "It's not clear why I'm showing them the arm angle when I don't show the complete exercise." Round 3 (thumbs cue + live-round reps) was approved 2026-09-30 and he asked to lock the rule into the short-editing skills.

**How to apply:** at segment selection, note where each named exercise's complete movement is in the source and pair the segment with it; reviewers check it.
