Dan, 2026-11-02, after cutting my Muhammad round-2 doc: *"some of your revisions were a little bit too nitpicky... we should be a little bit less nitpicky, especially as we're going towards the end of a job... If this was AI, I would do another round because there's no cost... he doesn't give us a low rating... if this was something we were editing, then I would change his stuff. But because it's... a human editing it, we want to be a little more tolerant of very small things at the end of a job."*

He deleted: a label wording swap (the only item on a video), AI start-frame tweaks (pen too thin, abs turned away), an opener crop about 20% short, and two 0.3 s double-image fades. He kept: a missing graphic, a cut-off last word, an AI opener not yet generated, and panels short of the edges. Small items only rode along on that needed round.

**Why:** another round costs a human editor's time and goodwill (his Upwork rating of us); an AI round costs nothing.

**How to apply:** for a human editor from round 2 on, put an item in the doc only when something is missing, broken, cut off or clearly wrong to a viewer at normal speed (or a rule-76 class). Everything smaller goes under "For Dan's call" with a paste-ready line, and rides along only if the video already needs a round. Our own pipeline edits keep the full standard.
