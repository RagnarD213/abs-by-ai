On the Belly Fat (SL-07, 2026-10-18) and Oura Ring Review (SL-09, 2026-10-21) shorts Dan picked the yellow-band design over an AI image of himself in the short's subject 7 times out of 10 picks, and the real pool or studio photo options only once. On SL-09 he turned down all five options for "Train Harder After A Bad Night's Sleep" and asked for "AI-generated images of me looking extremely sleep-deprived and also training hard ... bags under my eyes, look like a sleep-deprived zombie, but also ripped ... seated military press", and for "You're Not Sleeping As Well As You Think" a shirtless version of the tired thumbs-up man from the short's own AI clip.

**Why:** the picture has to act the title out at a glance; a calm portrait next to the headline doesn't sell a topic like bad sleep.

**How to apply:** in every shorts cover set, make the AI-Dan option (slot 3) show him visibly living the title, with the expression the topic needs (exhausted, wired, surprised), while staying ripped, and keep the yellow band among the layouts. The calm closed-mouth rule for AI clips ([[ai-clips-natural-or-regenerate]]) is about motion looking real, not a ban on expressive cover stills. Reusing a character from the short's approved AI clips is a strong option. Related: [[thumbnail-standard-codex-in-setup]], [[thumbnail-unique-designs]].
