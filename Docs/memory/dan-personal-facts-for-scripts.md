Facts Dan typed into his own scripts on 2026-09-21 (9/23 shoot doc), so they are safe to use in first person:

- **Glycine:** takes it every night, 3 g about an hour before bed, mixed with electrolyte powder for flavor. Calls it the sleep supplement that helped him most. Oura sleep score averaged 70-75 before, usually 75-80 now.
- **Hot sleeper:** used to wake up sweating. Sleeps best around 63 degrees; in the Texas heat his AC only gets to about 67; with someone in the bed they compromise at 66 or 67.
- **Zepbound:** intends to stay on it for life. Maintenance microdose 1 to 1.5 mg; if he starts gaining he goes up to 2.5 mg and lowers calories (doing that as of 2026-09-21). Believes a low dose benefits nearly all men.
- **Training:** works out first thing, around EIGHT in the morning (not six), seven days a week, at a real gym despite owning a $20k home gym.
- **Food:** drank large amounts of coconut water for years thinking he needed it for hydration; now uses zero calorie electrolyte powder. Buys pre-packaged hard boiled eggs. Genuinely likes sardines. Meal prep runs about $8 to $9 per meal.
- **AI:** says AI currently saves him at least ten hours a week; tells viewers to use a paid Claude plan.
