On 2026-10-08, reviewing the voice training Part 2 plan, Dan set what may and may not be used to learn his voice.

**Never use as voice samples:**
- His dictation and prompts to Claude or any other AI. His words: "a lot of that is more like functional things ... It's
  not really how I talk to a human being."
- Business email. "That's not really the way that I would talk to viewers."

**Sources he named as good:**
- His old off-the-cuff channel, youtube.com/@danielrose-socialresponse7768 (business and marketing topics, so the
  subject differs, but it is how he speaks off the cuff).
- His Abs By AI videos made from outlines with no script.
- His books: 15 Steps to Profitable YouTube Advertising, and The Sex God Method (PDF on Drive
  `1KVRHZ04oGliUoQd9EISUl0DySIVTu-C3`).
- More of his ads (he will supply them).

**Separate voices:** he suggested different voices for YouTube content, ads, and products, each trained on its own
material. "Products" has not been defined by him yet; Claude's reading (sales letters, VSLs, page copy, the app's words,
programs) is an interpretation to confirm.

**Why:** the test for a source is whether it shows how he talks to viewers or buyers, not whether he produced the words.

**How to apply:** when collecting or weighting samples of Dan's voice, sort by audience first and drop anything he said to
a tool or a business contact. Plan: `Handoffs/handoff-20261008-dan-voice-training-part2-plan.md`, Phase A2. Related:
[[dan-voice-guide]], [[script-zero-edit-lessons]].
