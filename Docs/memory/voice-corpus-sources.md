On 2026-10-08, reviewing the voice training Part 2 plan, Dan set what counts as his voice and how to split it.

**Four types of writing, each trained on its own material (his words, 2026-10-08):**
1. **Content.** YouTube and all the other platforms; "pretty similar across" them.
2. **Ads.**
3. **Website conversion videos**, such as the VSL. Similar to ads, but "more important, and I put more time into those",
   so a separate type. The /start sales letter belongs here, not under products.
4. **Products.** "Things the customer gets": The Sex God Method, The 15 Steps To Profitable YouTube Marketing, his Social
   Response Marketing product videos (the Black Belt course). Not VSLs, not the /start letter.

**Never use as voice samples:**
- His dictation and prompts to Claude or any other AI: "more like functional things ... not really how I talk to a human
  being."
- Business email: "not really the way that I would talk to viewers."

**How he rates his sources:**
- The Sex God Method (PDF on Drive `1KVRHZ04oGliUoQd9EISUl0DySIVTu-C3`): "one of the best examples of how I can
  communicate, one of the best examples of my writing style." It is how he talks to a regular consumer; 15 Steps is for
  a business audience. Of the two, The Sex God Method is more relevant for products and for YouTube content.
- His Abs By AI videos made from outlines with no script.
- His old off-the-cuff channel, youtube.com/@danielrose-socialresponse7768 (business and marketing topics, so the
  subject differs).
- His guest appearance on the Travel Like a Boss podcast (episode 252, 2020): "a very good example of my voice speaking
  off the cuff."
- Old Six Pack Shortcuts videos with him in them: usable but "a very long time ago" and "a little bit dated".
- More of his ads; HBI was the top account and he can supply more results numbers.

**Why:** the test for a source is whether it shows how he talks to viewers or buyers, not whether he produced the words.

**How to apply:** sort any sample of Dan's voice by type first, and drop anything he said to a tool or a business
contact. Claude's own added rule (not Dan's words): text Claude drafted is never a voice sample, even after his edits;
only his changed lines count. Handoff: `Handoffs/handoff-20261008-dan-voice-p2a-corpus-and-bench.md`. Related:
