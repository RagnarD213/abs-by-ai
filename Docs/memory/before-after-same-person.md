**A before picture and an after picture are always the SAME PERSON.** Dan, 2026-09-12, on the Ad 5 vertical's app demo:
*"Generally, going forward, don't mix before-and-after pictures. It should be the same person in the before and after.
That doesn't really make sense if you change the person."*

**Why:** the app demo uploaded one man's photo and then ended on Dan's own AI goal image. A viewer reads a before/after as
one person's transformation, so swapping the person quietly breaks the only claim the sequence is making.

**How to apply:** it binds everywhere a pair appears — an app recording, a result screen, a card, a thumbnail, a landing
page. If that person's after does not exist, **generate it for them** through the live product (a real generation, never a
composite — Dan rejected an overlaid photo within minutes, skill lesson A5.23) or change the before so the pair matches.
Never ship the mismatch and never crop around it.

Written into `AGENTS.md` and all ten video skills that carry the real-picture label rule (2026-09-12). The pair for the
app recording is now filed: [[app-recording-before-after-pair]]. Sits alongside Dan's other labelling rule, that his real
pictures carry "Real picture of me — not AI-generated" and AI ones carry "AI-GENERATED", one or the other on every
picture of his physique.
