When Dan asks to model a proven funnel or VSL, copy what the winning advertisers actually do, checked line by line in their transcripts and pages, and do not swap in Claude's own "better" idea from general knowledge.

**Why:** 2026-11-08, Great Again T outlines. Claude wrote each food as its own paid-off section ("unlike TestoGreens, we pay off the headline") on trust/value reasoning. The transcripts showed neither winner does that: V Shred names one food for about a minute and pivots; TestoGreens never lists its 5 foods and names them only in passing at 33:30 inside the bonus pitch. Dan: "Why do you recommend using something that is not proven by the data here?" Earlier the same day, Claude also proposed opening on Dan's story, which neither winner does.

**How to apply:** before outlining, extract the structure of each winner from the transcript (what is promised, when it is paid off, what gets the minutes) and keep it. Where two top advertisers converge, treat that as the rule. Any deliberate departure must be named as an untested bet, with the reason, never presented as the recommendation.
