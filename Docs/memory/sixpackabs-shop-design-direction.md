On 2026-11-04 Dan picked **Claude A, The Numbered Shelf** as the shop.sixpackabs.com website winner. Grokbot B came second: Dan may like B's opening better (one big plain headline on ivory, then a numbered index of all six products), but A's product presentation further down is stronger. The detailed design boards live in `Contest/claude/shop-final-design/` (canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/B5a3ooH254JfKdt8abBQAu). Dan's website ranking: Claude, Grokbot, Codex, Gemini. Design phone first ([[website-phone-first]]). The winning round folder `Contest/claude/website-round1/` stays unchanged.

Dan's standing design direction for the shop:
- **Avoid predominantly black websites.** Light ivory pages; black and gold as restrained accents (slim top band, primary buttons, number tags).
- **Center the product on phones.** He flagged an off-center hero tub on mobile.
- **Logo:** keep the existing SixPackAbs.com symbol, make the symbol and the wordmark the labels’ gold #C9A766, and set the wordmark in the design font (Archivo). Product images use the locked final labels (no ribbon, no numbers), **web-safe mockups only: `Contest/claude/labels-final-round1/mockups-web/`, never `mockups/` or any flat, print or SVG file** (the others carry his private address; label lock `Contest/approved/LABELS-FINAL-LOCK-20261105.md`). See [[sixpackabs-rebrand-decision]] for the brand split.

- **sixpackabs.com homepage (Nov 5):** black top bar with the gold shop logo; a "Supplements" row of six vertical, swipeable cards (Shorts-card shape) between the long-form videos and Shorts; no Electro Creatine strip.
- **No product numbers on the site; one offer for every product (Nov 5):** 1 for $47, 3 for $129, 6 for $234, or $42 every 30 days.

**Why:** his feedback on the four-entrant website contest and the mobile review gallery.
