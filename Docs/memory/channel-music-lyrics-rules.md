Dan, 2026-11-18, rejecting 15 Flow Music hook tracks as "AI slop": channel music lyrics must not refer to AI, computers or code ("rewriting the code", "every rep's a download, every meal's a patch", "patched every weakness"). He said: "I want to remove that from my channel branding, and I don't want these songs to refer to AI." No age lines ("Forty-one and I'm finally in control"): "obsolete by next year, and that is about me and not the prospect." Lyrics speak to the viewer. The "new version of yourself" idea is fine when it is not nerdy. Sound: flowing rap like "Version 2.0" (AI Joyner Lucas style track) and "Do It Tired", "not just a bunch of shouting over the beat"; no gang chants. He allowed modelling a real rapper's voice style if that is what it takes. Of the first batch he liked the "Outwork Yesterday" lyrics best.

**Why:** the first batch used shouted chant hooks and tech wordplay; he hated both the words and the sound.

**How to apply:** for any channel song, theme or jingle: write verse plus hook in full flowing lines, second person, no tech or AI wordplay, no age; prompt Flow for smooth articulate rap and say "no shouting, no gang vocals". My reading (not his words): the move away from AI in "channel branding" may reach beyond music; ask before applying it to titles or graphics. Detail: `Docs/FLOW_MUSIC_BRIEF_20261107.md`.
