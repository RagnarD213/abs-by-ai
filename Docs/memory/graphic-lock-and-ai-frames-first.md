Three rules from Dan's edits to Muhammad's first dedicated-shorts revision doc (2026-09-27):

1. **Lock the graphic style first.** No locked style for a format means the first deliverable is graphic variations on one video (editor's own, one imitating our locked style, two different ones: masculine, bold, modern, trustworthy). Every other graphic fix waits. If a style is locked, put its references in the brief / revision doc.
2. **AI clips: start and end frames approved before motion**, now for human editors too, in the video's orientation.
3. **Frequently, not always, open on an AI-generated clip.** Offer 2 concepts plus 1 of the editor's choice.

**Why:** renders were being thrown away when the graphics changed after the whole video was cut, for both human and AI editors.

**How to apply:** /revisions calibration pass 7 (rules 44-48), /editor-brief sections 4b/4c, VIDEO-RULES top section. See [[revisions-zero-edit-goal]], [[proven-direct-response-only]].
