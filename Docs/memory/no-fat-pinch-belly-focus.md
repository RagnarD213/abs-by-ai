Never build a shot around belly fat: no pinching or grabbing it, no push-in or crop centred on it. Applies to AI clips, stock, photos and graphics, in ads and organic videos, and matters most in the first 30 seconds.

**Why:** Dan rejected the RO-11 AI opener (heavy shirtless man on a scale grabbing his belly) on 2026-10-04: it risks disapproval for negative imagery and blocks promoting the video in engagement campaigns.

**How to apply:** show the situation, not the body part (his replacement: an overweight man at a table with a tiny plate of bland chicken and broccoli). In AI prompts say "do not show his stomach, no hands touching his body"; frame chest-up or hide the midsection. Full rule at the top of `.claude/skills/_shared/VIDEO-RULES.md`.
