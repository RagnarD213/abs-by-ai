Dan, 2026-10-07: the first follow-along workout scripts for the 10/17 shoot were "the weakest" and not realistic to film, because he cannot read a teleprompter during a round. The format he set:

- Filmed live, in one continuous take, outside by his pool. Cuts only at the beginning and the end.
- Nothing on the teleprompter during a round. All scripting is before the workout, after it, and between rounds.
- Each between-round script is about the length of the rest period at his usual talking speed, less 2 to 3 seconds to catch his breath. It briefly says how to do the next exercise.
- During the rest, a small video beside him labeled "Next Exercise" shows him doing the next exercise (picture-in-picture). Write that as a filming note.
- A workout with no rest between sets: he explains every exercise before the workout, with the picture-in-picture, and says nothing once it starts.

**Why:** the scripts have to be filmable by one person who is out of breath.

**How to apply:** `/scriptfromoutline` section O has the word budgets (about 50 words for a 30 second rest, 28 to 36 for 20 seconds) and the cue wording. Count every rest block with a script. A studio video that only talks about exercises is not a follow-along. Related: [[filming-scripts-first]].
