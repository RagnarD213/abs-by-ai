Dan, 2026-11-18, after Google Ads disapproved the Zepbound engagement ads (RO-28 "Why Zepbound Improves Your Health Even If You're Ripped", yt eLV6EluvyD0) for restricted drug terms a second time: never put Zepbound, other GLP-1 or drug names, needles, vials, pens or pharmacy imagery in the video title, description, thumbnail or ad text of any video that is or will be advertised. Neutral replacement title chosen for RO-28: "Got Abs? You need to see this."

**Why:** repeated "Eligible (Limited) / Policy (Restricted drug terms)" and disapprovals on the AT engagement ads; re-uploading and re-editing a flagged video costs an edit slot and time.

**How to apply:** at /video-setup, /youtube-packaging, /coverimage and engagement-ad creation, pick a neutral title and a thumbnail with no vial, needle or pharmacy scene from the start. Rename the drug back in only when the video's ads are all gone. Spoken words and in-film graphics are not covered; a slight re-edit makes a flagged film read as a new file, but its content may still be a risk, so say so.
