Two standing rules from Dan's GAT Upsell 4 round 1 review (2026-11-19):

1. Exercise demos shown anywhere in a video (app screens, phone scenes, cutaways) use the real-footage demos, not AI demos, wherever real footage exists. Pick where Dan looks most ripped; for general workout lines show one gym and one home/pool demo (his example: side laterals at the pool and at the gym).
2. In VSLs and upsells on sales pages, the call to action is text ("Click The Button Below To Try <Product>") with arrows pointing down. Never a button or pill graphic in the video.

**Why:** the AI demos read worse than his real footage; an in-video button makes older prospects (60s-70s) try to tap the video instead of the page's button.

**How to apply:** check every app or workout scene and every CTA graphic in a funnel video before review. Full text in `.claude/skills/_shared/VIDEO-RULES.md` (top). Related: [[exercise-demos-real-broll-first]], [[meal-showcases-appetizing-photo-first]], [[gat-upsells-cleaner-more-camera]].
