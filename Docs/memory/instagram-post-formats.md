Dan picked from the Instagram Template R1 mockups (2026-10-21): **C1 The Fix, C2 Receipts, C3 Steal My Workout** are the
carousel formats going forward, and the **cheat-sheet single** (protein per 100 calories) once it carries a small photo of
him. Rejected: **Swaps** ("a little too boring"), **Timeline** ("too much about me personally... not really a lot of
reason for the viewer to care"), and the Proof, Straight Talk, The Math and Routine singles.

**Why:** every post has to hand the viewer something to use (a fix, a finding, a workout, a reference). Dan's own story
only earns a place where it proves the viewer's takeaway.

**How to apply:** new Instagram and Facebook image posts use these four formats and the R1 look (ink, bone, red, yellow;
Anton and Manrope; real footage first). Do not pitch personal-journey carousels or quote cards again unless Dan asks.
Review pages for these are plain image pages, not a Design canvas (the canvas showed a black screen in his panel).
