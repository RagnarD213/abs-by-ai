Still images for ads (Google Performance Max, Demand Gen, any feed image) follow the direction Dan approved on 2026-10-01 after rejecting two rounds.

Rejected: a kettlebell and phone with "7 DAYS FREE", his photo in front of an empty gym, and text-heavy cards with bullets ("this is just bullshit that no one's going to click on", "looks like you're signing up to do a whole bunch of work", "too much text"). Also rejected for policy risk: a mirror image of a dad-bod man with a ripped reflection (any transformation).

Approved ("you nailed it"): one image tells a small story, with one 2 to 5 word headline from his own approved lines, white then yellow Manrope ExtraBold at the top.
- Style A: Dan's real photo, grinning, in front of an AI scene acting out the headline (furious trainers behind him, "HUMAN TRAINERS HATE HIM").
- Style B: a photoreal gray-haired dad around 50, strikingly lean, with his kids (his own idea). Attention comes from the mismatch.
- Style C: a comic staged scene (a fired trainer carrying his box while a robot spots a dad, "FIRE YOUR PERSONAL TRAINER").

**Why:** he wants images on the clickbait side of the line without crossing it; generic or informative images do not get clicked.
**How to apply:** lead with a scene that stops the scroll, not with the offer or the product. Show samples before writing a plan. Full spec: `Handoffs/handoff-20261001-campaign-images-build.md`. Related: [[ad-copy-no-unbelievable-claims]], [[thumbnail-no-claims]], [[proven-direct-response-only]].
