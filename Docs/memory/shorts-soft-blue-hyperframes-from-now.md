Dan, 2026-10-01, reviewing the SL-05 Stop Deadlifting shorts: "this is the last round of shorts I want to see with this graphic set going forward for new batches. I want to see everything made with the soft blue light and hyperframes graphics. The first time you do this in vertical and square, show me a thorough round of approval with all the assets pre-reviewed before you make it."

**Why:** he locked Soft Blue Light and the HyperFrames templates as the house graphics ([[hyperframes-decision]]); the J2 band and olive bars were only being carried on older shorts work.

**How to apply:** any new shorts batch (vertical or square) is built with Soft Blue Light + HyperFrames. The first vertical and the first square get a full approval round, every asset as a still on its real frame, before the build ([[review-page-what-i-decided]]). A batch already in revisions (SL-05) finishes in its current set. Rule text: `_shared/VIDEO-RULES.md`, "New shorts batches".
