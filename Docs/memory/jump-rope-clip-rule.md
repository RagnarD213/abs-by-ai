Dan, 2026-10-09, reviewing the RO-06 full film (the jump rope section at 3:51 used the both-feet clip as plain "Dan jumping
rope"): "I want to make a standing rule that this clip of me jumping over with both feet is meant to show a mistake. This
clip of me jumping over with both feet should never be used in videos unless I'm saying it's a mistake to jump over with
both feet. Basically, this clip is a mistake. Don't use it unless I'm illustrating a mistake. Always use the correct jump
rope footage of me quickly skipping over the rope, not the beginner footage of me going very slowly foot by foot, but the
footage of me skipping quickly like a boxer would."

- Correct clip: library `B0448` (roll C1674, high-knee stretch, about 155 steps a minute).
- Mistake demo only: `B0430` (both feet, a pause on each landing).
- Beginner demo only: `B0447` (slow, foot by foot). Not a correct-form clip.
- **Also both-feet, mistake demo only: 10/15 shoot roll `C1742`** (pool, Dan in green shorts with a knee sleeve). RO-28 used it as plain
  jump rope at 2:22 and Dan caught it again on 2026-10-27.

**Why:** the both-feet jump is a form mistake in his teaching; showing it as normal jump rope teaches the wrong thing.

**How to apply:** in every video, brief and revision doc, illustrate jump rope with the fast skip. If a film needs jump
rope twice, take a different part of the fast stretch of roll C1674 (my reading: do not repeat the same frames). The rule
is also in `.claude/skills/_shared/VIDEO-RULES.md` (top section) and in the clip library descriptions. Related:
[[cover-photo-selection]], [[clip-library]].
