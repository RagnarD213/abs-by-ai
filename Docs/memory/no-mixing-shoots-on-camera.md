Dan, 2026-10-10, on RO-03 (The Vacuum, workout only), after I planned to use vacuum holds from the 8/28 shoot between talking pieces from the 8/14 shoot: "I feel like it's going to look weird if we cut in footage from a different shoot and I change outfits and tan and everything." and "I just want to make sure we avoid mixing the two shoots. I feel like that's going to look weird if we mix shoots."

**Why:** Between shoots his sunglasses, necklace, shorts, tan and the background change, so sets from another day read as a different session.

**How to apply:** When a video's main footage shows Dan on camera, footage of him that the viewer reads as part of the same session (workout sets, demos, pickups between talking pieces) comes from the same shoot. If the shoot is short of footage, repeat what it has (he approved showing one hold three times) or say what is missing, and show him a side-by-side still of the two shoots before planning around the other one. My reading, not his words: short illustrative B-roll cutaways from other shoots, as in the approved talking videos, are a different case and stay allowed.
