Lessons from Dan's line edits to the 17-outline Shoot 3 batch (July 2026), which he otherwise called excellent:

- **No generic listicle content.** He deleted "The 5 Foods I Never Eat" entirely as "weak and generic — didn't add value." Every video needs a personal, specific, or contrarian angle only Dan can deliver; if the outline could be any fitness channel's video, cut it.
- **Real specifics beat evidence-based templates.** He replaced the templated supplement stack (creatine/D3/magnesium) with his actual timed stack (Athletic Greens on waking; post-workout food base + joint/skin/testosterone groups; Thorne Deep Sleep before bed). He takes AG1 and a Thorne test booster and does NOT take creatine (stomach problems) — never put those in a "don't take" list. Ask for or leave room for his real protocol instead of inventing a plausible one.
- **Workout format: Gymboss timed sets (30s work / 30s rest), not sets×reps**, for circuit-style videos — consistent with his older outlines.
- **Skill-progression videos (e.g. pushup levels) are demonstration-only** — no workout-only cut; "demonstrating levels makes sense, doing them all as one workout does not."
- **He adds psychology/identity angles** to physical topics (vacuum video: shirtless mirror scrutiny as anti-denial motivation; lockscreen images on pantry/fridge). Weave motivation psychology into workout content.
- **Blunt, socially spicy honesty is on-brand** (e.g. "fat girlfriend/fat friends" as weight-loss obstacles; pro-GLP-1 stance for plateaus; "alcohol should taste bad"). Don't sanitize.
- **Zepbound details he shipped:** 200 lb → 192 plateau → 181 on Zepbound over ~2 months (as of July 2026); prefers needles over Kwikpens, 1→2→2.5 mg ramp, Thursday-evening injection for weekend appetite control, 0.5 mg/week taper off against goal weight, ≥0.8 g protein per lb lean mass.
- Delivery format that works: outlines as a Google Doc (create_file with text/html), Dan edits with red highlights = delete, green = Claude adds content; read highlights back via HTML export ([[proof-banner-image-gen-process]] for image workflow; audience is men only, long-form YouTube first).
- **2026-08-07 batch review, killed 17 of 20 outlines — the sharpest calibration event yet.** Full lessons are written into `.claude/skills/ad-outlines/SKILL.md` ("Batch review 2026-08-07" section) since they're operational rules for that skill, not repeated here in full. The one-line versions: solve the prospect's PERCEIVED problem (he's fat, eats badly, doesn't work out — not scale accuracy, lighting, or body-fat-percentage nerdery), never assume he has a paid service he probably doesn't (gym membership, meal delivery, personal trainer), this is men-only so no group-class or "she's happy either way" reassurance framing (attractiveness/relationship-stakes as a stake to lose is now the wanted framing, not something to soften), never open by casting doubt on the product itself, and — the standing direction — be MORE controversial and direct-response-aggressive, MadMuscles/V-Shred style, not softened into polite observations. Compliance rules (no disease names, no sleep angle, no drug names, no "humiliating") still apply on top of the sharper tone.
