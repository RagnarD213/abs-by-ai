Dan edited the 2026-09-18 Zeeshan message and kept the result as the model. Three corrections:

1. **Be enthusiastically complimentary when the editor does something well.** He changed "The deadlift video is a good
   first cut." to "The deadlift video is a real good first cut!" and added "Thanks!" to the sign-off. Praise in these
   messages runs warmer than the measured tone of the revision doc. Exclamation marks are his, not a slip.
2. **Never name one editor to another.** "next to Muhammad's ad" became "next to my primary editor's ad". Zeeshan has
   no idea who Muhammad is, so the name is noise at best and awkward at worst. Say "my primary editor". This applies to
   the revision DOC as well as the message.
3. **The message is big picture only.** He deleted the line calling out the "AI Personal Traniner" typo: *"avoid
   mentioning very nitpicky small edits in these types of messages. I deleted one of those. Those are in the document.
   The message is really only for the big-picture things that we need to communicate, not the tiny tactical things."*
   He also cut the whole paragraph about the archive clip Claude had cut for him, because the clip and its link are in
   the doc.

**The shape that survived:** one line of thanks, a short paragraph closing out the finished video, a short warm
paragraph on the new one naming something specific that proves he watched it, the doc link, "three big things" as three
short paragraphs, one line saying the rest is in the doc, and a one-line sign-off asking for what he needs back.

**How to apply:** write the message at about half the length instinct suggests, keep every timecode and typo in the doc,
and never let a rival editor's name into anything an editor reads. No em dashes ([[no-em-dashes]]). The doc itself stays
in Dan's measured first person ([[revision-docs-in-dans-voice]]).
