Dan's instruction (2026-09-18), after reviewing the VSL Version B intro: don't say he ran an ad agency, or otherwise
identify him as a marketer/marketing professional, in ad or VSL copy.

**Why:** Saying he's in marketing reduces trust — it makes him sound like "a marketer pitching them something" instead
of a genuine fitness expert, which undercuts the whole pitch.

**How to apply:** where a script needs "I was running a company, stressed out, eating like garbage" as the reason he
let himself go, describe what AI did for his business without naming the industry — e.g. "I ran a company where AI
was coding my website, doing my bookkeeping" — never "ad agency," "agency," "marketing," or similar. Applies to all
future scriptwriting (VSL, ads, outlines). Related: [[revisions-zero-edit-goal]], [[vsl-version-b-intro]].
