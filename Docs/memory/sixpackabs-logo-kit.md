The SixPackAbs.com logo kit is at `sixpackabs/brand/logo/`, built 2026-11-05 from the lockup on the locked supplement labels. Dan is considering switching to this logo everywhere.

- `README.md` explains which file to use where.
- `logo-sheet.png` previews every version.
- Folders: `svg/`, `pdf/`, `png/` (high resolution), `png-web/`, `jpg/`, `icons/` (favicon, app icons, social avatar).
- The Open Graph image is `jpg/sixpackabs-og-1200x630.jpg`.
- Layouts: horizontal, stacked, symbol, wordmark. Colours: gold `#C9A766`, black `#1C1A17`, white, and solid gold-on-black, black-on-white and black-on-ivory.
- Gold goes on dark backgrounds only; use black on light ones.
- Rebuild with `python3 sixpackabs/brand/logo/_build/make_logo.py`.

The older `public/img/sixpackabs-logo.webp` (black, abs-grid symbol plus a lowercase ".com") is the previous logo. Related: [[sixpackabs-label-preferences]], [[sixpackabs-shop-design-direction]].
