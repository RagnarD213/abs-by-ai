On RA-15 (Never Pay A Nutritionist Again, AD) Dan rejected all five standard-mix designs (calm titles like NEVER PAY A NUTRITIONIST AGAIN, food scenes, museum relics) and three sinister ones (frowning photos, cyborg). He chose: a real photo with an **arrogant smirk** (studio-gray-55, hands behind head) in front of a blurred Codex crowd of **angry nutritionists pointing at him**, text **NUTRITIONISTS DESPISE HIM** in white Impact, DESPISE HIM on a tilted red banner. His words: the text should look "attention-getting and controversial", and the expression must match the text ("I'm looking arrogant, maybe smirking"); stern or frowning faces did not match.

**Why:** a calm headline that restates the ad title did nothing for him; a clickbait "they hate him" line with a smug face and the hated group visible sells the click.

**How to apply:** for a "replace X with AI" ad thumbnail, include at least one option in this shape: "[X] DESPISE HIM", a smirk photo (shortlist: studio-gray-55, studio-blue-28, photo-179, studio-blue-291), the X crowd angry behind him. Still follow the five-choice rule ([[thumbnail-design-system]]) unless he narrows it.
