Dan, 2026-09-10, reviewing the finished-ad thumbnails: **"avoid claims in these kinds of thumbnails because they'll
rarely be seen, but they'll be scrutinized by Google Ads compliance."** He replaced "THIS PICTURE GOT ME ABS" with
"HOW TO USE AI TO GET IN SHAPE". Two more rules from the same review: a vertical ad video gets a **9:16** thumbnail,
not a 16:9 one; and pool-shoot photos are **cropped at the waistline** so the swim brief never reads ("make it look
like regular shorts").

**Why:** an ad's thumbnail is a compliance surface, not a marketing surface — a claim there can get the ad or the
account flagged for almost no reach in return.
**How to apply:** headline = how-to / topic / command, never a result or a number; check the video's aspect before
building; recipe and measured waist crops in `/youtube-packaging` and `/coverimage`. Related: [[thumbnail-design-system]],
[[ad-suspension-prevention]].
