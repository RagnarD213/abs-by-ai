On 2026-10-11, after ruling on 90 tracked suggestions in the 10/17 shoot doc (81 accepted as written), Dan said: "all the edits that you're highly confident that I will accept are just made into the document. The only ones you are putting into track changes in Google Docs for me to review are ones where you're not certain whether or not I'll accept it."

Always straight in, his list: spelling, capitalization, punctuation, edits to filming directions, "anything that does not substantially change what I'm reading off the teleprompter", a left-out word or a sentence that makes no sense without the fix, and swaps he consistently accepts ("every day" for "every single day").

Tracked, his words: "sometimes I accept that, sometimes not, or if it's a new kind of edit or a complex edit."

Reaffirmed 2026-10-13. During the reaction shorts cut he said "make sure you're using the track changes feature in Google Drive so I can review all these edits", and I tracked five spelling and capitalization fixes along with the cuts. Afterwards: "only changes you're not sure whether or not to accept need to be done with track changes. Things like correcting a spelling error, capitalization, or things like that should be made directly in the document, since there's no need for me to review those changes... Just make those changes without track changes so that I don't spend time reviewing them going forward." My reading: a request to use track changes means the edits that need his judgment; it never pulls the simple fixes into review.

**Why:** he was accepting most edits one card at a time and wants to review only the ones that need his judgment.

**How to apply:** `/copy-edit`, section "Straight in, or tracked", holds both lists, the counts behind them and the rule for moving a category between them. He stated it for the copy polish skill. My reading, not his words: it also covers the mechanical follow-through on an instruction of his inside a script doc (renumbering, a title he dictated), and it does not make a new spoken line or a cut direct. Direct edits go through the Docs connector with `targetRevisionId` (safe while he is typing in the doc) and are checked with a before and after diff.
