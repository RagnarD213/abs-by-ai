Dan edited a client feedback reply before sending it (2026-11-27) and asked me to learn from the changes.

1. Answer the client's own greeting by name ("Thanks <name>! Hope you had a great Thanksgiving too."). I had written "It was a good one", a fact about him I made up.
2. A question about a choice (here: removing AI labels) gets his recommendation plus a concrete business reason from experience (ad networks may disapprove or limit ads), then the offer to do either. I had written a neutral "which would you like?".
3. End with a request for their answers soon and a committed delivery day ("I'll get you the next draft by Tuesday"). I had written "I'll come back to you with timing".
4. He cut my extra ask that an earlier message had already made (my reading: ask only what this round needs).

**Why:** clients get a clear recommendation and a date, and the message sounds like him.

**How to apply:** every client message draft (Upwork, email) for any client job. Put a proposed delivery day in the draft and flag it for him to change. Rule also in `client-vsl/reference/ROUNDS.md`, "Client messages". Related: [[client-ledger-gates-client-work]].
