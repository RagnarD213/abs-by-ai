Dan, 2026-10-07, on the 10/17 shoot scripts: most intros had a "Watch this video if X" line. In some it made sense, but in a lot of them it "seemed very formulaic and like AI slop": it repeated the line before it, or was inserted where it did not fit. He wants "more judgment and subtlety": only when it makes sense, with the language varied.

**Why:** the line came from his own two outlines and an earlier handoff turned it into a required third intro bullet for every video.

**How to apply:** treat it as one optional tool. Keep it when it names a viewer or a reason the first two lines did not (for example "if you think Zepbound is only for people who are fat"). Otherwise cut it or say the same thing another way ("If you've done hundreds of crunches and still can't see your abs, this video is for you."). Across a batch, most intros should not carry it.
