On a green-screen reaction or commentary video, the background behind Dan is never plain and keeps changing. Dan, 2026-10-24 (RO-24 round 2 review, after mockups of 8 options): he approved options 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8 and rejected 5. "Cycle through all of these options where it's appropriate in the video... always keeping the background visually interesting, always changing up the background... put extra effort into establishing a template for making these visually interesting."

The approved types (mockups: /Volumes/Expansion/_edit_work/RO-24/round3/mock/background_options*.jpg):
1. The person's clip or photos behind Dan, softened (blurred, darkened, navy-tinted).
2. Their clip full screen, Dan reacting in a corner (bigger than in reaction Shorts); also "Dan to the side while the clip plays in the background".
3. A scrolling photo wall. 4. Side panel: their before/after pair with weight lost and medication, Dan beside it.
6. Floating tilted photo cards drifting behind him. 7. Big faint type of the number and the drug, used when the number is said.
8. A realistic studio set (Codex plate) with a wall TV showing the person's clip or before/after.
Not 5 (the flat built TV wall).

**Why:** Dan: "we can't just have a white background the whole time"; the plain backdrop read as too plain after the intro.

**How to apply:** every green-screen reaction/commentary edit, Claude and Codex. Plan the background per section at cuts: right after an intro, 6 or 3; when a clip exists, 2 (or Dan to the side with the clip behind); when a number is said, 7; transformations, 4 with before/after, weight and medication; long commentary, 8 or 1. Before/after pairs show weight lost and medication when known; no photo repeated where avoidable. Template: built in RO-24 round 3 (`Handoffs/handoff-20261024-ro24-celebrity-ozempic-round3.md`).
