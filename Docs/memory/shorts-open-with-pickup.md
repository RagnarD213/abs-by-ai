A Short cut from a long-form must sound like a dedicated video. It never opens on a list number or a line that points back at the long-form ("Number two", "next", "this one"). Dan records a one-sentence pickup into his mic in the editing session ("Here's why Thursday evening is the best time to inject Zepbound"), and a clip, graphic or AI clip covers it. One point per Short: he split a candidate that argued both pen versus needle and which dose.

**Why:** Dan, 2026-10-11, on the SL-06 Zepbound shortlist: "avoid making it very obviously seem like something cut from a long-form video out of context, like starting with number 2 or number 3. Instead, we'll just record a short pickup into my mic right here in Claude, and then cover it with a graphic or AI clip or something." And: "It's a little confusing to have two points in a video like that."

**How to apply:** at segment selection, write three pickup options per Short through dan-voice-writer and send them WITH the shortlist, so he records when he picks. Full procedure: `.claude/skills/_shared/VIDEO-RULES.md`, "A Short opens like a dedicated video". Related: [[shorts-reason-to-watch]], [[shorts-show-whole-exercise]], [[dan-voice-guide]].

**Changed 2026-10-13 (Dan, on RO-20):** pickups are no longer recorded on the desk mic in the editing session. He heard desk-mic pickups in the first minutes and said "that audio doesn't work... I'm just going to record these pickups on my studio set instead", and asked for every outstanding pickup to be collected into the shoot doc and a pickups-only teleprompter document. **How to apply:** write the pickup line, put it on the next shoot's pickups list, tell him the job waits for the shoot; a desk take is a timing placeholder only.
