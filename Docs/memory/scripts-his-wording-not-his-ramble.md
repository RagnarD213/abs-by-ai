On 2026-10-10 Dan took the 20-pair blind voice test (his real words against Claude's imitation) and left a note on
each pair. Two things in his notes go beyond the score (13 right of 19).

**1. A transcript of him talking is not what he wants in a script.** On several pairs he said his real off-the-cuff
passage was rambling, run-on and unorganized, "might not be ideal for a script", and that Claude's version "might be
better" as a script. His words on one: "I don't know if I'd want to write a script which is kind of rambling and
unorganized like this with no line breaks."

**2. What he named as Claude's tells:** mild, hedged phrasing where he says it straight ("you need to get them out of
your life" and "maybe you need to break up" against his "got to go" and the blunt question; he called it "a very
Claude way of phrasing it, where you're trying to be mild and inoffensive"); words a notch too formal or neat
("several", "reoxygenated", "cursory", "make it available to more men"); a caveat before the instruction. He says
"totally broke", "literally", opens key sentences with "Listen", and writes "number 1, number 2". His general verdict,
again: Claude is "overly cautious, overly disclaiming", afraid to be controversial, and puts safety ahead of a
compelling message.

**Why:** the voice work could have chased the wrong target (verbatim run-on speech) because that is what the judges
compare against.

**How to apply:** when scripting for Dan, take his word choices, small words and directness from how he talks, but
give the script order and line breaks. Say the instruction first; a caveat, if truly needed, comes after and short.
Prefer the plain word. My reading, not his words: "sounds like him" and "a script he would read as written" are two
separate measures, and the second is his edit rate. Details: `Docs/voice-bench/2026-10-10-dan-blind-test.md`. Related:
[[dan-voice-guide]], [[script-zero-edit-lessons]], [[voice-corpus-sources]].
