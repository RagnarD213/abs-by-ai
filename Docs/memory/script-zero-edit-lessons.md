On 2026-09-21 Dan edited the four 9/23 shoot long-form scripts and asked that the script skills learn from it so future scripts need no edits. About 90 percent of lines survived; his changes were: rewrote 3 of 4 openers (strongest first-person claim, never "Today we're talking about", "Listen:" on paragraph two), removed underselling of the video's topic, ended paragraphs on a benefit payoff instead of a kicker or flat fact, added "That's why I do X, and why you should too", replaced an easy way out with higher stakes ("cash out your 401k"), filled the experience section with his real numbers, cut recycled bits / cute asides / standalone swears / rare medical caveats / clever arithmetic, and cut the generic closing app pitch in 2 of 4.

**Why:** his goal is zero edits; earlier Opus scripts needed heavy editing and read as generic Claude output.

**How to apply:** before writing ANY script (content, ad, short), read the WHAT DAN CHANGED section at the top of `.claude/skills/scriptfromoutline/SKILL.md` and `references/dan-edits-2026-09-21.md`, run the zero-edit checklist (process step 3b), then the blind reviewer agent. After he edits the next batch, diff again against the as-delivered copy in `Docs/` and extend the section. Related: [[revisions-zero-edit-goal]], [[dan-personal-facts-for-scripts]], [[video-outline-style]].

Second round, same day (calories pair): when the outline has a hook Dan wrote, it is the opener verbatim and he deletes anything wrapped around it, including HOOK TAKES; a study must prove the outcome the viewer cares about (weight lost, calories eaten) with a big plain number, never a surrogate marker (he rejected a fasting study with no weight loss); added outline points must be new to the viewer and squarely on the thesis; swears aim at advice, never at a group of people. Full write-up: sections J to N of the same SKILL.md.

Third round, 2026-10-20 (seven 10/23 pool long-forms): his biggest note, repeated across scripts, was the 1.5 to 2 minutes of warm-up between the hook and the first point (agenda, "stick with me", "I know that sounds crazy", "this video is for you"); write for a viewer with ADD, at most a few sentences before point one. His line edits then added a why to every claim tied to his daily routine, unflattering specifics in his own story, softer mechanism claims but blunter verdicts, numbers where the viewer acts, three proofs at most with no recap. Section THE 10/23 POOL LONG-FORMS in the same SKILL.md; rule also in `_shared/HOOKS.md`.
