On 2026-09-21 Dan edited the four 9/23 shoot long-form scripts and asked that the script skills learn from it so future scripts need no edits. About 90 percent of lines survived; his changes were: rewrote 3 of 4 openers (strongest first-person claim, never "Today we're talking about", "Listen:" on paragraph two), removed underselling of the video's topic, ended paragraphs on a benefit payoff instead of a kicker or flat fact, added "That's why I do X, and why you should too", replaced an easy way out with higher stakes ("cash out your 401k"), filled the experience section with his real numbers, cut recycled bits / cute asides / standalone swears / rare medical caveats / clever arithmetic, and cut the generic closing app pitch in 2 of 4.

**Why:** his goal is zero edits; earlier Opus scripts needed heavy editing and read as generic Claude output.

**How to apply:** before writing ANY script (content, ad, short), read the WHAT DAN CHANGED section at the top of `.claude/skills/scriptfromoutline/SKILL.md` and `references/dan-edits-2026-09-21.md`, run the zero-edit checklist (process step 3b), then the blind reviewer agent. After he edits the next batch, diff again against the as-delivered copy in `Docs/` and extend the section. Second round, same day (calories pair): when the outline has a hook Dan wrote, it is the opener verbatim and he deletes anything wrapped around it, including HOOK TAKES; a study must prove the outcome the viewer cares about (weight lost, calories eaten) with a big plain number, never a surrogate marker (he rejected a fasting study with no weight loss); added outline points must be new to the viewer and squarely on the thesis; swears aim at advice, never at a group of people.
