When Dan does his "excuse" voice in a video (an impression of someone whining an excuse, e.g. "Dan, I don't have time to go to the gym"), plan an AI clip of a character saying the line with the lips synced to Dan's own recording, and bring its start and end frames in the first approval round without being asked.

Dan, 2026-10-08, approving the RO-06 "How To Work Out At Home On A Budget" first minute (round 4): "I really love the way that you did the lip-syncing and the slap. Both of those turned out significantly better than I expected. I think in future ones where I'm doing the 'excuse' voice, like making fun of losers making excuses, let's get started in frames for those in the future because this turned out real good."

**Why:** the trainer-Dan and whiny-client clips (Veo 3.1 Fast from Codex frame pairs, sync/lipsync-2-pro driven by his recorded impression, a slap landing on "destroy") came out better than he expected, and each worked on the first take for $2.19 in total.

**How to apply:** my reading of "get started in frames" is: propose the frames up front whenever a script, outline or edit has an excuse-voice line. Scope is every future video (scripts, long-form, shorts, ads). Frame approval before motion, the $5 per video clip budget and the no-belly-framing rule still hold; never generate a new voice.
