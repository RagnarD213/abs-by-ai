A reaction short opens on the clip. Dan is on screen (green screen, the clip playing behind him) watching it without speaking. When the clip ends he gives his reaction. There is no spoken line from him before the clip, and his first line never repeats or summarizes what the clip just said.

Dan, 2026-10-13, while editing "10-17 Shoot - Teleprompter 2 - Green Screen": "a lot of these are written to have me deliver a line, then we show the clip, and then I give the rest of the script. I don't like that format. I want the clip to play first. I'm on screen reacting to it, and then I deliver my reaction. I'm on screen non-verbally watching the clip as it plays in the background, and then I deliver the reaction. No line for me before the clip starts." And on the Clavicular looks short: "There's no reason for me to say, 'Clavicular says your looks matter more than your personality,' or just repeat what was just said in the clip instead. It should just go into my reaction."

**Why:** all 15 reaction shorts written on 10-13 and the 4 Clavicular shorts opened with a hook line that named the person and restated the claim, then cut to the clip. The clip is the hook; a line before it delays it and says the same thing twice.

**How to apply:**
- Structure: clip (Dan visible, silent), then his verdict in his first sentence, then the lesson, then the close. His own first lines from that edit: "Bill Maher is right here. Fat shaming is good.", "Dr. Mike is right. Willpower is now obsolete, and Tirzepatide has replaced it.", "This is a genius move that anyone who's drinking soda should copy right now.", "This is hands down the stupidest fitness trend I've seen on social media.", "It's easy to laugh at Bert Kreischer for this. But YOU are probably doing the same thing."
- No HOOK TAKES for a reaction short. Alternate first lines before the clip have nowhere to go; he deleted them from every script he edited.
- The filming script's first cue is the clip, with a note that Dan is on screen watching and does not speak until it ends.
- A silent clip (an ad with no speech) may get one line of context after it; that is not repeating.
- A long-form reaction video still has an intro and short lead-ins between clips ("Next up is Amy Schumer."). A lead-in must not restate the clip either: "Even Clavicular agrees with me on this one. He says about 70%..." was cut to its first sentence.
- This replaces the hook-first shape in `/shorts-scripting` and `_shared/HOOKS.md` for reaction shorts only. Non-reaction shorts keep the spoken hook.
