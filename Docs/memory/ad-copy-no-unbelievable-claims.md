Google limited every Ad 1 ad for CLICKBAIT within minutes on "This Picture Got Me Abs" / "Abs At 40 - The Photo That Did It", and disapproved the description "…the AI picture that got him started, and what he did next."

**Why:** Dan (2026-09-10): "This picture got me abs (I think) would be too much. It might seem unbelievable if you don't know the context." Most viewers never read the copy (in-stream), but Google scrutinises it, and a limited ad never spends in this account.

**How to apply:** write the outcome as his plain first-person history — "How I Got Abs At 40", "How AI Got Me Abs", "How I Got Abs With AI" — never as a reveal or a withheld payoff ("what he did next", "the photo that did it"). When Google flags a line, rewrite ONLY that line and resubmit (his instruction), leave the rest. Rule 2 in `scripts/ads/ytads/headline-style.md`. Related: [[ad-retry-rule-and-no-trick]].
