In square and vertical versions, clips and photos fill the frame. Put one in a box (card on the blue field) only when filling would cut something that matters, it is a phone screen, or a label truly has no clear spot.

**Why:** Dan, 2026-10-02, on the Ad 6 square look page (centred man with a coffee cup shown in a card): "I don't feel like there's any reason to crop that into the box. Let's make that a rule for squaring verticals going forward: we should avoid cropping things into boxes unless there's a reason to do that."

**How to apply:** default to full frame; state the reason beside every boxed item on the review page. Rule text: `_shared/VIDEO-RULES.md`. Related: [[reuse-finished-footage-not-raw]] (same review: B-roll of Dan must be the color-corrected final, never raw).
