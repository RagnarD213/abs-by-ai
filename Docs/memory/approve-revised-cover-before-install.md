When Dan asks for changes to a thumbnail or cover he already picked, build the revision, show him the final file in every layout, and wait for his approval before installing, uploading or scheduling it.

**Why:** On 2026-10-22 his SL-18 Short 2 cover D revision (red X, new title) was installed straight into Blotato; he said "I want you to have me approve the final short before you install in the future."

**How to apply:** unrevised picks from the review sheet still go straight to setup. A revision is a stop point: show it, wait, then swap it on every platform and read back. Rule in `.claude/skills/_shared/VIDEO-RULES.md`. Related: [[cover-picks-ai-dan-acting-topic]].
