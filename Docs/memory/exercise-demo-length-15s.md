Every Trainer exercise demo (real or AI) targets 15 seconds and is never shorter than 10 seconds. Dan, 2026-11-15, after a 7.7 s chin-up and a 4 s walking lunge: "It seems too brief if it's just 1 or 2 reps. Every clip from now on, I want you to target 15 seconds and a minimum length of 10 seconds."

**Why:** short demos with one or two reps and one cue feel thin to Dan; he wants a fuller explanation (he asked for knee-not-spiked and calf cues on the lunge, and rep-pace cues on the chin-up).

**How to apply:** plan narration of about 5 cues (setup, the main movement, a form point, a common mistake, the pace of the reps) and enough footage to carry it. When clean footage runs short, chain more angles or reps (checking leg alternation and edges at every cut) instead of shipping short; say on the review page if a demo still lands under 15 s. Approved demos from before 11-15 (sled push 7.4 s, Batch 2 at 8-12 s) were not redone. Related: [[exercise-demos-real-broll-first]].
