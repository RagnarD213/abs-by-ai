On the 27 gym shorts for the 10/23 shoot Dan rewrote 23 of 26 openers and kept the bodies: "A lot of the beginnings were
not attention-getting. They started off in a very informational, claudish, straightforward type of way... You're
not putting enough effort into the beginning... I want you to be able to write these without me needing to rewrite
these so heavily."

**Why:** nobody watches past a weak first five seconds, however good the body is.

**How to apply:** write the opener last and spend the most effort on it. For a how-to: rank the exercise in absolutes
("the king of back exercises"), attack the popular alternative ("Stop doing squats - the leg press is way better"),
name the look he wants ("V-taper", "blocky abs"), then "Here's how to do them right." Never open on a bare label
("This is the pull-up."). Highly relevant, controversial when true. Full rules and his before/after pairs:
`.claude/skills/_shared/HOOKS.md`. Related: [[shorts-reason-to-watch]], [[shorts-open-with-pickup]].
