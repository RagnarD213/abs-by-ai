Every piece of B-roll (Dan's own library clips, raw rolls, dark stock) is colour corrected to sit with the film's approved look before it goes on a review page or into a video. Dan, RO-20 round 1 (2026-10-11): "Color correct all B-roll used in this video and all videos going forward."

**Why:** three of his own pool clips (B0455, B0456, B0434) looked "excessively dark" beside the kitchen footage. The library notes said they were graded (S-Log3 LUT); they were still dark because they were shot in low sun and shade.

**How to apply:** grade each B-roll clip against the presenter frames it cuts next to, then look at its still beside a talking-head still. A library "graded" note is not proof. If a grade cannot rescue it, use a clip that is already bright. Full rule: `.claude/skills/_shared/VIDEO-RULES.md`, "All B-roll is colour corrected". Related: [[broll-choose-where-dan-looks-best]].
