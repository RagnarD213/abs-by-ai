When proposing Shorts (any /shorts batch, extracted or recut), show each candidate's title, kind, length and the FULL word-for-word transcript of what it will say, in cut order, pickup line first, with skipped passages marked. Include already-picked Shorts too.

**Why:** Dan, 2026-10-22 on SL-16 (Intermittent Fasting): a timecode table with opening/closing quotes was "too confusing for me to tell what short is going to have which content." He asked for this as the standard process going forward.

**How to apply:** take the text from the film's corrected .srt trimmed to the planned cut points (not raw Whisper, which mishears words). Rule is also in `/shorts` SKILL.md and VIDEO-RULES.md. Related: [[shorts-reason-to-watch]].
