I just want one version per script, and I want you to keep in mind what has worked, what I've accepted, and what has worked historically... Use a similar style to our videos which have been most successful and which I have approved in the past. Take into account what I approved and what I preferred in the upsell and the VSL writing task. Just realize this is a different type, so you shouldn't use those types of writing... You do your best to get something that I'm not going to edit and that I'm going to approve as is."

**Why:** the three-styles-blind method is for ads and sales copy. For content videos he wants one finished script per video, not choices to wade through.

**How to apply:**
- Content scripts (long videos, Shorts, reactions): one script each, built on the hook he bolded and the outline as he left it, his lines word for word. No HOOK TAKES, no alternate versions, no menu.
- Carry over what he approved in the sales-copy work as habits, not as formats: keep his strong wording, add nothing the outline did not ask for, keep proof short, no staccato fragments, no softening, nothing that drags before the point.
- Do not use sales structures (option one / option two, CTA every two minutes, TestoGreens or V Shred phrasing) in content videos.
- Unanswered questions in the outline: never invent his habit or number; write around the gap and tell him which gaps were written around.
- My reading, not his words: this is the standing way to script content from an approved outline, not only this batch.
