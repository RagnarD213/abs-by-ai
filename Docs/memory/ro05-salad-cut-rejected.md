Claude's RO-05 "How I Make My Daily Salad" cut (r10, after 10 revision rounds, audio gate 13/13) was rejected on 2026-09-23: "This is not usable. This is not something that we can publish." Causes: grade lifted mids to median luma 0.38 (Muhammad's kitchen ads 0.22-0.27, saturation 0.32-0.39), a whoosh on every transition, generic self-designed graphics instead of Muhammad's, no finished-product hook, punch-ins that cropped hair the camera captured. He called the effort a waste of tokens and re-ran the job from scratch in Fable and Codex Astra (handoffs `Handoffs/handoff-20260923-ro05-recut-{fable,astra}.md`).

**Why:** none of those failures is measured by the delivery gate; passing gates is not publishable.

**How to apply:** for any organic/ad edit, prove grade (stills at Muhammad's numbers), graphics and transitions (style board from his frames) and one finished 60-90 s section side by side with Muhammad BEFORE rendering the full video, and put the look in Dan's first approval packet. Open recipe/how-to videos on the finished dish. Measure hair densely per shot; never punch into captured hair.
