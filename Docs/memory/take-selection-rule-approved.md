On 2026-09-24 Dan reviewed `_shared/cut/takes.py`'s picks on a labelled clip of every take of the spray-tan roll (C1512) and agreed with all three: intro take 5 of 5, closing take 4 of 4, and the 4:00 stumble cut as the whole restated sentence ("the second one is definitely better"). His words: "usually the last take is clean. If there's no problem with it, keep the last take", but "not all the time, maybe there's a stronger take before."

**Why:** he retakes because something bothered him, so the last take is the default; a hesitation, restart or noise inside it is the "problem" that hands the pick to the latest clean earlier take.

**How to apply:** trust the picker's default on a roll; when it flags "no fluent take" or "needs a listen", that is the case he means by a stronger earlier take, so show him those groups only, on a clip of the takes, never as a table.
