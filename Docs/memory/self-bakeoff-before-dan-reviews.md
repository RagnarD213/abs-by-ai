Run the look and sound comparison BEFORE the first review, not after a rejection. Ship my own recommendation; offer the runner-up as a one-click alternative.

Dan, 2026-10-10, RO-07 round 1 (he rejected colour and sound, I then built options, and he picked both of my recommendations): "I feel like you could be working up these options before I even review, and then I chose both of your recommendations. If you had just worked up these options for yourself before the review and then gone with the recommendations, I think that we could have saved some time here."

**Why:** the first minute went out with a grade picked from measurements (never looked at beside an approved video) and a voice the audio gate passed 12 of 12 that a blind listen scored below its own untreated lav. Both faults were findable in ten minutes.

**How to apply:** `.claude/skills/_shared/PRE-RENDER-APPROVAL.md`, section "Before Dan sees anything". Trigger: a set with no approved look or sound, or one that measures differently (dark, echoey, new mic layout). At least three grades viewed at full size beside two approved videos; at least four voice treatments including the untreated lav, blind ranked; a treatment that loses to the untreated lav never ships.
