Every audio comparison or audition clip starts with Dan speaking in its first second and is speech throughout. Dan, 2026-10-11, on the RO-19 voice options: "the first 28 seconds were nothing... going forward, just always make sure it starts with speech immediately."

**Why:** I typed a roll time for the audition window that fell in the gap between two takes (27 s of room tone, 4 s of speech), ran nine blind listening trials on it, chose a voice from them and never listened to the clip. The whole comparison was void and he found it by ear.

**How to apply:** take the window from a kept piece of the cut, never a typed roll time; measure it before any trial (speech in the first 0.5 s, no pause over 1.5 s); listen to one option before sending anything. When he asks for voice options, build the ones he names (on RO-19: four warm, less-room-echo versions plus a few with slightly more bass for authority).
