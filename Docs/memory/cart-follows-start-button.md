So the cart cannot show or mention their uploaded photo, their goal picture, body fat or goal weight. It must make sense as the very next screen after "Start My 7-Day Free Trial" ($0 today), so the offer on the cart matches that button.

Also: do not put Dan's before and after pictures in the cart.
