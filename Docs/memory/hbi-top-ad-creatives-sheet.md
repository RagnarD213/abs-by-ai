Google Sheet "Top 10 All Time HBI Ad Creatives": `1RtcUMgsJAdOHu5VKKaMOhhVXNvGi8DvfZ8XU3CCrKtw`. Columns: name, script link, YouTube link, lifetime spend, lifetime CPA, creative producer (several are Social Response Marketing, Dan's agency, so the scripts are his).

Dan pointed to it on 2026-11-12 as a better model for Great Again T food ads than a TestoGreens-style or V Shred-style script: "model the 5 foods."

- "Five Foods | Jesse": $1,691,482 lifetime, CPA 69.79. Script in Doc `1nCn7t0bF3aC0GrJZiG04UD26TRl86EhWCCR4EY_XAUQ` ("Jesse's Scripts For 9/10 Shoot", which also holds "Eat This, Not That If You Have Arthritis" and the "Protein = Pain" adaptations). Video: youtu.be/K0iACRzW4kU.
- Its device, in the script's own HYPOTHESIS line: reveal the first food and leave the others under cloths, never revealed, to build desire for the full video. Hook, credential, "Let's start with the first food you should NEVER eat…", "That's right -- tomatoes", three plain sentences of why, "try eliminating it for a few weeks and I guarantee you'll feel a difference", "just one of the health foods … four others", "click Watch Now", what else is in the presentation, a deadline, "I'll see you there!"
