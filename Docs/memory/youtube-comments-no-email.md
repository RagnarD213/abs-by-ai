YouTube removed two @danrosefit comments within minutes on 2026-11-09 because they contained dan@absbyai.com (they vanished from the page and from Dan's comment history). The same offer without the email stayed up. Dan agreed: "I think it was the email address thing."

**Why:** outreach that silently disappears looks sent but reaches no one.
**How to apply:** never put an email, phone or link in a YouTube comment; ask them to reply in the thread. After posting, reload the page and check the comment is still there. Dan also asked to post outreach comments himself: type them in his Chrome tab and leave them unposted.
