Do not put a word in FULL CAPS in every lower third or card. Title capitalization is the default; capitals are for the rare graphic where one word really carries the emphasis.

Dan, 2026-10-09, reviewing RO-07 "Why You MUST Work Out Every Day" round 1 (he listed 16 of 45 lower thirds to change back to normal capitalization): "With the graphics, a lot of the time you're just blindly capitalizing words in a very AI-slop deterministic way. I want you to use more subtlety here. Capitalizing these words in the graphics is good every now and then when we need to make emphasis on a word, but if you're just capitalizing random words in every graphic, then there is no emphasis. I need you to use more subtlety and judgment, not a deterministic rule."

**Why:** emphasis on every graphic is no emphasis, and it reads as machine output. Labels (exercise names, round names, "Gymboss", "Morning", "Night") never need capitals.

**How to apply:** write every point in title case first. Then ask of the whole film: which few graphics hold a real contrast or punch word? My reading: about one in six at most. Exercise names, section labels, times of day, app names and list items stay normal. This narrows the 2026-09-18 KEY POINT format ("the one or two words that carry the punch in FULL CAPS"), which I had been applying to every graphic. See [[no-em-dashes]] for the other "reads as AI" rule.
