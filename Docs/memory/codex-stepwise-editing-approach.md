On 2026-09-28, reviewing Claude's RO-05 Fable recut (a full 15-minute cut built in one shot, then reviewed), Dan said: *"the reason why Codex is making way better videos than you is that it's taking a way more stepwise approach. Rather than trying to one-shot it and edit the video all in one, there are many small rounds of approval. I want you to copy this approach going forward."* He ordered every Claude video skill rewritten to the Codex method (studied from the WV-01 VSL rounds).

**Why:** Dan is pickiest about the opening and the graphics; a full render before those are locked wastes tokens and gets rejected on sight.

**How to apply:** for every video: (1) export only the FIRST MINUTE for approval (colour, intro, pacing); (2) show EVERY graphic first as a still screenshot with its exact text and timing, Dan edits text and approves, then the moving version; (3) approve every AI clip (frames first), stock and B-roll clip; (4) only when everything is approved render the full video. Each round ends with a handoff; the next round runs in a fresh session. Graphics are Soft Blue Light (`_shared/SOFTBLUE.md`). Related: [[ro05-salad-cut-rejected]], [[graphic-lock-and-ai-frames-first]], [[codex-ds17-beat-claude-lessons]].

**Confirmed 2026-09-29** after RO-05 round 2 (grade options, new opening, first minute, 47 graphic stills): *"I like this process much better than what we were doing before. This is going to be a huge breakthrough and I think using this process you can get your video edits up to Codex's quality. It might even justify moving these videos back to you. This new process is very good. Let's continue this for all video editing."* Keep it for every video; the routing back to Claude is his call, not yet made (see [[video-editing-executor-routing]]). Round 3 also showed why the moving check matters: 3A left cards that were clear on the approved still covered him on handheld kitchen footage.

**Corrected the same day (2026-09-29):** the per-item approvals were too much. The steps stay; the approvals do not. See [[decision-budget-per-video]].
