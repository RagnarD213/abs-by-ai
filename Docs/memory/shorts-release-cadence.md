Dan approved on 2026-10-17: five Shorts a week at 9 AM Chicago. Thursday is the one dedicated Short; Monday (from
Sunday's long-form), Tuesday, Friday (from Wednesday's long-form) and Saturday are Shorts cut from long-forms. Each new
long-form gets 3 Shorts. Every already-edited Short releases in 2026; a Short still to edit gets at least 10 days.
Long-form social copies moved to noon so 9 AM stays the Shorts slot. Dated plan: `Docs/SHORTS_RELEASE_CALENDAR.md`.

**Why:** ~50 dedicated Shorts in the pipeline vs ~100 long-form Shorts expected; three of the top four channel Shorts
were cut from long-forms; Thursday had the best median views (1,369), Saturday the worst (876).

**How to apply:** schedule Shorts by the calendar; `video_topup.py` sends DS jobs to Thursday and others to
Mon/Tue/Fri/Sat. Related: [[longform-strength-ranking]].
