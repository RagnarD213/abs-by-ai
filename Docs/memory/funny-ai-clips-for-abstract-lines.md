When a line has no straightforward literal clip to generate, propose a funny, obviously unreal AI gag that acts the line out, instead of a forced literal picture.

Dan, 2026-10-10, approving the RO-07 A3 frames for "life is going to punch you in the face" (a giant cartoon boxing glove on a spring comes in through the front door and knocks the man down): "I think a lot of times, when we have a hard-to-illustrate AI concept like this (where there isn't a straightforward literal clip to generate), doing something funny like this is good. It's obviously not real, but making it funny, I think, is going to make it more accepted by the viewer. This looks like it'll be real funny and entertaining. I really like this one."

**Why:** his words: a clip that is plainly not real but funny is accepted by the viewer more readily; it also entertains.

**How to apply:** when writing AI clip concepts, a line with a real literal picture gets that picture played straight; a line without one gets a slapstick or cartoon idea, big enough to read at a glance (his round 1 note on the first A3: the glove was too small and came out of the gym bag; he wanted it much larger and coming from outside). Frames before motion as always. My reading: this is a preference for abstract lines in content videos, not an instruction to make every AI clip a joke.
