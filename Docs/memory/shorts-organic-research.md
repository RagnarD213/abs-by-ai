Measured live 2026-08-25 off YouTube's own channel data: **130 Shorts across 13 men's
fitness channels**, real file durations matched to real view counts, plus 3 Instagram
accounts sampled directly (IG rate-limits hard after ~4 profile calls). This is the
ORGANIC counterpart to [[shorts-ads-research]], which covers paid.
Full brief: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/242183ec-95ed-4f16-9b50-fbdbb7552ead

**LENGTH — 45–60s, and the generic "15–30s" blog advice is wrong for this niche.**
Nine of eleven authority-lane channels have a median between 53 and 70s.
`@GravityTransformation` (the closest analog to Abs By AI — fat loss for regular men)
has **not posted a Short outside 52–60s** and does 75K–16M on them. Nippard/Ethier/
Tennyson all sit at 55s. The only creators winning under 20s are doing visual stunts
(Chris Heria, Matt Does Fitness), which is not a route a talking head can take.
Instagram agrees: Socialinsider's 2026 study of 140K Reels puts 30–60s at a 5.60%
reach rate vs 5.20% under 30s and 3.50% over two minutes.
**Spec for scripts: 165–205 spoken words** (Dan's measured 198–222 wpm ⇒ 45–58s).

⚠ **"OVER 40" IN THE TITLE COSTS 1–2 ORDERS OF MAGNITUDE OF REACH.** Shows up three
independent ways: the "how to get abs over 40" / "belly fat men over 40" searches return
196–103K views while "lose belly fat fast men" returns 1.3M–32M; `@LiveAnabolic` (every
title says "After 50/60") does 6.3–18K; `@FitFatherProject` (men 40+) does 1.4–10K.
A swipe feed serves on predicted watch time, so the demographic word pre-shrinks the test
pool. **Dan's age goes on screen and in the script as proof, never in the hook.** Jeff
Cavaliere is 50 and has never put it in a title.

**SIX TITLE SHAPES carry the breakouts:** calibration ("What 20% Body Fat Really Looks
Like", 4M — this one IS Abs By AI's product and nobody owns it), correction ("NEVER Do
Planks Like This", 9.7M), tight list of THREE ("Lose Fat Without Losing Muscle (3 tips)",
16M), self-test ("How Fit Are You REALLY?", 2.1M), versus ("Millionaire vs 6 Pack Abs",
11M), personal stakes ("How I Cured My Body Dysmorphia", 7M).

**INSTAGRAM is a different game with the same file.** Mosseri's confirmed signals: watch
time, likes/reach, **sends/reach — a DM share is worth 3–5× a like** and is the main route
to non-followers. So a Reel's last five seconds should name a person to send it to, not
ask for a subscribe. Reels reach is 9.78% under 5K followers vs 5.00% at 100K–1M, so
`@danrosefit` is in the best-paying tier it will ever be in ([[instagram-account-state]]).
Followers are not reach: `@vshred` has 1.47M and posts pulling 3.3K plays.

**TWO PLATFORM TRAPS worth remembering:**
1. A YouTube Short between **1:00 and 3:00 with ANY Content ID claim is blocked
   GLOBALLY** — music-library licences were never extended past 60s. Any Short over a
   minute needs its bed cleared before scheduling.
2. **YouTube view counts stopped meaning anything on 2026-08-24** (counted from frame 1,
   no minimum). Judge on average percentage viewed (>70%) and swipe-away (<30%).

**A RULE THAT TURNED OUT TO BE AD-ONLY:** keeping GLP-1 drug names out of copy is an
**ad-compliance** rule, not an organic one. `@RenaissancePeriodization` did 502K on
"Most Adults Should Take Tirzepatide" with no visible suppression; James Smith PT and
Kinobody name drugs routinely. Dan's Zepbound story is filmable as a Short.
