No excessively staccato writing in anything written for Dan: no runs of one to three word sentences such as "Clean. Lean. No fat.", "Same workouts. 12 weeks.", "More strength. Less body fat." Join them into full sentences.

**Why:** Dan, 2026-11-12, reviewing the L04 ad blind test (he was quoting the V Shred-style script): "one AI tell that's working its way in here is very staccato sentences ... I feel like these are a tell that this is AI-generated, and it sounds like slop. I want you to eliminate excessively staccato writing like this."

**How to apply:** all ad scripts, VSL sections and sales copy, and by my reading other outward-facing writing too. It cuts hardest against the V Shred style, which leans on fragments: keep V Shred's structure and lines of argument, drop the chop. A single short beat in his own words ("That's right. A potato.", "Same amount of protein.") is fine; the problem is a run of them. Also listed under "Never" in `/dan-copywriting`.
