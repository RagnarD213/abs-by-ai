In reaction Shorts, keep Dan's laptop visible while he watches the clip, and fill the silent panel behind his reply with footage that proves what he's saying, instead of letting the source clip run on.

**Why:** Dan on Terry Crews Fasting R1 (DS-43): "I need my laptop to be visible while I'm reacting, so it doesn't just look like I'm looking at the ground", and the sitcom footage that followed in the panel was "a little bit non sequitur". He asked for horizontal clips of Terry shirtless, jacked and flexing above him, including on the lines about his physique.

**How to apply:** The R5 watching layer crops the laptop off, so add the real lid as a still foreground layer (recipe: `.claude/skills/shorts/reference/ds43-terry-crews/README.md`). When the reply names something visual, keep the panel up and cut matching horizontal footage into it. Check every found clip's first and last frames for logos, screens and dissolves.
