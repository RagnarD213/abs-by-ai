Deep-dive done 2026-08-03, building on [[youtube-ad-competitor-research]] and [[ai-ad-creation-research]].

**THE headline: the FTC sued and a federal court temporarily HALTED MadMuscles' parent on June 17, 2026.** FTC v. Genesis Tech (N.D. Cal.): 15 corporations + 8 individuals (founders Volodymyr Mnoholetnyi & Vasyl Ulyanov), Cyprus/Delaware shells (AmoApps Ltd = MadMuscles/Harna/Unimeal). Accusations: hidden auto-renew terms, unauthorized/double charges, deliberately obstructed cancellation (FTC Act + ROSCA), shell-company rotation to evade fraud monitoring. ~$250M revenue from 5 named products early-2023→mid-2025; ~$700M through connected PayPal accounts in the 12 months to Sept 2025. Genesis overall: $1B+ valuation, 25+ companies, 1B+ downloads. Despite the June halt, our own VidTao check on 2026-07-27 showed MadMuscles ads still running at massive scale, and madmuscles.com operates normally as of 2026-08-03.

**Business model (why the FTC came):** web-to-app quiz funnel — 48-screen gender-split quiz → tiered paywall billed ON THE WEB (bypasses Apple/Google billing rules AND 30% fee): $6.93 1-wk trial → renews $39.99/mo; $15.19 4-wk; $25.99 12-wk (pre-selected default) → $69.99/3-mo renewal. App-store IAP is only ~$5M lifetime — essentially ALL revenue is web payments. The real product is the renewal trap; the app (template workouts + meal plans) is commodity.

**Funnel/ad machine numbers (web2appworld, Feb 2026):** ~$29.4M/mo YouTube ($195.6M trailing 12mo; VidTao's $487M/yr estimate is likely overstated — modeled from impressions), 2,335 FB ads / 3,498 YouTube ads / 3,240 display ads live at once, 493 quiz landing pages, funnel iteration at v490, 17.4M site visits Jan 2026 (+116% MoM). ~18,000 AI-generated ads total. Top creative (podcast-style tai chi story) $14.2M lifetime. Discipline: kill creatives at ~$5k, scale winners to $200k+/30d, 5–10 versioned variants per script, theme rotation (tai chi 54%→22% share as military rose) to beat fatigue. Ad-theme→funnel-theme congruence (tai chi ad lands on /funnel/taichi-v490/).

**Their AI is all in MARKETING, not product** — synthetic characters + AI VO story ads targeting men 50+/women 40+ with fantasy authority figures (tai chi master, drill sergeant). Product personalization is quiz-branching templates. Abs By AI is the inverse: AI IS the product (the transformation image).

**Strategic conclusions for Abs By AI:** copy the machine (quiz-funnel depth, ad→landing congruence, versioned creative iteration with kill/scale rules, gender-split everything, theme rotation, web-first billing) but NOT the dark patterns — our compliance-first posture (disclosure above Subscribe, easy cancel, honest AI labeling, real support email) is the moat in a post-FTC category. Nobody else matches their AI-ad scale (Fitme $48M/yr, Muscle Booster $35M/yr, V Shred testing AI hooks). Collaboration is off the table while they're under federal injunction. Opportunity: their displaced/burned users ("MadMuscles alternative/refund" search demand) and the ad-inventory vacuum if the injunction sticks.
