All marketing, funnel, landing-page, offer and ad advice must be grounded in proven direct-response tactics that the top fitness players (V Shred, MadMuscles, BetterMe, Noom) actually run. If the leaders are not doing it, do not recommend it, and do not steelman it.

**Why:** Dan judged the "upload your photo, get a free AI abs generation" front-end funnel a failure on 2026-09-24. He believes the speculative, invented approach is why it failed, and that Claude gave bad advice by arguing it "had legs" instead of pointing to what the leaders do.

**How to apply:**
- Before recommending a funnel or tactic, name which proven player uses it (see [[madmuscles-deep-dive]], [[youtube-ad-competitor-research]]); if you can't, say so and don't recommend it.
- Default formats: VSL, quiz funnel, personalized plan preview, annual-plan-forward offers.
- A sales video is long. Never recommend a 60 to 90 second video to do a VSL's job; the proven funnels run 20 to 40 minutes (Dan, 2026-08-24 and 2026-09-02).
- When Dan asks to steelman something unproven, give the case honestly but lead with "the leaders don't do this" as the deciding fact.
- Judge ads by cost per trial and cost per paying customer, never by a mid-funnel action like generations.
- The AI generation stays a member feature, not the front-end hook. Ad and script copy written before 2026-09-24 that sells "upload your photo free" as the entry point is out of date.
