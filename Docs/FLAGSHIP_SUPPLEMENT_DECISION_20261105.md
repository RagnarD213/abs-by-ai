## Keyword searches, sorted by last-30-day spend

- **"testosterone":** Male Excel ($44K, $33K, $8K, $7K active ads), Prime Labs Prime Test Pro $7K/30d, hims $3.5M lifetime. Men 40+ low-T is an actively bought audience; mostly clinics and Rx, few supplement sellers at scale on YouTube right now (opening).
- **"GLP-1":** dominated by prescription/telehealth: GoodRx three ads at $771K, $520K, $501K per 30d; LifeRx, TrimRx, Noom, Juniper. Supplements riding the term: Grüns ($82K, $42K), Bioma. The GLP-1 term is where Rx drugs spend; a supplement competes directly against the real drug.
- **"fat burner" / "belly fat" / "burn fat while you sleep":** no US fat-burner supplement ad above ~$20K/30d. Spend is meal kits (Factor), workout programs (Meredith Shirk), teas.
- **"berberine":** Thorne, BiOptimizers, European berberine brands, all under $31K/30d. Small on US YouTube.
- **"tongkat ali":** only Indonesian / shilajit sellers. No US tongkat brand at scale (no one owns it).

## Why Great Again T

- **Proven by the closest model.** V Shred, the fitness-for-men YouTube advertiser Dan most resembles, put more ad money behind its testosterone booster than any other supplement, about 3x its fat burner, and is still running it.
- **Exact fit with Dan's buyer and story.** Men 30 to 60, fat loss first. The VSL bridge is native: "why belly fat stops moving after 40" -> testosterone -> what Dan (41, abs) does. V Shred's running angle is "testosterone-killing foods" via quiz.
- **Highest perceived value and price ceiling.** Sculpt Nation Test Boost Max sells at $49; male vitality supports $49-69 and multi-bottle bundles better than a fat burner.
- **Uncrowded in supplements on YouTube now.** Current low-T ad money is clinics (Male Excel, hims). A natural, non-prescription option for men who don't want TRT is a clear position.
- **Compliance is manageable on the evidence.** V Shred ran Test Boost Max on Google CPC at multi-million scale. Keep claims to "supports healthy testosterone, energy and drive"; no sexual-performance, ED or steroid comparisons (Google restricts products claimed comparable to steroids or prescription ED drugs).

## Why the burner stack is runner-up

- Matches the Abs By AI fat-loss promise directly, and is the classic V Shred / Sculpt Nation pairing (Burn Evolved + Burn PM).
- Weaker evidence: about $1.9M lifetime at V Shred, and no US fat-burner supplement is spending big on YouTube right now. More commodity, more saturated, lower price ceiling.
- Best use: upsell/bundle on the Great Again T funnel ("T + Burn" stack), and the backup flagship if the T VSL fails.

## Why not GLP-1 Support as flagship

- The search term is owned by prescription drugs (GoodRx alone ~$1.8M/30d). The supplement gets compared to Ozempic, which the formula (digestive enzymes, probiotic, B vitamins, 18 mg iron) cannot match; expect refunds.
- Google restricts products claimed comparable to prescription weight-loss medications, which is the whole angle.
- The money that works in this space sells gut health (Gundry Total Restore $937K/30d, Grüns), mostly to women 45+, not Dan's audience.
