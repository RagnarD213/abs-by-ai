# Great Again T upsell scripts: fact check of the study lines (2026-11-11)

Scope: the eight blind scripts in Google Doc `17EPYViJQEcawLgUNXOgbV8zZkfJ6rVxrvIzLF430eFE` (four upsells, Script 1 and Script 2 each). This pass checks every study, number and sourced fact against the paper itself. It flags only statements that are false or have no source, and it does not soften the copy. It does not say which script is which style (the blind key stays local).

## Verified as written

| Line in the scripts | Source checked | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Tongkat ali trial: testosterone tested at weeks 4, 8 and 12, strongest at week 12 | Chinnappan et al. 2021, Food & Nutrition Research, full text table | True. 200 mg group total testosterone: 200.5 at the start, 215.4 at week 4, 218.9 at week 8, 225.0 at week 12 (ng/dL). Also measured at week 2. |
| "The longer you stay on it, the better it works" | Same table | Supported for the 12 weeks studied: both dose groups rose at every visit. |
| Short sleepers on the same diet lost 55% less fat (University of Chicago) | Nedeltcheva et al. 2010, Annals of Internal Medicine | True. 5.5 versus 8.5 hours in bed, same calories; fat was 55% less of the weight lost. Small study (10 adults). |
| One week of 5-hour nights cut young men's testosterone 10 to 15%; most testosterone is made during sleep | Leproult and Van Cauter 2011, JAMA | True. 10 healthy young men. |
| Oklahoma football players on creatine for 9 weeks: bench up 5.2%, squat up 8.7%, peak power up 19.6% | Bemben et al. 2001, Med Sci Sports Exerc, full text | True. Those are the creatine group's gains. 25 players in the study, 9 of them took creatine. |
| Adults over 50 who took creatine while lifting gained about 3 pounds more muscle (22 trials) | Chilibeck et al. 2017, Open Access J Sports Med | True. 1.37 kg more lean mass, 22 trials, 721 people, average ages 57 to 70. |
| Adults over 50 on creatine lost more body fat (19 trials) | Forbes et al. 2019, J Funct Morphol Kinesiol | True for body fat percentage (0.55 points more, p = 0.04). Pounds of fat lost were about 1 lb more but not statistically significant. Keep the wording "more body fat"; do not add a pounds figure. |
| More than 250 supplements reviewed, creatine first for lean muscle | Nissen and Sharp 2003, J Appl Physiol | True. Creatine 0.36% lean mass per week, HMB 0.28%; the others showed no significant gain. |
| Over two pounds of steak a day to get a 5 gram dose of creatine | Beef holds about 2 g creatine per pound raw | True (about 2.5 lb). |
| Garcinia standardized to 50% HCA; 5 g creatine per scoop; 400 mg tongkat ali per serving | Supplier label templates | True. |
| Prepaid pack math: 6 for $199 is $33 a bottle, 66% off $97, less than half of six monthly bottles at $67 | Arithmetic | True. |

## Notes for later

- Megadosing outlines (on hold): the same trial showed muscle strength rising in BOTH dose groups at week 12, so "only the double-dose men got stronger" would be wrong. "Testosterone up at week 4 on the double dose, week 12 on the single dose" (versus placebo) is right.
- Not checked here because they are Dan's business facts, not studies: the 8 to 12 week restock time and the stock-out lines.
- "Studies show the last three or four reps are the ones that build muscle" (Upsell 3, Script 2) is Vince's line. No single study says it in those words; research on training close to failure supports the idea. Left as written.
