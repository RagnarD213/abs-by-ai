# Voice bench: setup `set12`, 2026-10-10

set5 with 12 real passages instead of 5 (about 3,500 words of him). Still no guide and no rules.

Writer: claude-opus-5-5. Judges: a fresh claude-opus-5-5 and gemini-3.1-pro-preview, each shown 8 real reference passages of the same type and one real / generated pair in random order. A judge is "right" when it picks the AI piece. Chance is 50%. The finish line is 60% or less.

**Judges picked the AI piece 64 times out of 80: 80%** (95% range 70 to 87%).

In the judges' words (most confident right calls, one per cue):

- **too clean grammar** (content, gemini): Passage A is too grammatically perfect and structured for off-the-cuff speech, lacking the conversational looseness and slight run-on tendencies seen in the reference texts. Passage B captures the speaker's natural rhythm, including minor grammatical fragments and casual phrasing like 'throw that inside' and 'makes the salad a little more salty'.
- **over explaining** (content, gemini): Passage B over-explains simple concepts, adding 'with acne all the time' to explain breaking out and awkwardly describing a double chin as a 'chin with the fold under it'. It also cleans up the grammar and fixes a natural human typo ('look at a lot better') present in Passage A.
- **performs the voice** (conversion, gemini): Passage B chops the text into overly short, punchy sentences and adds extra conversational filler to mimic a sales tone, whereas the real author writes slightly longer, more straightforward sentences.
- **structure too organized** (conversion, gemini): The AI over-corrects the lists for perfect parallelism by adding the word 'team' to every single item, and it replaces natural phrasing like 'getting rich' with the slightly more cliché 'making fortunes.'
- **punctuation formatting** (conversion, gemini): Passage B removes the author's characteristic trailing ellipses and all-caps emphasis on words like blown away. It also replaces his conversational flow with overly tidy transitions like first of all and second.
- **safe generic content** (products, gemini): Passage A sanitizes the author's typical pickup-artist vocabulary, changing phrases like 'get laid' to 'sleep with any of them'. It also uses very rigid, simplistic sentence structures and generic transitions like 'However,' which lack the conversational, slightly edgy flow of the original text.
- **sentence rhythm** (content, claude): A is cut into neat short punchy sentences, with a staged rhetorical question and a tidy three-beat run (simple / 20 seconds / anywhere). B has the looser, redundant spoken phrasing of the references, with restarts like 'see yourself, what you would look like', the site name repeated, and demonstratives such as 'those ab exercises' and 'that next set'.
