# Voice bench: setup `set5`, 2026-10-16

Step B2 and B3 alone: no guide and no rules. The writer sees 5 whole real passages from the sample bank, chosen by format and length (never by topic, never from the same source as the test passage), and writes the next piece in the set.

Writer: claude-opus-5-5. Judges: a fresh claude-opus-5-5 and gemini-3.1-pro-preview, each shown 8 real reference passages of the same type and one real / generated pair in random order. A judge is "right" when it picks the AI piece. Chance is 50%. The finish line is 60% or less.

**Judges picked the AI piece 72 times out of 80: 90%** (95% range 81 to 95%).

In the judges' words (most confident right calls, one per cue):

- **tidy ending** (ads, gemini): Passage B adds a neat, summarizing conclusion ('So even if you're totally new to this...') that is typical of AI-generated text. It also uses overly structured transition words ('First', 'Then', 'After that', 'Next', 'And finally') compared to the author's more natural, repetitive phrasing in Passage A ('I'll teach you', 'I'll also show you', 'Then, I'll show you', 'Next, I'll show you').
- **sentence rhythm** (ads, gemini): Passage B over-corrects the short sentence structure, making it feel unnaturally choppy and robotic. It also awkwardly paraphrases standard self-defense disclaimers into weird phrases like 'afraid you're going to die' instead of the natural 'fear for your life' seen in A.
- **punctuation formatting** (ads, gemini): Passage A removes the author's signature ellipses and conversational paragraph breaks, replacing them with standard commas and periods. It also loses the exact repetitive phrasing and formatting seen in the references.
- **too clean grammar** (content, gemini): Passage A smooths out the natural, slightly clunky spoken phrasing into perfectly grammatical sentences. It replaces conversational run-ons with tidy, textbook-like structures that lack the spontaneous rhythm of the speaker.
- **structure too organized** (content, gemini): Passage A is too neatly structured, with perfectly balanced transitions ('Now, during that...', 'Now, when is...', 'So here's the main takeaway...') and a very tidy, summarized conclusion. Passage B sounds much more like the reference transcripts, with slightly looser phrasing ('All right guys, so the big takeaway...', 'Alright guys, that's it for today's video') and the exact phrasing of the AbsByAI plug seen in Reference 1.
- **vocabulary** (content, gemini): Passage B uses unnatural synonyms like 'second chin' instead of 'double chin' and 'upgrades your face' to avoid copying the original text. It also fixes the human typo 'look at a lot better' found in Passage A.
- **performs the voice** (content, gemini): Passage B tries a bit too hard to sound conversational by adding 'okay, Dan' and 'like real abs that I can see in the mirror', which feels like an AI performing the persona. Passage A matches the straightforward, slightly repetitive, and less polished rhythm of the reference transcripts.
- **over explaining** (products, claude): Passage B smooths out the false starts that A keeps (like the self-correcting 'you've got you've done'). It also adds emphatic wrap-up lines ('really the whole point of it', 'really, really important') and restates the right-away point twice, which reads as performed clarity rather than live speech.
