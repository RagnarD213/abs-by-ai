# Voice bench: setup `pilot`, 2026-10-16

Phase C pilot (2026-10-16): Llama 3.1 8B Instruct (4-bit, MLX) with a LoRA trained on Dan's tier 1 text only (no file that holds a held-out passage), 589 training chunks (of 726 cut; Haiku marked the rest unusable, mostly explicit sex instruction) each paired with a full-strength brief. It writes from the bench's brief directly, with no passages and no rules in its prompt. Drafts come from scripts/voice/pilot_write.py.

Writer: the pilot (Llama 3.1 8B with Dan's LoRA, step-600 checkpoint, temperature 0.8). **Judge: Gemini only** (a cheap first read, 2026-10-16; the Opus judge was not run because the session was over its budget, and the 4 pairs Gemini refuses got no verdict). The judge is shown 8 real reference passages of the same type and one real / generated pair in random order. A judge is "right" when it picks the AI piece. Chance is 50%. The finish line is 60% or less.

**Judges picked the AI piece 32 times out of 36: 89%** (95% range 75 to 96%).

In the judges' words (most confident right calls, one per cue):

- **other** (conversion, gemini): Passage B suffers from a severe AI generation loop, repeating the exact same sentence dozens of times. Meanwhile, Passage A accurately reflects the author's specific background and natural sales structure.
- **structure too organized** (ads, gemini): Passage A uses overly structured transitions like 'Okay, so let's walk through' and 'Step one... Step two,' which feels like an AI organizing its thoughts. It also combines sentences into longer, grammatically dense paragraphs, whereas the real author (Passage B) uses short, punchy, single-sentence paragraphs with casual phrasing and occasional missing punctuation.
- **sentence rhythm** (ads, gemini): Passage A uses overly smooth transitions and dense, multi-clause sentences ('Abs By AI will calculate the perfect way...'), whereas Passage B uses the author's signature short, punchy paragraphs and simpler sentence structures ('It's easy to make a picture...'). Passage A also lumps the text into blocky paragraphs, missing the author's typical line-break formatting.
- **punctuation formatting** (ads, gemini): Passage A groups sentences into standard, chunky paragraphs instead of using Rose's signature single-sentence lines. It also completely misses his frequent use of dramatic ellipses and all-caps for emphasis.
- **too clean grammar** (content, gemini): Passage B cleans up the natural run-on sentences and removes demonstrative speech like pointing out a motion. It replaces spoken, off-the-cuff phrasing with tidy, grammatically correct descriptions.
- **facts or specifics** (content, gemini): Passage B strips out the specific real-world example of Organifi and its green juice, replacing it with a safe, generic summary about businesses growing big. It also awkwardly overuses the word 'Now' to artificially fake a conversational speaking rhythm.
- **tidy ending** (conversion, gemini): Passage B ends with an unnatural, polite sign-off that makes no sense for a direct response sales letter. It also condenses the pitch into denser, more traditional paragraphs rather than using the author's signature punchy, one-sentence paragraph style.
- **vocabulary** (conversion, gemini): Passage B uses slightly unnatural phrasing like 'telephone sales teams' and 'monetize it' instead of the simpler, more direct language found in Passage A and the references. It also feels like it's trying too hard to sound conversational ('Hello, I'm Dan Rose', 'Back in the day'), which comes across as a forced imitation of the author's voice.
