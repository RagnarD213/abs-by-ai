Today's guide as a writing skill would load it: DAN-VOICE.md, every file in _shared/voice/ (the Part 1 examples, the P2A example files and the swearing rules), WRITING-RULES.md and the personal facts entry.

Writer: claude-opus-5-5. Judges: a fresh claude-opus-5-5 and gemini-3.1-pro-preview, each shown 8 real reference passages of the same type and one real / generated pair in random order. A judge is "right" when it picks the AI piece. Chance is 50%. The finish line is 60% or less.

In the judges' words (most confident right calls, one per cue):

- **vocabulary** (conversion, gemini): Passage B replaces the author's specific marketing jargon like 'higher ticket' with the generic 'more expensive' and removes the characteristic all-caps emphasis on 'BLOWN AWAY'. It also awkwardly changes 'audiobook' to 'audio version'.
- **structure too organized** (products, gemini): Passage B breaks the text into overly neat, short paragraphs and replaces the author's conversational, slightly clunky phrasing with sterile, formal equivalents. It removes natural repetition and smooths out the sentence rhythm to sound more like a textbook.
- **too clean grammar** (products, claude): B reads like a smoothed paraphrase: it lowercases his capitalized key terms Dominance and Emotion, swaps his blunt wording ('laying her', 'garbage') for polite neutral phrasing, and tidies everything into even, correct sentences with a semicolon. A keeps his quirks, such as 'over night', the all-caps headings, and the abrupt imperative 'Be a man, it says'.
- **performs the voice** (ads, gemini): Passage B over-performs the copywriter voice with perfectly spaced single-sentence paragraphs, numbered lists, and capitalized emphasis. Passage A contains clunky repetition and a dangling colon that an AI would likely smooth out.
- **sentence rhythm** (content, gemini): Passage B smooths out the repetitive, slightly clunky phrasing found in Passage A (like 'It improves your... It improves your... It improves your...') into a more polished, conversational flow ('It's going to help... it's going to help...'). Passage A matches the speaker's habit of repeating exact phrases and slightly awkward, unedited sentence structures seen in the reference texts.
- **punctuation formatting** (content, gemini): Passage B replaces the author's characteristic dashes with commas and cleans up the slightly awkward, conversational phrasing found in the original. It also changes 'And finally' to 'Last,' missing the exact transitional rhythm the author naturally uses.
