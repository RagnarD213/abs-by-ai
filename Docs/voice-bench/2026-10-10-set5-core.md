# Voice bench: setup `set5-core`, 2026-10-10

set5 plus the short core notes (voice/CORE.md) and the notes for the type (voice/type-<type>.md), placed after the real passages.

Writer: claude-opus-5-5. Judges: a fresh claude-opus-5-5 and gemini-3.1-pro-preview, each shown 8 real reference passages of the same type and one real / generated pair in random order. A judge is "right" when it picks the AI piece. Chance is 50%. The finish line is 60% or less.

**Judges picked the AI piece 74 times out of 80: 92%** (95% range 85 to 97%).

In the judges' words (most confident right calls, one per cue):

- **structure too organized** (ads, gemini): Passage B includes a literal placeholder 'CTA TEXT' which is a classic AI generation artifact. It also uses overly repetitive, perfectly balanced sentence structures and corporate phrasing like 'Our AI' instead of the author's more casual, punchy style.
- **too clean grammar** (content, gemini): Passage B removes the natural conversational repetitions and slightly awkward phrasing found in A, replacing them with overly tidy, structured sentences that lack the spontaneous rhythm of off-the-cuff speech.
- **safe generic content** (content, gemini): Passage B opens with 'Hey guys, today I'm going to explain,' which is a generic YouTuber cliché that the real author avoids in the references. It also smooths out the phrasing into standard, predictable AI sentences rather than the punchy, direct style seen in Passage A.
- **vocabulary** (ads, gemini): Passage A uses rigid list formatting like 'Tip number one:' and replaces the author's specific phrasing like 'nutrition plan' and 'when life gets in the way' with generic alternatives like 'meal plan' and 'daily life'.
- **sentence rhythm** (ads, gemini): Passage B flattens the rhythm by removing the author's characteristic ellipses and conversational transitions (like 'And here's the best part...'). It also breaks up the list of things you don't need into repetitive, overly tidy sentences rather than the author's typical flowing, comma-separated list.
- **performs the voice** (conversion, gemini): Passage B over-performs the conversational tone by padding sentences with fillers and explicitly stating the author's name, a classic AI persona tell. It also relies on highly rigid, repetitive parallel structures, such as repeating the word winning three times.
- **punctuation formatting** (conversion, gemini): Passage B capitalizes every word in the subheadings and uses slightly stiffer, more formal phrasing ('The first reason is that...', 'The second reason is that...'), whereas Passage A uses Dan's typical conversational rhythm, ellipses, and formatting ('First, now that...', 'And secondly...').
- **over explaining** (products, claude): B reads like a flattened paraphrase: it avoids contractions, softens the crude wording ("sleep with" where A says "get laid"), and adds an explanatory clause about socializing not coming naturally. A keeps the loose, idiomatic touches of the real author, such as "100% better", "Needless to say" and "I knew this was it".
