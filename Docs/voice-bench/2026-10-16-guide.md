In the judges' words (most confident right calls, one per cue):

- **too clean grammar** (content, gemini): Passage B smooths out the natural, slightly disjointed phrasing of spoken language into perfectly structured, complete sentences. Passage A contains the conversational fragments, abrupt transitions, and slightly repetitive phrasing typical of the speaker's unscripted style.
- **vocabulary** (products, gemini): Passage B smooths out the directness of the original into slightly more generic, polished phrasing like 'initial pickup' and 'deeply emotionally invested,' while clumsily over-explaining the rocks and gold metaphor.
- **structure too organized** (ads, gemini): Passage B uses overly structured transition words like 'First,' 'Next,' 'After that,' and 'And finally,' which is a common AI pattern for organizing lists. Passage A uses a more repetitive, natural sentence structure ('I'll teach you,' 'I'll also show you,' 'Then, I'll show you') that matches the repetitive, slightly less polished rhythm of the reference texts.
- **sentence rhythm** (content, gemini): Passage B is too polished and repetitive in a structured way, lacking the natural, slightly disjointed flow of spoken language found in Passage A. Passage A includes typical conversational phrasing like 'I wasn't very educated about this' and 'less appetite than I was intended', which feels more authentic to an off-the-cuff transcript.
- **punctuation formatting** (products, gemini): Passage B smooths out the author's idiosyncratic double-dash punctuation into standard periods and commas. It also ends with a repetitive, overly tidy summary typical of AI paraphrasing.
- **over explaining** (products, claude): Passage B smooths A's false starts and run-ons into tidy sentences. It also adds explanatory padding like the line about really trusting the numbers and a summarizing 'whole point' line, while A keeps the restarts, odd phrasing and abrupt cutoffs typical of Rose's spoken transcripts.
- **performs the voice** (content, claude): B scatters casual markers like 'you guys' and 'All right guys' over smoothly built sentences, and adds a contrived 'real abs on my real body' flourish. A has the unpolished run-on stacking ('if you're... if you also want... and you want') and the blunt repetition typical of his transcripts.
