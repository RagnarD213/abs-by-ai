How It Works

Abs By AI

Abs by AI turns one photo into a personalized visualization and a plan to help you get there. Here's exactly what happens.

1

Upload a photo

A front-facing, well-lit photo of yourself works best. It's sent to our server over an encrypted connection.

2

Choose your goal and intensity

Pick how dramatic a change you want to visualize — Subtle or Ripped.

3

Our AI generates a visualization

We send your photo and goal to AI image models (Google Gemini and, for some requests, models hosted on Replicate) which generate a new image — a visualization of what a leaner, more defined version of you at your goal might look like, keeping your face, pose, and setting.

4

See your result

You get a before/after comparison you can save, share, or print. This image is an AI-generated visualization, not a photograph or a guarantee — see our Disclaimer.

5

Close the gap with AI coaching

Members get a training program built around their equipment and experience level, AI meal & macro tracking from a photo of your food, a sleep coach, and supplement audits — all personalized and updated as you make progress.

Every "after" image on Abs by AI is generated by an AI model based on your photo and your stated goal. It is not a prediction and not a guarantee of your actual results. Full details in our Disclaimer.
