About Abs by AI

Abs By AI

Abs by AI is an AI fitness visualization and coaching product: upload a photo, tell us your goal, and see an AI-generated visualization of a possible future version of yourself. From there, members get an AI-personalized training program, meal and macro tracking, a sleep coach, and supplement audits — all built to help you actually close the gap between today and that visualization.

Abs by AI is built and operated by Abs By AI, a small independent software business based in Georgetown, Texas.

What we believe

Seeing a concrete, personalized picture of a goal is motivating in a way a generic before/after stock photo never is. We built Abs by AI to make that picture accessible in under a minute, and then to back it with the same daily tools (training, nutrition, sleep, supplements) a real coach would use to help you get there.

The technology

We use commercial AI models from Google (Gemini), Anthropic (Claude), and image models hosted on Replicate to generate visualizations and power our coaching tools. Every AI-generated "after" image is clearly labeled as such — see our Disclaimer.
