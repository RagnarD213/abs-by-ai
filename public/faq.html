Frequently Asked Questions

Abs By AI

What do I actually get?

Upload a photo and Abs by AI generates an AI visualization of a possible future version of you at your chosen intensity. Members also unlock a full AI-personalized training program, meal & macro tracking, a sleep coach, and supplement audits.

Is the "after" photo real?

No. It's an AI-generated visualization, not a photograph and not a guarantee of results. See our Disclaimer for the full explanation.

Is my photo private?

Yes. Photos are sent over an encrypted connection to generate your result. If you don't create an account, we don't keep a photo library. If you do create an account, your photos are stored so your gallery works, and you can permanently delete everything at any time. Full details in our Privacy Policy.

What does it cost?

You can try it with free generations, or buy credits one-time (from $4.99). For unlimited access to every feature — image generations, the AI trainer, meal tracking, sleep coaching, and supplement audits — membership is $19.99/month or $69.99/year, and every new member gets a 7-day free trial. See Terms for full billing details.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel any time from the Manage Subscription card in your Member Hub, or email support@absbyai.com. Charged and changed your mind? Full refund within 7 days, no questions asked — see our Refund Policy.

When do I get billed?

On the same day each month (or year) as your original signup, once your free trial ends. Your next charge date is always shown in your Manage Subscription settings.

Is this medical or fitness advice?

No. Abs by AI's tools are AI-generated informational content, not a substitute for a doctor, dietitian, or trainer. Talk to your physician before changing your diet, training, or supplements. See our Disclaimer.

Can I use my own photos and delete them later?

Yes. Upload any photo of yourself (18+). You can permanently delete your account and all associated photos and data at any time from Member Hub → Account → Delete my account.

How do I get printed?

From your result screen, choose Print — posters and canvas prints are fulfilled by our print partner and shipped directly to you, billed as a one-time purchase at checkout.

Still have a question?

Email support@absbyai.com — see our Contact page.
