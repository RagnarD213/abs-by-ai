Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Abs by AI ("we", "our") provides an AI-powered fitness visualization and coaching service at absbyai.com and through the Abs by AI iOS and Android apps. This policy explains what data we handle and how.

Photos you upload

  • Photos are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our server, which forwards them to AI providers for review and image generation: Anthropic (Claude) for photo review/verification, Google (Gemini) and, for some generations, Replicate-hosted models including BytePlus Seedream, for image generation.
  • Photos are treated as biometric/appearance data for purposes of this policy — they depict your body and are used to generate a personalized visualization.
  • If you create an account, your uploaded photos and the transformation images we generate for you are stored so they appear in your Transformations gallery. If you provide your email to receive your result, your before/after pair is stored with that email. Otherwise, photos are processed in memory to generate your result and are not retained by us afterward.
  • You can permanently delete your stored photos and account data at any time — see "Your choices" below.
  • In the app, we tell you what will be sent and to whom, and ask your permission, before your photo or personal details are first shared with an AI service.
  • AI providers process images under their own terms: Anthropic Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy, and Replicate Privacy Policy. They process your photos solely to provide this service to us, and their privacy commitments provide equal or greater protection to those described in this policy.

Face data

Some photos you upload may include your face. This section explains exactly how face data is collected, used, shared, stored, retained, and deleted.

  • What we collect: the only face data we handle is the photographs you choose to upload (your before photo, and any progress photos), which may include your face, and the AI-generated images we create from them. We do not use facial recognition, and we do not extract, compute, or store faceprints, facial geometry, or any other biometric identifiers or templates.
  • How we use it: your photo is used for one purpose only — to check that the photo is usable and to generate the fitness visualization image you requested. Face data is never used for advertising, profiling, identification, or training AI models.
  • Who it is shared with: to create your image, the photo is transmitted over an encrypted connection to our AI service providers — Anthropic (photo review), Google (image generation), and, for some generations, Replicate running the ByteDance Seedream model (image generation). They process the photo solely to provide this service to us. Face data is never sold and never shared with data brokers or advertisers.
  • Where it is stored: if you have an account, your uploaded photos and generated images are stored in our database, hosted on servers in the United States, so they can appear in your Transformations gallery. If you provide your email address to receive your result, your before/after image pair is stored with that email so we can send it to you. Otherwise, photos are held temporarily in server memory only long enough to complete your generation (up to about one hour) and are then discarded — we keep no copy.
  • How long it is retained: stored photos and generated images are retained until you delete them or delete your account, at which point they are permanently deleted. If we hold images only against your email address, you can have them deleted at any time by contacting support@absbyai.com. Without an account or email submission, no photo is retained.
  • Deletion: Member Hub → Account → Delete my account permanently deletes every stored photo and generated image, immediately.

Fitness details you enter (AI coaching features)

Features like the AI Trainer, AI Nutritionist, Macro Tracker, Sleep Coach, Supplement Audit and Daily Brief work by sending the details you type or photograph — age range, height, weight, goals, equipment, meal photos, sleep times, supplement labels, and any medication names you choose to share — over an encrypted connection to Anthropic (Claude), which generates your plan or analysis. These details are used only to produce the coaching you asked for. They are not sold, and Anthropic processes them solely to provide this service to us. In the app, we ask your permission before your data is first shared with an AI service. If you have an account, your plans, logs, and check-ins are stored so the product works across sessions; deleting your account permanently deletes all of it.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card details.

Analytics

We use PostHog to collect usage analytics (pages visited, buttons clicked, approximate region, device type) so we can improve the product. We do not sell your data.

Age requirement

Abs by AI is intended for adults. You must be 18 or older to use the service. Photos that appear to depict minors are automatically rejected.

Data retention

If you create an account, we retain your account data, uploaded photos, generated images, and feature history (training programs, meal logs, sleep check-ins, supplement audits) for as long as your account is active, so the product can work across sessions. If you don't create an account, we keep no photo library and no personal profile — server logs (IP address, request time) are retained briefly for security and rate limiting, then discarded.

Your choices

You can permanently delete your account and everything associated with it — photos, generated images, and feature history — at any time from Member Hub → Account → Delete my account. This is a real, immediate deletion, not a deactivation. For any other privacy question or request, contact us and we'll respond promptly.
