Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Abs by AI ("we", "our") provides an AI-powered fitness visualization and coaching service at absbyai.com and through the Abs by AI iOS and Android apps. This policy explains what data we handle and how.

Photos you upload

  • Photos are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our server, which forwards them to AI providers for review and image generation: Anthropic (Claude) for photo review/verification, Google (Gemini) and, for some generations, Replicate-hosted models including BytePlus Seedream, for image generation.
  • Photos are treated as biometric/appearance data for purposes of this policy — they depict your body and are used to generate a personalized visualization.
  • If you create an account, your uploaded photos and the transformation images we generate for you are stored so they appear in your Transformations gallery. If you don't create an account, photos are processed in memory to generate your result and are not retained by us afterward.
  • You can permanently delete your stored photos and account data at any time — see "Your choices" below.
  • AI providers process images under their own terms: Anthropic Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy, and Replicate Privacy Policy.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card details.

Analytics

We use PostHog to collect usage analytics (pages visited, buttons clicked, approximate region, device type) so we can improve the product. We do not sell your data.

Age requirement

Abs by AI is intended for adults. You must be 18 or older to use the service. Photos that appear to depict minors are automatically rejected.

Data retention

If you create an account, we retain your account data, uploaded photos, generated images, and feature history (training programs, meal logs, sleep check-ins, supplement audits) for as long as your account is active, so the product can work across sessions. If you don't create an account, we keep no photo library and no personal profile — server logs (IP address, request time) are retained briefly for security and rate limiting, then discarded.

Your choices

You can permanently delete your account and everything associated with it — photos, generated images, and feature history — at any time from Member Hub → Account → Delete my account. This is a real, immediate deletion, not a deactivation. For any other privacy question or request, contact us and we'll respond promptly.
