Questions

Is the image real?

No. It is an AI-generated visualization of the goal you choose, made from your photo. It is not a photograph of you, not a prediction, and not a guarantee of any result. Full details in our Disclaimer.

What photo works best?

Shirtless (men) or a sports bra or swimsuit (women), a neutral standing pose, even lighting, phone at chest height. Fully clothed photos can't be used — the AI edits the body it can see.

Are the numbers a medical measurement?

No. Body fat, lean muscle, fat to lose and goal weight are visual estimates for motivation, based on published body-fat ranges (sources). Talk to a doctor before changing your diet or training.

What happens to my photo?

It is sent securely to our AI partners (Anthropic, Google, Replicate) to create your result. It is never sold, and you can delete it anytime. Privacy Policy.

What does the trial cost?

Nothing for 7 days. After that it's $19.99 a month or $69.99 a year, and you can cancel anytime in two taps — cancel before day 7 and you're never charged. Refund policy.
