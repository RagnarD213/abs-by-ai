Source and verification notes

How to choose after this review

Judge a later test by cost per trial, cost per paying customer, trial-to-paid conversion and retention/refunds. A prettier cart or more trials alone is not a business win.

All concepts keep monthly selected first, identical price terms and the same sample profile. The annual equivalent is $5.83/month, billed $69.99 yearly after the trial. The 71% saving compares with 12 monthly payments.

No price change, experiment, live cart replacement or billing connection has been made. Browser screenshots cover 1440 px desktop and 390 px mobile; 360 px is also checked.
