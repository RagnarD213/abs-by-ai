# Writing rules shared by every writing skill

Read this before writing or editing any words that carry Dan's name: scripts, outlines, shorts, ad copy, titles,
descriptions, articles, sales pages, emails. It holds only the rules that apply to MORE than one writing skill. A rule
that belongs to one skill lives in that skill. Where a skill and this file disagree, the newer dated line wins.

Each line: the rule, who said it and when, and why. Added 2026-10-06 from a sweep of Dan's past chat corrections, so the
rules no longer live only in old chats and local memory. A cloud session has no chat history: this file and
`Docs/memory/` are all it gets.

## 2. Who Dan is on the page

- **Never call Dan a marketer, a professional marketer or an ad agency owner.** Say AI coded his website or did his
  bookkeeping. (Dan, 2026-09-18.) Why: it makes him read as a marketer pitching, not a fitness expert. Older lines in
  skills and outlines that say "a dad running a successful ad agency" predate this and are not to be reused.
- **He is not married. He has a girlfriend. He has one child, a daughter.** Never "my wife", "my kids" or "my son" in
  his voice. A loose hook ("same business, same kids, same stress") is fine. (Dan, 2026-08-07.) Why: he killed an
  outline over this error.
- **Use his real facts and numbers only. Never invent a habit, a number, a story or a credential.** The facts on record
  are in `dan-personal-facts-for-scripts` and in each skill's own facts section. If a fact is missing, leave a marked
  gap for Dan, do not fill it. (Standing; restated by Dan 2026-09-21 when he corrected "6 am" to "8 am".)
- **Before photo: 38 years old, about 200 lb. Abs back at 40.** About two years apart. (Dan, 2026-08-07.)

## 3. Sound like Dan, not like an assistant

- **No em dash and no en dash, anywhere.** Use a comma, a colon, parentheses or two sentences. Count them before
  delivering; the count must be 0. (Dan, 2026-09-18.) Why: it is the first giveaway of AI writing.
- **Kill the AI tells.** Dan's words on a draft: "it seems like generic Claude writing", "very inoffensive", "bland",
  "never says anything controversial". Write it the way his own scripts read. His finished scripts are the reference,
  not a general idea of good writing. (Dan, 2026-08-24.)
- **Take a side and say it bluntly.** Dan's scripts are opinionated and sometimes controversial. A script that offends
  nobody reads as AI. In organic content he swears; write it where he would. (Dan, 2026-08-24.) Ad copy that Google
  reviews stays clean.
- **On dating, attraction and relationships, write in line with the looksmaxxing community and the philosophy of
  attraction of Rich Cooper, Myron Gaines and Andrew Tate.** Never the generic mainstream AI view. (Dan, 2026-08-24.)
  Why: it is the view he holds and, in his words, the one most men online hold.
- **Do not add compliance, legal or policy commentary** unless the job is shipping sales copy or Dan asks. Then apply
  the rules on record, briefly. (Dan, 2026-09-25.)

## 4. What every piece must do for the viewer

- **Plan the first 5 seconds word by word.** If the interesting thing lands at second 8, it is too late. (Dan,
  2026-08-07 and 2026-09-21.) Hook patterns: `.claude/skills/_shared/HOOKS.md`.
- **Give a reason to watch.** No brag-only piece. The viewer leaves with a tactic. (Dan, 2026-08-17.)
- **Every point must feel new and worth having.** Cut a point that is obvious ("cut ultra-processed restaurant food") or
  not tightly tied to the title. No generic listicles ("The Five Foods I Never Eat"). (Dan, 2026-07-29 and 2026-09-21.)
- **Keep it to what the viewer personally cares about.** An aside about someone else (bodybuilders' bubble gut) goes.
  (Dan, 2026-08-25.)
- **A short cut from a long-form idea gets its own hook.** Do not transcribe the long-form's points in order.
  (Dan, 2026-09-21.)
- **Proof stays brief and plain: under 90 seconds, no pile of science talk up front, but it must still prove the
  claim.** Pick the study that shows the result the viewer wants (weight lost), not a lab marker. (Dan, 2026-09-21.)

## 5. Selling

- **In sales copy, persuasion beats scientific precision.** Give the prospect a named idea he did not know (his
  "somatotype") over a restatement of what he knows ("you are 50 lb from abs"). (Dan, 2026-09-22.) This is about
  framing. It never licenses an invented fact or an unbelievable claim.
- **A sales video is long.** Never recommend or write a 60 to 90 second video to do the job of a VSL. The proven funnels
  run 20 to 40 minutes. (Dan, 2026-08-24 and 2026-09-02.)
- **Only proven direct-response structures.** If V Shred, MadMuscles, BetterMe or Noom do not run it, do not write it
  or recommend it. (Dan, 2026-09-24.)
- **The entry offer is the 7-day free trial on the /start sales page, not a free AI image.** Since 2026-09-24 the
  photo generation is a member feature shown inside the pitch, not the hook and not the call to action. Do not write
  "generate yours free" or "upload your photo free" as the ask in new copy. Sections of `/scriptwriting` and
  `/ad-outlines` written before that date still say to sell the generation first; this line replaces them.
- **State the offer exactly as the cart does.** Monthly: 7 days free, then $19.99 a month. Lifetime: 7 days free, then
  one charge of $69.99, no recurring billing. 365-day guarantee. (Dan, 2026-10-02; source of truth `Docs/WEB_CART.md`.
  Check it before writing any price.) Do not promise a reminder email before the charge. (Dan, 2026-09-30.)
- **No invented social proof.** Never "thousands of guys", never a member count. (On record since 2026-09-10.)
- **An ad says "tap the button below". Organic content says "go to AbsByAI.com" and never mentions a button.**
  (Dan, 2026-09-28.) Why: that line is how an ad is told apart from content, and an ad is never posted organically.
- **Ad headlines must make the fitness topic obvious on their own** and must still make sense next to any video or
  image they can be paired with. "Fire Your Personal Trainer" shapes are his. Headlines in Title Case.
  (Dan, 2026-09-08 and 2026-10-02.) Detail: `/ad-copy`.
- **Words Google Ads has refused or that Dan bans in ads:** "trick", "humiliating", disease names, drug names, any
  promise of a physical result. (Dan, 2026-08-07, 2026-08-11.) Organic content may name the drug.

## 6. Documents

- **Outlines keep real nested bullets** (sub-points indented under their parent), never a flat list of dashes.
  (Dan, 2026-08-14.)
- **Scripts: a blank line between paragraphs and between a filming note and the spoken words.** (Dan, 2026-09-16.)
- **The teleprompter copy matches the filming script word for word.** Change one, change the other. (Dan, 2026-09-19.)
- **When moving or reorganizing existing text, add nothing.** No new content appears in a doc Dan asked you only to
  sort. (Dan, 2026-08-05, after a session invented content while splitting an outline doc.)
- **When Dan edits a draft, diff his version against yours and write the lesson into the skill** before the next piece.
  The goal is a draft he does not need to edit. (Dan, 2026-09-21.)
