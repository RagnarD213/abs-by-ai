## Soft Blue Light: all videos

Use **Soft Blue Light**, option 1 from WV-01 round 3, for graphics across all videos going forward. The reference has a deep navy field, restrained drifting blue light, soft depth, pale readable type and purposeful entrances. Motion should be visible but quiet. This is a selected treatment, not permission to invent another Blue Glass variation.

This is the standard graphic family across all video formats. Use the moving navy field for full-screen graphic scenes and translucent blue glass for appropriate cards and overlays. ## Photo formats and choices

- A three-photo screen always contains **three vertical portraits**, with consistent frame proportions filled by portrait imagery. Never put two horizontal photographs and one vertical into these three tall frames. Do not stretch photos to fit.
- Choose real result photos where Dan looks maximally muscular and has a smile or a strong, natural expression. Reject slumping, frowning and awkward expressions. Compare full-size candidates before using them.
- Show horizontal result photographs **one after another**, following Muhammad Ad 1's moving sequence. Do not force them into a portrait triptych. Verify the actual reference's pacing and motion.
- Keep true before/result/AI identities and required disclosures. Place labels clear of face and abs. This is a selection/layout rule, not permission to alter Dan's physique or expressions.

## Credible iPhone demonstrations

Phone demos must read as a real modern iPhone: rounded outer body and display, coherent bezel, integrated Dynamic Island/sensor treatment, correct proportions and screen content clipped precisely to the rounded display. No square pasted rectangle, white corner spill, duplicate bezel or phone inside a phone. Do not draw both an obsolete full-width notch and a second Dynamic Island. Use one coherent modern device model.

Preserve approved screen content, actions and timing when the requested change is the surrounding device. An approved simulation remains internally identified as simulated; a better device frame does not establish live functionality. Inspect the whole moving demo at display size, including its corners, scrolling and touch transitions. Validate one short working prototype before applying the device treatment across the remaining demonstrations.
