## Watermark

The avatar menu has a **Visible watermarking** switch; on Dan's Pro account it was already off. Every downloaded take was
checked one frame per second, full frame and a zoom on the bottom-right corner (where Gemini-app videos put their
sparkle): **no visible watermark on any of the five**. The invisible SynthID mark is always embedded.

## What went wrong first (the learning run, before the batch)

The main session's own first clip (A1) went out with the END frame in both slots: the picker list re-sorted as the
uploads finished, so the click landed on the other file. Fixes, both in place: the runbook picks frames by exact-name
search, and `veo.py collect` rejects a take that does not open on START (it caught this one: open 7.2 from START, 2.8
from END). That learning take is kept as `A1-learn-flow-t1.mp4` and costs 20 credits.
