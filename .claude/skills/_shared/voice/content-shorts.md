## S1. A whole short Dan wrote: problem, promise, three plain steps, comment ask

Source `1yZjcG5pkbw0kPsfTvc7OOr2bX6v0bVYMqquUiRENQ4k` (Shoot 5 scripts, August 2026), "How To Jump Rope". Dan, Tier A.
Became "How to Jump Rope Without Tripping" `LTkjlBr_3tg`, the channel's top short (5k views).

> Jump rope is the best cardio you can do at home, but most people are doing it wrong.
>
> Here's how to from tripping over the rope to skipping rope like a boxer in just a few minutes.
>
> First, don't hop over the rope with two feet. Skip over the rope one foot at a time. It's less impact on your joints, and it's easier to push your cardio without tripping on the rope.
>
> Skipping over the rope one foot at a time is hard to do at full speed when you first start. So your first time, move the rope slowly and skip over it one foot at a time.
>
> Once you're good at jump roping, do it for two minutes every morning when you wake up to get your blood pumping. Or do two minutes before your workout for a quick cardio warmup.
>
> Comment below to let me know how learning to jump rope this way worked for you.

Why: hook = a superlative plus "most people are doing it wrong". Then the deliverable. Every step is an instruction with
its reason in the same breath. Ends on an ask, not a kicker. (The typo "how to from" is his; do not copy typos.)

## S2. Problem-plus-result short with his own numbers

Source `1yZjcG5pkbw0kPsfTvc7OOr2bX6v0bVYMqquUiRENQ4k`, "How To Lose The Last Ten Pounds Of Fat". Dan, Tier A.

> Here's how to lose the last ten pounds of fat before you see your abs.
>
> This was me at 190 a few months ago - barely had abs.
>
> First, take Zepbound. Losing the last ten pounds was difficult for me, and I struggled at the 190 plateau for nearly a year. Zepbound got me to 180 in a matter of a few months.
>
> If you can barely see your abs right now, losing that last ten pounds to get them fully defined is so worth it. Try those three tips, and let me know how they work for you.

Why: first line names the outcome. Second line is a picture beat with a spaced-hyphen aside. The fix is stated as a
command ("First, take Zepbound."), proven with his plateau numbers. Close = a payoff plus "let me know".

## S3. Credential in one flat line, then the honest verdict

Source `1yZjcG5pkbw0kPsfTvc7OOr2bX6v0bVYMqquUiRENQ4k`, "Does Jiu Jitsu Get You Abs?". Dan, Tier A.

> I'm a Brazilian Jiu Jitsu black belt who's been training for 22 years. And I've had a lot of people asking me if jiu jitsu is a good way to get abs.
>
> Jiu jitsu does give you a great ab workout, both with the conditioning exercises we do and with wrestling live rounds. But it might be the hardest possible way to get abs.
>
> Also, remember that nutrition is 80% of getting abs whether you do jiu jitsu or not. You can't out-train a bad diet, not even with jiu jitsu training.

Why: credential with a real number, no brag words around it. Concede the good part, then the blunt turn with "But".
