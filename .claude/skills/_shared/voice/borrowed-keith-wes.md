# Borrowed techniques: Keith and Wes (NOT Dan's voice)

Tier K. Keith and Wes are outside copywriters whose HBI ads made the client's all-time top 10 next to Dan's. Dan rates
them highly. **Learn their techniques; never quote them or describe them as Dan's voice.** Excerpts are 150 words or
less (client scripts). Spend and CPA from the "Top 10 All Time HBI Ad Creatives" sheet
(`1RtcUMgsJAdOHu5VKKaMOhhVXNvGi8DvfZ8XU3CCrKtw`). Collected 2026-10-06 (Dan Voice Training P1).

Weighting: Dan's own SRM ads hold the two best CPAs ($48.14 and $48.70) and the biggest spend ($5.96M). Keith wins
with very short, emotionally simple scripts (138 to 224 words). Keith's longest, most dramatic script has the worst CPA.
Totals: SRM rows about $9.4M, Keith/Wes rows about $8.9M.

## K1. Three numbered changes, the first one trivially easy (Keith)

"3 Simple Changes", Dr. Paris (`15TvJnQGCOcmp8HUqJojhLqhOX09iHwrqy7DmJILKQSA`, March 2021). Rows 6 and 9: $1,033,345 /
$70.61 and $883,674 / $68.26; row 2 ($2,784,155 / $78.47) is very likely the same script. About $4.7M combined.

> There are 3 simple changes I tell all of my Arthritis patients to make.
>
> #1 - You have to start drinking more water, you wouldn't believe how dehydrated most people are.
>
> #2 - You need to make a decision to take matters into your own hands, that's not to say you should go against your current doctors, but you don't have to settle for a cabinet full of pills…
>
> #3 - I tell them to raid their own refrigerator.
>
> Out of the 5 foods listed as the Worst Foods for Arthritis… most of my patients were eating at least 4 of them, EVERY SINGLE DAY!

Technique: item 1 is so easy the viewer agrees at once; item 2 gives permission to act; item 3 is the offer. Then a
stat the viewer checks against himself ("at least 4 of them"). Abs By AI use: "3 changes I made to lose my belly fat at
40", with the last change leading to the app.

## K2. The same winning script, retold by a peer in the first person (Keith)

"In The Fridge", read by a senior customer, Elmer (transcript in Dan's doc `1V7Xo51ZarLXPvoGrJpwjFZpCcf7xzWyHqT-wVCZKZnU`).
**$1,331,699, $57.99 CPA**, better than the doctor version.

> You know I made three simple changes to my diet that drastically reduced my arthritis pain. Number one, I started drinking more water. I had no idea how dehydrated my body really was.

Technique: take a proven expert script and have a customer say it about himself. Cheap variant, better CPA. Abs By AI
use: Dan already speaks as the customer; a member or a peer can retell his winning scripts.

## K3. Flip what he believes about the hero ingredient, then raise the stakes (Keith)

"Elmer Egg Photo", voiceover over one still photo, then testimonials (same Dan doc). **$950,076, $57.67 CPA, Keith's
best.** Dan wrote in 2023 that it "substantially outperformed other ads".

> Say, did you know protein may be the cause of your arthritis and chronic joint pain? Yeah. My whole life, I thought protein was good for me. The truth is not all protein is good protein. ... In fact, nine out of 10 Americans are exposed to this protein every day, even vegans. It's so serious researchers are now calling it the joint pain protein.

Technique: 138 words. Overturn a belief the viewer holds about something "healthy", name the villain, give a number,
escalate. The format (a still photo plus voiceover) is the cheapest ad there is to make.

## K4. The enemy is inside you, and it's not your fault (Keith)

"Bionic Boomers" (`1dZA0-mG7myrGuAqCMuOfu1ge70CYomxBfFDtBqLdZ7k`, 2022). $1,039,871, $85.36 CPA (worst in the top 10,
but a long-running control).

> The reason why so many people are still struggling with stiff - achy joints… is not genetics, it's not getting older… or wear and tear…
>
> For most people… it's the Joint Pain Protein…
>
> It's not your fault… you were listening to experts, people that are "supposed" to have all the answers…

Technique: rule out the three causes the viewer blames ("not genetics, not getting older"), name one hidden cause, then
forgive him and blame the experts. It also opens on a news-feature voiceover ("They're called…. Bionic Boomers…").
Dan uses the "not your fault" move too (`ads.md` A4); Keith's version leans harder on ellipses and emotion.

## K5. Two enemies in a row (Wes)

"2 Bad Proteins", Dr. Paris (transcript in Dan's doc `1V7Xo51…`). $879,959, $73.78 CPA. Roughly the back half of this
script is Dan's own SRM close almost word for word; only the two-enemy structure, the clinic credential and the outside
quote are Wes's.

> Now, the first protein you should never eat if you have arthritis is going to surprise you.
>
> It's gluten. ...
>
> Reducing your gluten intake can have a powerful effect on your arthritis pain, but scientists have discovered a second protein that has even greater impact on your joint pain than gluten.

Technique: give away a known enemy first (credibility, and the viewer feels smart), then hold back a bigger one for the
click.
