## L2. Opener: the verdict as a command, then the objection out loud

Source `15gg6GP_Huy93ZBfTpbQuUtHGDrgHL-bs6Op-nN2UBr8` (Second Shoot Outlines, 2026-07-14). Dan, Tier A.

> Today I'm going to show you why you MUST work out every single day.
>
> Even if you are a total beginner who doesn't work out at all right now, I still recommend you work out every single day. That includes holidays, your birthdays, when you feel awful…every single day
>
> You're probably thinking…how can I work out every day if I can't even get myself to the gym every once in a while?
>
> And the answer is…NO. You won't be overtraining. And it's actually easier to make yourself work out every day than 1-2 days a week. And I'm going to show you why

volley ("Is that fair? No."): the question is the viewer's, not a rhetorical trick.
