## V1. Letter open: headline, hook, the reveal held with an ellipsis

Letter, Dan's rewrite, Tier A.

> I Fired My Personal Trainer And Used AI To Get Abs Instead
>
> Hi, I'm Dan. In today's video and article, I want to tell you the wild story of how I finally lost my stubborn belly…and got the six pack abs I always wanted at the age of 40.
>
> How did I do it?
>
> Believe it or not, it was by FIRING my personal trainer and nutritionist - and replacing them with an AI app.
>
> Today I want to teach you the five AI techniques that I used to get in the best shape of my life. I promise these are going to surprise you…ESPECIALLY the last one.

Why: an enemy headline ("Fired My Personal Trainer"). The question he answers himself on the next line, with one word
in caps. The promise ends on a tease for the last item.

## V2. Story and agitation, then "Maybe you can relate"

Letter, Dan's rewrite, Tier A.

> At 38, I was constantly busy and stressed out as hell.
>
> The result? I steadily gained fat year after year. Until at 38, I had a bloated, weak, out-of-shape dad bod.
>
> Maybe you can relate.
>
> Does it feel like you're so slammed with responsibilities that making time to work out and eat right is nearly impossible?
>
> If you feel that way, you're not alone. That's how MOST hard-working, regular guys like us feel.
>
> The truth is, the old way of getting in shape works just fine for college kids. But it doesn't work for guys like us.

Why: his low point in plain words with a mild swear. "The result?" as a turn. Then the bridge to the reader ("Maybe you
can relate."), a question in the reader's own words, reassurance, and an old way vs. new way frame that blames the
method, never the reader.

## V3. Failure, then the obsession that proves it

Letter, Dan's rewrite, Tier A.

> I got real excited about the idea. So I went to ChatGPT, asked it to make me a personalized fitness plan, and…
>
> My First AI Fitness Plan Turned Out Absolutely Awful!
>
> Most people would have given up here. But I didn't. I knew that AI could create a superior fitness plan for me and help me get in shape. I just had to put in the work.
>
> I subscribed to five different frontier AI models, spending over $1000 a month on AI subscriptions and API credits.

Why: the sentence breaks off into a Title Case subhead ending in "!" (his letter device). A failure he admits, then
real effort in a real number. Same move as the book: his own loss first, then the lesson (`book.md` B3).
