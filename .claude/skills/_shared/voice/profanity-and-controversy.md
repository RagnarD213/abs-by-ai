# Dan's profanity and controversy: the rules

Built 2026-10-08 from every swear and blunt line in his own words (about 565,000: the 2026 Abs By AI videos, the 2020
to 2021 business channel, podcasts, two books, ads, sales videos) and from what he did to Claude's swears. Full tally:
`voice-corpus/_work/swearing/tally.tsv` (local only). S = spoken, W = written.

**No swear is ever required.** 12 of his 22 off-the-cuff 2026 videos have none. If the outline holds no junk, waste or
excuse to name, write none. Never add one to sound like him.

## The rules, ranked by evidence

**1. Most pieces have none. Swears cluster in opinion, review and money videos.** All 8 workout, exercise demo and
recipe videos: zero. The Oura review alone has four.

**2. The swear is the plain name of the thing, not a stock idiom.** 14 of his 24 swears in 2026 videos are "bullshit"
as a noun for junk, "shitty" as an adjective, or "crap". Stock idioms ("like hell", "the hell out of", "the damn
picture", "do shit for you") are 7 of Claude's 13 and 2 of his 24. Off the cuff in 2026 he says no "hell", "ass" or
"piss", and "damn" once, in the viewer's mouth. He never minces ("freaking", "heck": zero).
- "You're just wasting the money you do have on bullshit." (Oura review, 2026, S)
- "...yet you're sleeping on a shitty mattress right now..." (Invest More In Your Health, 2026, S)

**3. It lands on a thing (46%), the viewer's excuse or habit (29%) or himself (21%). Never on a person or a group:**
0 of 24.
- "...show you why they're all a crock of shit..." (the viewer's excuses; Work Out At Home On A Budget, 2026, S)
- "I uploaded my shitty pictures from my last photo shoot up into my AI" (My First Spray Tan, 2026, S)
- "If you're not working out or eating healthy, you're basically shitting all over God's temple." (he typed it into
  Claude's script and said it; 2026)

**4. It sits inside a sentence that does other work.** Median sentence: 20 words. 62% mid-sentence, 29% the last word
of a full sentence, 8% a short verdict after the reason is given ("It is the shittiest solution."). Never the opener:
his earliest is 135 words in.

**5. Insult words name a condition the viewer can change, never his character.** Fat, broke, poor, lazy, cheap: yes,
stated as cause and effect with the fix next. "Stupid" is for his own old thinking. He softened Claude's "you are too
squeamish" to "you don't have the skill".
- "But that type of short term thinking is the reason why you're poor right now." (2026, S)
- "That was stupid thinking." (his old view of Zepbound, 2026, S)

**6. The edge is the opinion, and it needs no swear.** "Stop doing deadlifts." has none.

## How he states a controversial opinion

2026 fitness content:

1. **Flat, as a command or a numbered reason.** "Stop doing deadlifts." (first sentence, S) "Reason number one. You have
   a fat girlfriend or a fat wife." (RO-14, W)
2. **A flag, if any, comes before the claim: one plain sentence, then the reason.** "Now, I know that sounds extreme. So
   let me explain to you why you have to do this." (S; the script said "I know how that sounds") Never an apology
   after it. He cut "I know how that sounds. I mean it anyway." and wrote "You will literally die sooner if you settle
   for a fat wife..." (RO-14, W). He cut the balancing paragraph ("And before anybody gets upset...").
3. **He speaks the viewer's objection in the viewer's words, then denies it in four.** "I deserve a break because I
   work hard, but you don't deserve a break. You deserve a break once you're ripped" (S). He makes 21 flat denials of
   this kind in 75,000 words; Claude's drafts make none. Claude's winks at the audience ("to be fair", "hate me for
   this", "somebody has to"): 8 in 15,000 words. His: 1 in 75,000.
4. **He concedes the fact and keeps the stance.** "...I know y'all nerds in the comments right now are saying, that's
   not what the scientific research says. And you're right." (S)
5. **Medical: one sentence, then the opinion.** "I'm not a doctor, and this is not medical advice, so talk to your
   doctor first. But I'll tell you what I really think." (S)
6. **Backing: his own reversal or loss, a number, a consequence.** "...I have gotten more injuries from deadlifts than
   every other lift in the gym combined." (S) "So stop putting that money in your 401k. Instead put it into your
   health." rests on a friend's diabetes bills. A hard sacrifice is made temporary: "I'm not telling you you got to
   sacrifice that permanently." (S)
7. **"In my opinion" is his** (48 uses). It claims the stance. It does not soften it.

2020 to 2021, business topics (S): the stance is the first sentence at full strength ("College is a scam. And if
you're going to college, you need to drop out right now."), then one beat on the reaction and straight to math or an
authority ("some of you might have gotten a little mad watching that intro"). When a hook aimed a name at people, his
next breath explained it or took it back ("I don't really think you're an asshole I just think you're a little bit
misinformed just like I was").

## Where a swear belongs in a script

- Opinion, review or money video? If not, none.
- Is there junk, waste or an excuse in the outline to name? If not, none.
- The word is the name of that thing ("bullshit", "shitty", "crap"), inside a sentence of 12 words or more that also
  carries the consequence.
- Not the opener or first minute, not a kicker, not its own sentence, not at a person or group, not in a title.
- A second swear only on the same target. Then stop.
- Delete it. If the line still makes its point, leave it deleted.
