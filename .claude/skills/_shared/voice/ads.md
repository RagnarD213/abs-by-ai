agency Social Response Marketing (SRM) for clients and for on-camera talent (a doctor, a business owner, a self-defense

because the scripts are the client's. Raw copies: Drive folder `1FE1_fv6XhV96w4OQDji51w7Lrz6ZpiOM`.

**Copy the moves, not the topic.** HBI's audience is older people with arthritis and joint pain; Abs By AI's is men 35
and up who want abs. Keep the structure (question hook, time box, testimonials, named mechanism, free-value close) and
drop the age framing, the doctor voice and the compliance hedges.

The structure Dan names himself in his outlines (Spy Briefing, 2022): **Skip Stopper, Believable Promise, First CTA,
Conditioning Content, Second CTA.** His 2019 book teaches the same parts. Every ad below follows it.

## A1. The biggest ad: a quiz over props, a time box, testimonials do the proving

HBI "Dr Paris Protein Equals Original Testimonials" (`1MTi-ReCiFmSSiNPfDy0KyehULpXrC8mRINWZGKXIuS8`, July 2020, read by
Dr. Brian Paris). SRM, Tier A. **$5,963,939 spend, $66.66 CPA, the client's biggest ad of all time.** Adapted from Dan's
April 2020 script for talent Mark ("Protein Equals Pain Testimonials-Mark", $904,578, $48.70 CPA).

> Hi, I'm Dr. Brian Paris. And I have an important question for anyone with arthritis.
>
> Which one of these foods contains a protein that can make your arthritis pain worse?
>
> [4 foods on screen: Tomato, Bread, Cucumber, Apple]
>
> You'll learn the answer in this quick 3 minute video.
>
> Here's what happened when people like you with arthritis stopped eating this protein:
>
> [testimonial montage]
>
> Pretty amazing, right?
>
> This presentation will only be free for a limited time, so I urge you to watch it now while it's still available. Don't procrastinate! Click "Watch Now" to take advantage of this opportunity, and to learn which protein makes your arthritis pain worse.

Why: the viewer plays a guessing game in the first five seconds. The time box ("quick 3 minute video") lowers the cost
of staying. Dan's words are only the frame; real people's results carry the ad, and "Pretty amazing, right?" is the
whole bridge out of them. Close = free for a limited time, "Don't procrastinate!", and the promise repeated in the CTA.

## A2. Best CPA: aspiration first, the cause is "not medication", a 60-second reveal

HBI "ad scripts | hbi | april | 2020 | MARK" (`1Q32YqOspDsiDXsx9wc3knuSzo07pPxPFiEEzAC4yrpQ`), "Seniors having fun"
section. SRM, Tier A. Most likely the row "Five Foods To Never Eat If You Have Arthritis | MARK": **$854,862 spend,
$48.14 CPA, the best CPA in the top 10** (the match to this section is the reader's inference).

> It's so amazing how people are living active lives with arthritis in their seventies, eighties, and beyond. Just a few years ago, doctors thought this would be impossible.
>
> How are people with arthritis enjoying gardening...dancing...golf...and the active life they've always loved? Why are some seniors having the time of their life hiking….biking...and skiing, while others can barely leave the house?
>
> It's not because of new medication or treatment. It's simply because these people started making small changes to their diet. And I'll reveal exactly how these people started eating differently in just sixty seconds.

Why: it opens on the outcome the viewer wants, seen in other people. The question splits the audience into winners and
losers. "It's not because of X. It's simply because Y." names the mechanism by ruling out the obvious one. Abs By AI
shape: "How are busy dads over 40 getting abs…?"

## A3. Mechanism in plain words, then "But here's the good news…"

Same doc, same section. SRM, Tier A.

> Most people aren't even aware the foods they eat have a huge effect on their arthritis pain. But there is a huge body of scientific research proving that it does.
>
> That's because certain foods have been proven to cause a process called "inflammation" in your body. This is when your immune system responds to a perceived threat, causing swelling and pain.
>
> But here's the good news…
>
> The doctors at our Institute have found that you can significantly reduce inflammation in your body just by avoiding five common foods. Some of these foods are commonly thought of as healthy -- and for most people, they are. But for those of us with arthritis, they're proven to make our pain worse.

Why: "Most people aren't even aware…" makes the viewer an insider. One plain definition. Then the turn on its own
line. The villain hides inside a "healthy" food, which is true for most people but not "for those of us with…". His
dash is a double hyphen, never an em dash.

> Listen - dieting sucks. You have to choke down disgusting healthy foods, and you have to give up all your favorite treats, and you have to suffer through grueling exercise.

## A5. Concede, then argue with concrete numbers (2022)

"ad scripts | spy briefing | february 2022 | MIKE'S SCRIPTS" (`1AZUSGQdzksSBRS7-wQI7CQvmpzJ5Bn_lqlEDWz6AEkc`). SRM, Tier A.
The doc calls the offer its "top performing offer".

> Do NOT use boxing in a street fight! It could get you killed!
>
> OK…so to start off with, I want to say that I don't hate boxing. Boxing is great exercise, and it's an entertaining sport for fans to watch.
>
> Let's say you're 5'6" and 150 pounds. If you get attacked by someone who's 6'5" and 300 pounds, no amount of boxing technique will save you.
>
> Or let's say you're seventy years old, and you're attacked by a twenty year old punk. Boxing is not going to help you!

Why: a command with a capitalized NOT. He grants the enemy its good side before attacking it, so he never sounds
absolutist. "Let's say…" turns the argument into numbers the viewer can picture.
