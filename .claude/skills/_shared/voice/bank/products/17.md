okay so in today's video we're going to talk about the exploration and drilling method of maximizing campaign profits this is a strategy that we've developed over the past few years and it's worked really well for us to maximize profits in our clients accounts and I think it's gonna work really well for you too so before we talk about the strategy let me talk about the two major types of campaigns that you can set up in Google Ads so first there's single ad group campaigns these are campaigns that usually have one ad group and one ad in each ad group there's also multiple ad group campaigns and these would be campaigns with many different ad groups maybe 10 or 20 or 30 different ad groups and multiple different ads in each ad group where there might be two or three or four different ads that you're testing in each ad group. Now I've looked at many different Google Ads accounts and I've seen people doing very well with both types of campaign strategies. I've seen ad managers that do really well with single ad group campaigns and on the other hand there's also ad managers that do well with multiple ad group campaigns. So obviously both strategies work.

But the question that we had in our agency is which strategy is the best? We had ad managers doing well with both types of campaigns. So we did this multi-month test across multiple different client accounts to determine once and for all which type of campaign was the best. And what we found out was actually you need both types of campaigns.
