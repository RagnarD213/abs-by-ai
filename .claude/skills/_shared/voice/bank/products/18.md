However, just know for now that placements is when you target an app, a channel, or a website to serve your ads on. Keywords are essentially when you put in certain words into your Google targeting system, and then it finds pages or videos related to those keywords to serve your ads on. So let's say, for example, I put in as my keyword, YouTube advertising. Google will then serve my ads on videos on pages related to YouTube advertising.

Now, it's important to understand that display keywords, what we're using to target our ads, function differently than search keywords. Now, let's say if I put in YouTube advertising as a search keyword, if you're familiar with search advertising, if I put in like a phrase match or an exact match, and even if I put in what's called a broad match in search advertising, Google would serve my ads on searches that are very highly related to YouTube advertising. For display keywords, what we're using, they function differently. Google will still serve my ads on pages and videos related to YouTube advertising, but it will take a much broader view of what's related than in search advertising.

So it won't necessarily be every video it serves on is all about YouTube advertising. Some will be, but it might also be some videos about online advertising in general that tangentially touch on YouTube advertising. It might be about videos related to that people interested in YouTube advertising have watched. It's going to be kind of a broad interpretation of what's related, but it will be related and it will be people that are interested in this display keyword.

Next targeting option you have to know about is topics. Topics are basically manually curated categories of content that Google creates that you can advertise your video on. Now, let's say, for example, if you're advertising to bodybuilders, there is a specific topic for bodybuilding. You could serve your ads just on bodybuilding related videos.

And again, it's a broad interpretation. It's not necessarily only things with bodybuilding in the title of the video and all about bodybuilding. It might be also things generally related to nutrition and muscle building and tangentially related to bodybuilding.
