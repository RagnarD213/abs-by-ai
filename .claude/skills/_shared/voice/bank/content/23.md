Some people think that you should pay off the highest interest debt first. Other people think that you should pay off the smallest debt first. Paying off the highest interest debt first makes the most mathematical sense. But I think you should pay off your smallest debt first because that makes the most psychological and emotional sense.

And I say this from experience, having talked with many people recently that are in debt and having thought about a lot what's really going to get these people momentum to pay off their debt and to eventually get to financial freedom and wealth. What I've found is that getting in debt is really an emotional, psychological process much more than a mathematical process. Yes, it does make mathematical sense to pay off your highest interest debt first. But let's say your highest interest debt is $100,000.

dollars it's gonna take you a long time to pay off that debt and you're not gonna get a dopamine hit of good emotions from having it all paid off in my experience from talking with people in this situation going after your smallest debt let's say something that's just a few hundred or a few thousand dollars will pay off more in the long run the reason for that is that once you have that debt paid off you're gonna feel good about yourself and it's gonna encourage you to pay off more of your debts. So pay off your smallest debt first, not necessarily your highest interest debt. You don't even have to calculate the interest rates on your debt right now. Just go from smallest to biggest and get everything paid off.

Alright so last step, getting out of debt I do think is going to be a process of building momentum and building positive emotional momentum more than anything. So what I want you to do every time you pay off your debt, even a small one for just a couple hundred dollars, I want you to reward yourself with a celebration.
