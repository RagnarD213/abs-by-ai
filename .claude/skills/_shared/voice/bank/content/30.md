Does jiu jitsu get you abs?

I'm a Brazilian Jiu Jitsu black belt who's been training for 22 years. And I've had a lot of people asking me if jiu jitsu is a good way to get abs.

Jiu jitsu does give you a great ab workout, both with the conditioning exercises we do and with wrestling live rounds. But it might be the hardest possible way to get abs. Ab training is a great bonus if you love jiu jitsu. But if you just want abs and you don't care about wrestling, gym ab exercises do the job faster.

Also, remember that nutrition is 80% of getting abs whether you do jiu jitsu or not. You can't out-train a bad diet, not even with jiu jitsu training. So if you want to get abs, you should be mainly focused on how you're eating, not how you're training.

So if just want abs, focus on your nutrition. But if you love jiu jitsu, burning extra calories and getting in a great ab workout is a good bonus to the sport.
