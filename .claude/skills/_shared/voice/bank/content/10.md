With the leg press that's going to be a machine at the gym. You load up that machine with weights, you set your safety stop so you can't go down too far, you go down to your safety stop and then you go back up. You push through your heels just like you're doing a squat. So this does require a gym membership most of the time to get that machine but the leg press is the safest way to train your legs and remember staying injury free and training with leg presses for a couple years straight will build more muscle than training with deadlifts building more muscle to begin with but then periodically getting injured and getting set back.

The final exercise that I do to build an aesthetic back is the rear delt fly. There are two ways to do this you can use the machine that I'm showing you at the gym right now or you can use this way that I'm showing you on screen right now with the dumbbells going in the bent over row position and then doing your rear delt fly back. So whichever variation you do you want to make sure that you're not just rowing backwards when you do this and that you're going in a circle and contracting your back. One detail that helps me focus on this is imagining there is a pencil between your shoulder blades and with each rep you want to squeeze that pencil between your shoulder.

This exercise is really going to help your posture build those rear delts and again build an aesthetic back in a low-risk way.
