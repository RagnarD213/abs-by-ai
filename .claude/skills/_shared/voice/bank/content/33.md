Hey guys, here are the top five ab exercises you should concentrate on if you want to get six pack abs.

Number one, the ab wheel. I love the ab wheel because it gives you constant tension on your abs, meaning you're working the whole time without giving your abs a break. It's very challenging, even if you have a strong core. And if you're a beginner, you can still do it - just roll the wheel our less far.

Number two, the spiderman plank. I love these because they work your transverse abdominus - the ab muscle that stabilizes your core, and makes your waist look tight and small. They also give you a great workout for your entire upper body. And they're way less boring than just holding a regular plank

Number three, the cable crunch. The cable crunch is the upgraded version of the ab wheel you can do if you have gym access. I love it because it's easy for beginners to do and very low risk, even if you're just getting started. But it can also be very difficult for advanced lifters as well if you do it with good form and increase the weight.

Number four, the V-sit twist. I love this exercise because it works your obliques - the muscles on the sides of your abs. This doesn't make a huge difference if you still have belly fat. But once you're lean, building shredded obliques will make a huge visual difference.

And number five, the toe touch. I love the toe touch because it's simple for beginners to do, you can do it at home, and it's a great ab workout if you use the right form. If you're a beginner, you can get a great ab workout without needing any weight. And if you're advanced, add a five pound weight or medicine ball to make it more challenging.

Those are my top five ab exercises. Leave a comment to let me know which one is your favorite, and any others that you think guys should be doing.
