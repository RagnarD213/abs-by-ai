The way I'm going to be demonstrating today, because this is the way most people should do it, will be without any equipment. I'm going to have my toes and my feet on the ground. I'm flat up in a line and then I'm going to jackknife my body and touch my hands to my toes. So in order to do this, it's important that you get a good contraction at the top.

And there's a few details that you need to focus on in order to make sure that happens. So number one, make sure you're getting your head and your butt as far off the ground as possible. If you're doing toe touches like this, but your head and your butt are still on the ground, you're just waving your arms and your legs around. You're not really activating your abs.

So focus on getting your butt and your head off the ground. The second detail to focus on sounds a little bit weird, but what I focus on when I'm doing this is I try to knee myself in the face as hard as I can. Just try to break my own nose. So when you're doing this, don't worry about it.

You can't actually break your own nose when you're doing this kind of exercise. But if you aim for that type of motion, you'll be getting the right type of contraction. So I'm here and then I aim to knee myself in the face and you see that makes my butt and my head come off the ground a little bit more. That means my abs are working a little bit more.
