OK...so let's talk about the best way for you to make money from home.

When most people think about how to start a home based business, they typically think about getting some type of product and selling it. But this is actually the WORST way to make money from home. The much better way that I recommend is developing a high value skill, and getting businesses to hire you with that skill.

Unless you invest at least $50,000 in getting your business up and running, you'll be at a severe disadvantage. Most people don't have that kind of money laying around. And even for those that do, most people aren't comfortable with putting this much of their savings at risk.

But when you have a high value skill, it's easy to get businesses to hire you. And since you don't have to pay for product costs, inventory costs, or shipping costs, your profits will be much higher.
