Businesses need customers -- or they die. And most businesses don't understand how online advertising works.

If you can figure out how to send them customers from ad campaigns, they'll happily send you a portion of the revenue they make from the customers you send them.

Now, I'm doing very well with this business model. But I'm not the only one doing this. Thousands of entrepreneurs all around the world have used this business model to become financially free, while risking very little of their own money up front.

There are many types of online advertising campaigns that you can run for businesses. I've run them all at one point or another. But I made the decision to specialize in one form of online advertising, because it's far more lucrative for me than all the rest.

And that's the form of advertising you're seeing right now. YouTube advertising!

I've done so much better managing YouTube ad campaigns for clients than I have with Google search, Facebook, or Instagram campaigns. And that's for one simple reason -- on YouTube, there's far less advertising competition.

Facebook, Instagram, and search are all saturated with deep-pocketed, highly skilled advertisers competing against you. Of course, there's competition on YouTube too. But most YouTube advertisers are doing a horrible job running their campaigns, which presents a great opportunity for you to do it better.
