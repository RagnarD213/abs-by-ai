## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Spoken by Dan Rose direct to a viewer who has seen several video ads in his series but has not yet claimed his book.

Points, in order:
- Opens by ordering the viewer to pay attention, addressing them with a crude insult.
- Introduces himself as Dan Rose, self-described "Godfather" of advertising on YouTube, and says he is about to make a deal the viewer will be unable to turn down.
- Says he is puzzled: this is the eighth video of his the viewer has seen in the series. The viewer evidently cares about running YouTube ads and sees him as the person to teach it, yet still has not acted to get the "15 Steps" book.
- First possible reason: the viewer is broke and lacks the $20 the book costs on Amazon. Answer: get the book at no cost from his site by clicking the "Claim Book" button below.
- Second possible reason: the viewer dislikes reading. He admits he does too. An audiobook can be added to the order, far cheaper than on Audible. Paperback, eBook and audiobook formats are all available when claiming.
- Says he can see the viewer's YouTube views in his stats, which show eight videos watched from him and Marina within this one series.
- Asks whether the viewer has sat through eight ads from any other advertiser, and doubts it. Says holding their attention that long proves he and Marina have worthwhile lessons to pass on.
- Says if the viewer could hold their own audience the same way, they would likely earn a lot more.
- Says it also shows the viewer is brighter and more driven than most people. Some skip such ads and return to cat videos, while motivated people like the viewer recognise a chance to improve their lives.

The point: Remove the viewer's excuses (price, dislike of reading) and flatter them into clicking to claim the "15 Steps" book now, using their own repeated viewing as evidence that the method works.

## Claude, cold, from that brief
Listen up, you little shit.

My name is Dan Rose. I'm the Godfather of advertising on YouTube, and I'm about to make you an offer you can't refuse.

But first, I've gotta be honest with you. I'm confused.

This is the eighth video you've seen from me in this series. The eighth. So obviously you care about running YouTube ads, and obviously you think I'm the guy to teach you how to do it. And yet you still haven't taken action and gotten my "15 Steps" book.

So what's the deal?

Maybe you're broke. Maybe you don't have the twenty bucks the book costs on Amazon. Fine. Then don't pay for it. Go to my site and get it for free. Just click the "Claim Book" button below.

Or maybe you don't like to read. Hey, I get it. I don't like to read either. So add the audiobook to your order. It's way cheaper than you'd pay on Audible. Paperback, eBook, audiobook, they're all right there when you claim your book.

Here's the thing. I can see your YouTube views in my stats. And my stats say you've watched eight videos from me and Marina in this one series alone.

Let me ask you something. Have you ever sat through eight ads from any other advertiser? I doubt it.

So what does that tell you? If we can hold your attention for that long, that's proof that me and Marina have something worth teaching you.

And think about this. If you could hold your own audience's attention the way we're holding yours right now, you'd probably be making a whole lot more money.

It tells me something else, too. It tells me you're smarter and more driven than most people. Look, some people see ads like this, hit skip, and go right back to their cat videos. But motivated people like you? You recognize an opportunity to make your life better when you see one.
