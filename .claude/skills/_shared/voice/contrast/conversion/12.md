## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan, the head of a YouTube advertising coaching business, speaking in first person to a prospect who is being offered the Gold tier as a lower alternative to Platinum.

Points, in order:
- Opens by saying he will explain how the Gold tier differs from the Platinum tier of coaching.
- Gold includes the identical plan in writing, covering three months, prepared by the company's experts.
- On Gold, the first call that follows the free call is run by his team; he does not attend in person.
- The customer still receives guidance from top-level YouTube specialists schooled in his method.
- His coaches still hold regular calls to keep the customer on track and point out further ways to improve their campaigns.
- On Gold those calls happen fortnightly rather than weekly.
- Instruction: press the button for claiming the free call to begin.
- Reminder: he has forbidden his coaches to do any sales pitch during the free call; they will only share their best advertising ideas and their methods for managing ads.
- So the prospect risks nothing and is certain to come away with strong recommendations tailored to them.
- Repeats the instruction to press the free-call button, then signs off saying he will speak with them soon.

The point: Show that Gold keeps most of what Platinum offers (minus his personal presence and with half as many calls) and get the prospect to book the free, no-sales call right away.

## Claude, cold, from that brief
So let me explain how Gold coaching is different from Platinum.

With Gold, you still get the exact same written 3-month plan, put together by our experts.

The difference is that your first call after the free call will be run by my team. I won't be there in person.

But you're still going to get guidance from elite YouTube specialists who've been trained in my method.

And my coaches will still get on regular calls with you to keep you on track and show you even more ways to improve your campaigns.

The only other difference is that on Gold, those calls happen every two weeks instead of every week.

So to get started, just click the "Claim My Free Call" button right now.

And remember, I've forbidden my coaches from doing any kind of sales pitch on your free call. All they're going to do is share their best advertising ideas and their methods for managing ads.

So you've got nothing to lose, and you're guaranteed to walk away with some great recommendations made specifically for you.

Go ahead and click the "Claim My Free Call" button now, and I'll talk to you soon.
