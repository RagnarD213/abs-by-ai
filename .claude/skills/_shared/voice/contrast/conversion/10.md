## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Written in Dan's first person, addressed to prospective buyers of his advanced course on YouTube advertising.

Points, in order:
- Headline promise: the reader will learn an easy, tested method for rapidly scaling ad campaigns on YouTube.
- Introduces the list of what the buyer receives on claiming the discount. The main product is the "Black Belt" course in managing YouTube ads, normally $950 and $450 today.
- Scarcity: 158 early-bird places are left, after which the cost returns to $950.
- The course is described as the world's top advanced training in this subject, covering all that is required to turn a profit from YouTube ads fast and without complexity or difficulty.
- Included ebook: its title refers to fifteen steps toward profitable marketing on YouTube. It is described as a top seller on Amazon, covering ad script writing, ad creation and running winning campaigns.
- Included audiobook of the same book, for building ad skills while exercising, driving, doing housework or otherwise occupied.
- Members may put any question about YouTube ads to Dan and get his reply on video. This is compared to having him on a $9500-a-month consulting retainer, but at no charge as part of membership.
- Bonus FAQ video series: answers to every earlier student question, searchable so you can check whether yours has been covered.
- Guarantee: your profits from YouTube ads will grow through this course, or Dan refunds twice what you paid.
- How to claim: send a screen capture of your campaigns showing $1000 or more of advertising spend during which you tried to apply the course material. If that first $1000 was not profitable, he will happily pay the double refund.

The point: Get the reader to buy the course now at the discounted early-bird price, by stacking up everything included and removing risk with a twice-the-price refund guarantee.
