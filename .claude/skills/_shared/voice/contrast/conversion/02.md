About Dan Rose

Dan Rose is widely regarded as the #1 YouTube direct response marketer in the world. He is the founder and CEO of two businesses: Six Pack Shortcuts and SixPackAbs.com. Over five years, he personally managed more than $27.2 million in ad spend.

Using YouTube ads, he took the company from zero sales and zero employees to over $22 million in revenue in 2016, with a worldwide team of more than 107 people.

Now Dan and his team offer coaching and hands-on help to other businesses with their YouTube ads. In this video, Dan gives you his 3 best tips that you can apply right away to make your campaigns more profitable.

Our Approach

Our approach is simple. We merge the strengths of brand-style ads with the measurable, profitable nature of direct response.

Here's why.

Brand-only advertising is the wrong choice for most businesses. You can't measure the results, and you don't get any immediate profit from it.

But going direct response only is wrong too. It doesn't build a community, and a community is what keeps your ad success going over the long term.

The truth is, advertising is changing. Consumers see so many ads every single day that a good sales pitch alone isn't enough anymore. Your pitch has to be supported by strong proof, so your prospects know that your business is the real deal.

That's why every ad we make is designed to do two things at once: build your brand AND earn an immediate profit.

And every ad is supported by a content strategy, so your ads are growing a long-term community at the same time they're bringing in sales.

This is the reason we have some of the longest-lasting channels and brands on YouTube. And it's the reason this approach has paid off for us over the last seven years.
