The point: To prove with two client cases, a beginner and an established business, that his YouTube advertising method produces real income, so viewers at either level believe it will work for them and buy the book.

## Claude, cold, from that brief
And here's the thing. When you follow the right method, in the right order, step by step, the way I lay it out in my book, marketing on YouTube is actually really easy to pick up. Now, you might be thinking, "Okay Dan, that sounds great, but what kind of results are people actually getting right now?" Fair question.

So let me share a couple with you. And keep in mind, my clients are at every level of experience, from total beginners all the way up to some of the best marketers in the world. Let's start with Amy. Amy was the very first client in my strategic coaching program.

At the time, she was working for me while also holding down a regular job. And she did not like that job. Her goal was simple: leave it and make her income on the internet. So over several months, I trained her in marketing on YouTube.

How to script ads. How to produce the creative. How to manage campaigns. Everything that's covered in my book.

And in her first few months, Amy landed her first paying client. She managed that client's YouTube advertising for $6,000 a month. Now, if you're an experienced YouTube advertiser, you might be thinking $6,000 a month isn't a lot of money. I get it.

But for Amy, it was a strong start. That was the income that let her walk in and resign. And she's gone on to achieve far more since then. So if you have a job right now, and you want to quit and make your money on the internet, that's a target you can hit in a matter of months.

If you really commit to it. But maybe that's not you. Maybe you already own a business, and you're wondering what you can expect. So let me tell you about two more clients of mine, Glenn and Natalie Ledwell.

They own Mind Movies, one of the leading companies in the personal development field. I've been handling their YouTube advertising for a while now. Before we started working together, they already had a successful business. They were already among the top marketers on the internet and in their field.

But they were doing very little on YouTube. In our most recent month working together, their YouTube campaigns alone brought in over $300,000 in revenue.
