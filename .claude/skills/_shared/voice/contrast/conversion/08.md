## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Spoken by Dan, the book's author, directly to viewers of a sales video who are considering YouTube advertising, both beginners and people already running campaigns. It opens as an affirmative reply, as if answering an interviewer.

Points, in order:
- Start with a yes, then say there is one important thing to consider before the viewer requests the no-cost book.
- First scenario: ask them to picture not requesting the book today and carrying on as they are. Guarantee that nothing in their life will change.
- Spell out the risks of that path: they may never begin because they lack the confidence to act, or, worse, they may launch YouTube campaigns without the know-how and lose a lot of money.
- Second scenario: ask them to imagine how things could change if they apply the method taught in the 15 Steps.
- The book arrives by mail within days. Within the early chapters they will realise they have found a hugely valuable source of marketing knowledge, feel an immediate boost of confidence and know they are capable of doing this.
- Within a few weeks they can put the first steps into practice. Newcomers to YouTube marketing will build a campaign and see profit sooner than they expected; people already running campaigns will see improved ad results in a similar timeframe.
- Next they will scale up quickly and earn larger profits using the author's advanced techniques, with steady growth, more money every week, and a brand that customers love and stay loyal to for years.
- Finally, ask them to imagine three things: learning to turn YouTube profits into passive income; partnering with people who handle all the campaign work for them; and owning a highly engaged YouTube channel that generates sales every day on its own, regardless of whether ads are running.

The point: Get the viewer to request the free book now by contrasting a stagnant or money-losing future without it against a step-by-step picture of growing confidence, profit, scale and eventually passive income with it.
