## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: The seller speaking directly to a customer who has just bought the print version of his book, The 15 Steps, on an upsell page with a countdown timer.

Points, in order:
- The audiobook is sold on its own on the seller's website for $47. Because the customer has proved they take action by getting the print book, he wants to give them a special discount.
- Upgrading inside five minutes brings the audiobook price down to $37, which is $10 below what other buyers pay.
- Added bonus: anyone upgrading today also receives the eBook version free. It usually sells for $27 and comes at no extra cost.
- Taken together, the audiobook plus eBook deal works out to half off.
- To stop the discount link being passed around, the deal switches off when the on-page timer runs out. Anyone wanting the audiobook after that pays the full amount.
- Reasoning for upgrading: the customer has put money into the book and knows it is the system required for profitable YouTube ad campaigns, but they are busy and will struggle to find reading time. The audio version makes learning the material easy, convenient and achievable.
- For anyone still undecided, there is a guarantee. The seller is sure they will love the audio version and rate it the top route to learning his approach to YouTube ads; if they are unhappy for whatever reason, he refunds the whole amount paid.
- To get a refund, email or phone the customer service staff. The full amount is returned without hassle or questions.
- Final call: take the 50% discount now, since the timer ends soon and the offer then disappears for good. He says he will meet them on the following page.

The point: Get the print-book buyer to add the discounted audiobook and free eBook immediately, before the timer expires, by stressing the saving, the convenience and the risk-free refund.
