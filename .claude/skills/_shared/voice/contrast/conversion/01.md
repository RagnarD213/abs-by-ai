## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan, speaking in the first person to people in his own network (video script for a pre-order offer).

Points, in order:
- Dan greets the audience and says he is very excited that his book is nearly finished.
- The title is made up of: The, then the number 15 as a numeral, then Steps To, followed by Profitable YouTube Marketing. He believes it will be the best book available on marketing through YouTube.
- It goes on sale at Amazon roughly one month from now, priced at $19.95.
- He needs testimonials from people he knows before the full launch, so anyone who pre-orders gets the book at no charge, and he pays the shipping too.
- The single condition: the recipient must agree to record a video testimonial with their honest view of the book.
- He then lists what the book teaches: writing strong ad scripts; shooting and editing good-looking commercials on a low budget; growing a channel on YouTube with an active audience that backs up the advertising; creating either a funnel for YouTube traffic or an online shop that converts well.
- The last topic is running a Google Ads account, with three sub-topics: targeting, campaign structure, and advanced campaign types.
- Call to action: complete the forms right away to secure the free copy.

The point: Get viewers to pre-order now by filling in the forms, receiving the book free with shipping paid in return for a promised honest video testimonial.
