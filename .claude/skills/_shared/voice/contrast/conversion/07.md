## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Spoken in first person by the course creator (Dan) to a customer who has just bought his 15 Steps training and is about to begin it.

Points, in order:
- Acknowledge the viewer wants to begin, but say there is one last chance at a discount before they start the 15 Steps.
- The 15 Steps teaches some of the creator's best formulas for making strong YouTube ads; he has used them to produce numerous ads that earned a million dollars, and believes the viewer can do the same.
- To go further, the viewer needs deeper, more advanced training in ad creation, which is why he will soon release a course called Creative Black Belt (for YouTube).
- That course is a masterclass on writing winning ad scripts: generating ad ideas, picking the strongest ones, converting them into outlines, and then into finished scripts.
- It goes beyond scripts: it also covers making those scripts into finished video ads that earn money.
- He will show his exact method for sourcing talent, so the viewer can profit from YouTube ads without appearing in the videos themselves.
- For those who do appear on screen, he will share the drills and exercises he personally uses to improve his performance; doing them over a few weeks produces a big gain in delivery.
- He will also cover filming and editing: which gear to purchase, camera and lighting setup, and video editing.
- He will show how to find low-cost camera operators and video editors, and how to get high-quality work out of them.

The point: Get the new customer to see the upcoming advanced course as the natural next level beyond the 15 Steps, and to be receptive to a final discounted offer on it before they start.

## Claude, cold, from that brief
Hey, I know you're ready to get started, but before you dive into the 15 Steps, I want to give you one last chance to get a discount.

Here's the deal.

In the 15 Steps, you're going to learn some of my best formulas for creating powerful YouTube ads. I've used these formulas to create numerous million-dollar ads, and I believe you can too.

But if you want to take it to the next level, you're going to need deeper, more advanced training on ad creation.

That's why I'll soon be releasing a course called Creative Black Belt for YouTube.

This is a masterclass on writing winning ad scripts. I'm going to show you how to come up with ad ideas, how to choose the strongest ones, how to turn those ideas into outlines, and then how to turn those outlines into finished scripts.

And it doesn't stop at scripts.

I'm also going to show you how to turn those scripts into finished video ads that make money.

For example, I'll show you my exact method for sourcing talent. That means you can profit from YouTube ads without ever appearing in the videos yourself.

Now, if you do want to be on camera, I've got you covered there too. I'm going to share the drills and exercises that I personally use to improve my own performance. If you do these for just a few weeks, you're going to see a big improvement in your delivery.

I'm also going to cover filming and editing. I'll show you which gear to buy, how to set up your camera and your lighting, and how to edit your videos.

And if you'd rather not do all of that yourself, no problem. I'll show you how to find low-cost camera operators and video editors, and how to get high-quality work out of them.
