## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan, speaking in the first person, to a prospective customer who runs YouTube ad campaigns and is being offered an upgrade called Platinum Coaching.

Points, in order:
- When the program begins, Dan builds a tailored YouTube advertising plan: he reviews the customer's account and ads and maps out 90 days of campaign growth.
- He says big companies have paid him more than $50,000 for such plans before, but here it comes at no charge as part of the coaching.
- Benefit: no more guessing how to scale campaigns. The customer simply carries out simple action steps one at a time and can be confident profits are rising.
- Once the plan exists, Dan himself joins the customer's first call to go over it. The customer can ask him directly any question about YouTube advertising, which gets the campaigns started well.
- From then on there is a call every week with two of his senior staff, the Chief Creative Officer and the Chief Advertising Officer. They run the campaigns of his largest clients and rank among the top YouTube Ads experts anywhere.
- On those weekly calls they answer questions about carrying out the steps set for that week, look through the account for further ways to raise ROI, and keep the customer on schedule with the plan so they succeed as other students have.
- Dan is sure the customer will love the calls and that they will help the business grow, which is why the first one costs nothing.
- Ask: claim the free call and get personalised expert advice on improving YouTube Ads. It is highly valuable even for someone who never joins Platinum Coaching, and after speaking with a coach the customer will be well placed to judge whether the program suits them.
- Instruction: press the button underneath, whose label is the word Claim followed by My Free Call. There is no risk, because nothing is charged until the customer says they wish to carry on. The call might turn out to unlock bigger ad campaigns and financial freedom.
- Repeat the instruction to press that button now, and sign off by saying the team will speak with them soon on the call.

The point: Get the viewer to press the button and take a free first coaching call, by showing what Platinum Coaching includes (custom 90-day plan, personal call with Dan, weekly expert calls) and stressing there is no cost or risk in trying it.
