## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: The author, speaking directly to a customer who has just ordered his book on YouTube advertising and is partway through checkout.

Points, in order:
- The order is nearly finished; before the book is sent out, the author offers a final chance to upgrade at a large discount.
- Since his book, titled 15 Steps, came out, readers have sent strong feedback. Some reported extra monthly income from YouTube ads of ten thousand dollars, others twenty thousand, and a few as much as sixty thousand.
- He was glad to hear that, but many readers also wrote about something they wished had come with the book.
- Their request was a way to tailor the book's advice to their own case: how precisely to use its methods, and where their campaign-building ought to differ from the typical approach.
- He states his belief that his title is the finest YouTube-ads book a reader will ever come across, and that it supplies a strong strategy for constructing ad campaigns.
- Like any book, though, it is a general template written for every kind of business owner. A general strategy gets you far and beats having none.
- Better still is having the strategy plus a person who guides you one-on-one, answers your questions and shows how the advice fits your own circumstances.
- So he offers to personally build a custom plan for growing the buyer's YouTube advertising, then get on a phone call to go through it and show how to begin scaling their campaigns.

The point: Persuade the buyer to add a discounted upgrade to their book order: a personalised YouTube ad plan and a one-on-one call with the author, positioned as the thing readers asked for that a general book cannot give.

## Claude, cold, from that brief
Hey, it's Dan Rose again.

Your order is almost complete. But before I send your book out to you, I want to give you one last chance to upgrade your order at a huge discount.

Here's what's going on.

Ever since my book 15 Steps came out, I've been getting amazing feedback from readers. Some of them told me they've added an extra ten thousand dollars a month in income from YouTube ads. Others told me twenty thousand. And a few have even told me they've added as much as sixty thousand dollars a month.

I love hearing that.

But a lot of those same readers also wrote to me about one thing they wished had come with the book.

They wanted a way to customize the advice in the book to their own situation. They wanted to know exactly how to use the methods I teach, and where they should be building their campaigns differently from the typical approach.

Now, I believe 15 Steps is the best book on YouTube ads you're ever going to read. And it gives you a powerful strategy for building your ad campaigns.

But like any book, it's a general template. I wrote it for every kind of business owner. And a general strategy will take you a long way. It's a lot better than having no strategy at all.

But you know what's even better?

Having the strategy, plus having someone who walks you through it one-on-one. Someone who answers your questions and shows you how the advice fits your specific circumstances.

So here's what I'd like to do for you.

I'm going to personally build a custom plan for growing your YouTube advertising. Then I'll get on the phone with you, go through that plan, and show you how to start scaling your campaigns.
