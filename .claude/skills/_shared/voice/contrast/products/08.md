## Claude, cold, from that brief
Keep in mind that birth control pills can fail, just like every other method of contraception. No method is 100% effective. For that reason, you need to agree with your woman ahead of time on what the two of you would do if she became pregnant unexpectedly.

In the United States, you have no legal say once she's pregnant. The decision on whether or not to have an abortion is hers and hers alone. That said, women who are strongly devoted to you will almost always go along with whatever you want, whether that's keeping the baby or ending the pregnancy. If she has firmly held beliefs that clash with yours, you should think seriously about going back to condoms or taking her out of your rotation.

One last tip on contraception: have her keep a dose of emergency contraception on hand. In the US, at the time of this writing, you legally need a prescription for it, so get it beforehand. Waiting even one day to get a prescription makes it far less effective. In most other countries it's sold over the counter. If an emergency happens, put her in the car and drive her to a pharmacy right away.

Abortion

A lot of guys get careless about contraception because they figure abortion is an easy fallback. On paper, it is. The procedure is straightforward and routine. In reality, it's a terrible ordeal, and you should avoid it whatever the cost.

First, after a surgical abortion, you can't have intercourse for six weeks. Starting again any earlier will do her lasting harm.

Second, nearly all women are emotionally crushed by it. She's gotten used to the feeling of a child inside her, and now she feels like she's lost a piece of herself. It can be even worse with a woman who is deeply devoted to you. Women like that often dream of marrying you and having your children, and to her the abortion feels like that dream being brutally taken away.

Third, she may change her mind at the last moment and refuse to go through with it. Her emotions are running high, and that makes her unpredictable. She may not do what she told you she would do.

So do everything possible to avoid ever needing an abortion. Treat it purely as a last resort.
