## Claude, cold, from that brief
Sex God Method is a complete guide to becoming an expert in bed. Before you start, there are a few things you need to know.

First, any time you have sex with someone, there is some chance of pregnancy and of sexually transmitted disease. Safer practices will lower that chance. They will never remove it. Use protection every single time until you are confident your partner can be trusted.

Second, this book covers practices that a lot of people find controversial, including fantasies of coercion and fantasies of rape. I endorse these as role-play and nothing else. Actual rape is always wrong.

Third, keep an open mind all the way through. I called this book Sex God Method for a reason. It is not a collection of handy tricks. It is a whole system for turning someone who is sexually inept into a "Sex God." If you are not willing to have every belief you hold about sex questioned, stop reading now.

Fourth, you must be over 18 to read this book. As a capable adult, you accept complete responsibility for whatever results from your sexual conduct.

Now, if you want to get the most out of this book, you have to genuinely commit to studying the ideas in it. That takes time. Your sexual habits are deeply ingrained, and they are not going to shift quickly. So before you turn the page, make yourself a promise: you will use this material to improve over the long term.

Here is how to do it.

Read the whole book through one time. Then take an honest look at yourself and work out where you are naturally strong and where you are naturally weak sexually, using the DEVI framework you will learn in these pages. Pick out your largest area of weakness and go back and reread the part of the book that deals with it. Then focus on putting that part into practice until you have fully learned it.

Once you have, do the same thing with your second-largest weakness. Then your third. Keep going, one weakness at a time, until you reach sexual mastery.
