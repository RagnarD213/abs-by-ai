## Claude, cold, from that brief
3. She has a high sex drive. You want a woman whose natural love of sex hasn't been damaged by social conditioning. She should enjoy sex enough to keep up with you.

4. She doesn't have any deep-seated insecurities or limiting beliefs. Every woman has some fears and doubts. That's normal, and you can gently lead her past them. However, some women have been damaged so badly by past boyfriends, by their upbringing, or by other people that it's just not worth the effort.

An example would be a woman with low self-esteem who believes she could never fully satisfy a man. Stay away from women like this, even if they're hot. Their limiting beliefs are toxic, and they will rub off on you.

A few of the women you meet won't be right for a long-term relationship, but most of them will be.

Whenever you're screening a woman for a relationship, always consider her sexual potential. If she has all four of the traits I've covered, she can become an amazing partner in bed.

The ideal situation is to have more women with good relationship potential than you can handle. Once you're at that point, choose the ones with the highest potential and let the rest go.

In my own experience, anything more than four girlfriends becomes unmanageable. It simply takes up too much time.

Everyone's preferences are different, though. Decide how much of your time you actually want to spend managing relationships, and build your group of girlfriends to fit that.

Finally, never get complacent. Just because you have a great rotation right now doesn't mean there isn't something better out there. Keep going out, keep meeting new women, and keep improving your relationship life.
