## Claude, cold, from that brief
Sex is the greatest experience a human being can have. And when you're good at it, nothing else even comes close.

If you know what you're doing, sex means intense ecstasy for both you and your partner. Most people settle for mediocre relationships. Yours could be great instead. Give a woman truly great sex and she can fall for you so hard that she'd do anything for you.

The benefits don't stop at your relationship, either. When you know you can amaze any woman in bed, your self-esteem and self-assurance go way up. And that makes you even more appealing.

But there's a flip side. If you're bad in bed, sex can be awful. It gets spoiled by insecurity and the feeling that you're just not adequate. Every letdown drains a little more of your confidence, until you end up bitter and unable to enjoy a satisfying partnership with the opposite sex.

With sex mattering this much, it's no surprise that sexual self-improvement is an industry worth millions of dollars. There are hundreds of e-books online that claim they'll turn you into a legend in bed. Sex books are mainstream now too. Walk into your local Barnes and Noble and you'll find dozens of them on the shelves.

I've read a fair number of these books. Most of them are dreadful.
