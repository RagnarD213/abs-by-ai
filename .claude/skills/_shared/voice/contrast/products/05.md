## The content, as a neutral brief
Points, in order:
- Over-planning should be avoided away from the bedroom too, not just during sex.
- Example: when you want to bring in a fantasy, do not fix its precise details three weeks ahead.
- On the scheduled day, circumstances might not suit it.
- Pushing the fantasy through when circumstances are wrong makes it less effective.
- Instruction: adapt to the moment and do not give in to the temptation to script everything in bed.
- Caveat: spontaneity can itself be overdone.
- For the sake of Variety (a named principle), occasionally arranging sexual activities with a partner beforehand is worthwhile, because it lets both people build anticipation.
- Example: tell your girlfriend that on her birthday you will give her the best sex she has ever had; she will look forward to it, and the payoff will feel better as a result.
- Overall rule: in general, being spontaneous in bed makes sexual encounters better.

The point: Don't over-plan sex or fantasies ahead of time, because rigid plans may not fit the moment; plan ahead only occasionally to create anticipation, and otherwise default to spontaneity.

Who: Addressed directly to a male reader with a female partner, as advice in a sex-advice book.

## Claude, cold, from that brief
You should also avoid planning too much outside of the bedroom. For example, if you want to introduce a fantasy, don't decide on its exact details three weeks in advance. When the day you picked finally arrives, the situation might not be right for it. And if you force a fantasy into a situation where it doesn't fit, it won't be nearly as effective as it could have been.

So adapt to the moment, and resist the temptation to script out everything you do in bed.

That said, you can take spontaneity too far. For the sake of Variety, it's worth occasionally setting up sexual activities with your partner ahead of time, because doing so lets both of you build anticipation. For example, tell your girlfriend that on her birthday, you're going to give her the best sex she's ever had. She'll look forward to it, and when the day comes, the payoff will feel that much better because of it.

But in general, being spontaneous in bed will make your sexual encounters better.
