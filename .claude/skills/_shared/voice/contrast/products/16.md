## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan, the course instructor, speaking to paying customers at the start of a lesson video on advanced remarketing.

Points, in order:
- Announces that this video covers advanced remarketing tactics, with the aim of making the viewer highly skilled, ninja-level, at remarketing.
- Recaps that earlier lessons covered basics: building a remarketing list and running a simple campaign aimed at people who visited the website within the last 30 days.
- Says doing that basic version beats doing nothing, and that some advertisers do not even do that much.
- Urges viewers not to stop there, because the advanced tactics bring much better return on investment and more scale than typical advertisers get from the basic 30-day visitor approach.
- States that in some accounts he manages, remarketing generates most of the profit.
- Clarifies that remarketing is not most of the ad budget; in a typical account the campaigns run slightly above break-even, around 10 or 15% profit.
- Says such margins are not realistic from cold traffic campaigns, which is why he concentrates so heavily on remarketing.
- Adds that remarketing will never be a large portion of ad outlay, yet may account for a large portion of profit when these techniques are well tuned.
- Promises that the video will show how to build more elaborate, multi-layered remarketing campaigns in which those margins are achievable.
- Begins a review of the three kinds of remarketing list, starting with website lists: people who visited the site, either any page at all or specific pages such as the shopping cart page.

The point: Persuade viewers that basic remarketing is only a starting point and that learning the advanced tactics in this lesson is worthwhile because remarketing can supply a disproportionate share of profit; then start the lesson by reviewing list types.

So in the earlier lessons we went over the basics, right? It's never going to be a big chunk of what you're spending on ads. But it can end up being a big chunk of your profit, if you get these techniques dialed in.
