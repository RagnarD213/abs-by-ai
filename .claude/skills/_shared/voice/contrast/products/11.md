## Claude, cold, from that brief
Many people believe that writing a good ad script is a matter of creativity and luck.

This is completely wrong.

The truth is that winning ads on YouTube are mostly the result of following tested marketing formulas. If you learn and use the same formulas that successful advertisers rely on, you can dramatically increase the profits of your campaigns.

Why Tested Formulas Beat Creativity

In this chapter, you're going to learn how to write strong scripts for your YouTube commercials. In the advertising world, these are often referred to as your ad "creative".

Here's your first lesson: despite that name, creativity plays only a small role in the process.

Don't get me wrong, creativity is useful. It can add some polish to your ads. But it is not the main thing that determines whether your ads are profitable.

What separates the people who have made millions of dollars through YouTube marketing from the penniless would-be entrepreneurs is this: the winners know the tested formulas, and they use them in their ads.

Because ads look so different from one another on the surface, a lot of people never notice that the winners are all built from just a handful of formulas. In fact, a very large percentage of them use a single "Master Formula", which I'm about to teach you in this chapter.

If you're a beginner, my advice is to script every one of your ads using the Master Formula, just to keep things simple. Later in this book, we'll cover more advanced and subtle scripting formulas you can use to improve your creative.

Ad Scripting: The Biggest Key To YouTube Advertising Success

Why should you stick with tested formulas for any ad you're paying for with your hard-earned money?

Because the marketing message in your script matters more than any other factor in determining whether your campaign succeeds.
