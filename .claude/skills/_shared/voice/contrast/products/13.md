## Claude, cold, from that brief
If you have a very long sales video, I'm talking 30 minutes or more, I recommend you also test a much shorter edit of it.

I like to do this in two stages. First, cut the video down to around 20 minutes. Then cut even harder and make a 10-minute version.

Now, you or another good marketer will need to decide exactly which sections of the script get removed. Picking the right sections to cut is crucial, and it takes real marketing expertise. But once those decisions are made, any video editor can make the cuts based on the directions you give them.

After you've tested a shorter edit, the next thing I recommend testing is a new introduction for your sales video. More people watch the opening of your video than any other part of it, so the opening tends to have the biggest effect on your results.

Here are a few intro ideas that have reliably won for my clients:

1. Media Appearance Clips

This is my most reliable winner by a wide margin. The key is to trim it down hard. Use only the strongest 30 to 60 seconds. That way you get the media credibility without losing too many viewers before your main video even starts.

2. An Introduction From Someone Else

Have another person present you and brag about your achievements before your script begins. This can lift your conversions. If you're just starting out, you can pay a performer a small fee to do this, or get one of your existing customers to do it.

3. An Intro From A Different Copywriter

Sometimes a person who didn't write your original script can more easily find a way to beat it. And you can get these short intros written at a very low cost.
