## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan, speaking to camera to viewers (mainly men) who put earning money ahead of their health and appearance.

Points, in order:
- Opens by saying he will explain why viewers should put more into their health, however little they currently have.
- Reason one is the halo effect.
- Many people badly undervalue their appearance, dismissing it as vanity and unimportant.
- Their reasoning: money comes first, so there is no time for looks; they would settle for being overweight but rich.
- He says this is backwards: if money is your top priority, your looks must be a priority too, because of the halo effect.
- Explains the effect: science has shown people judge you as smarter, more competent at work, funnier and more likeable based on appearance alone; even very smart business contacts do this without realising.
- Example: when applying for a job, looking better hugely raises your chances versus looking worse.
- This applies to men too; the effect is stronger for women but still significant for men.
- Example: a potential business partner will judge you negatively if you are overweight, sloppy or neglect your appearance, making the partnership less likely.
- Conversely, a well-kept appearance signals you are disciplined, trustworthy and get things done, making the partnership more likely.
- Begins a further example: you are in a relationship and starting to gain some weight (cuts off there).

The point: Looking after your health and appearance is not vanity but a direct lever on money and career success, because people judge your abilities by how you look, so viewers should invest in it.
