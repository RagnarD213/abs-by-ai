## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan, a guest on a 2020 podcast interview, answering the host, Johnny, and speaking to him directly at one point.

Points, in order:
- By then his fitness company, SixPackOut.com, had grown into a firm with a sizeable office. It was not quite corporate, but it was a real office setting with an HR department and larger-company rules to follow.
- For some time he believed this was necessary in order to earn money.
- He eventually saw that although he liked the financial rewards, he did not want an office-based life forever.
- A second reason: he had always wanted to appear on camera himself and build a business with himself as the on-screen expert. He felt he was the wrong face for a fitness company. He is in fairly good shape, but fitness models could do that job better and far more cheaply.
- Within that company his best role was the advertising. From the very start his aim had been to exit one day and live with more freedom.
- He would not call it a definitely good experience. It got him to his present position, and he is glad he got the exit. He now works at home and enjoys his freedom.
- He tells Johnny that Johnny should appreciate his own life: earning money, doing as he pleases, working on his own terms.
- Office work seemed not to matter to him at first, but after many years he realised he did not wish to pass his whole life like that. Those were his reasons.
- He says he cannot discuss the sale figures: the other party asked him not to reveal exact numbers, and he is honouring that as a courtesy.
- He can say a little about the process: the negotiating was extremely hard.

The point: To explain why he sold his fitness company. He wanted freedom from office life and a business where he could be the on-camera face. He also signals that he cannot share sale figures but will describe the tough negotiation.
