## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan, speaking directly to a YouTube audience in a video arguing Bitcoin is worthless.

Points, in order:
- Opens by citing a Bitcoin theft worth billions of pounds (the figure given later is £2.5 billion).
- Claim: a currency from which billions can be stolen, with the thieves probably escaping punishment, cannot be accepted as a place to keep wealth safe.
- This is likely just the start, because thefts of Bitcoin have been growing as the years go by; the next could be 5, 10 or 20 billion.
- Nobody really knows how big the theft risk is for someone holding Bitcoin. He flags this as the first takeaway.
- Second takeaway: the two thieves were aged 17 and 21. One was a minor who had not yet finished high school, and took £2.5 billion, about $3.4 billion, in Bitcoin.
- He says this should be very worrying: the pair are very young, and far more skilled financial criminals exist.
- Seeing someone that young pull off a theft this size will encourage other thieves to raise their efforts, and the more skilled ones will likely take larger sums.
- From their appearance, the two look like heavy partiers and nightclub regulars, not top-tier financial criminals.
- The truly skilled criminals are about to move into Bitcoin crime now and will probably exceed $3 billion.

The point: Bitcoin is unsafe to hold as a way of preserving wealth, because huge thefts are possible, are getting bigger, and were pulled off by very young amateurs, which means worse is coming from professionals.
