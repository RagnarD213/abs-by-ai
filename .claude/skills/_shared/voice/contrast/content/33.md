## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan, talking straight to camera in a YouTube Short, addressing a general audience of men interested in fitness.

Points, in order:
- Greets viewers and says he will list the five best abdominal exercises to focus on for a visible six-pack.
- First: ab wheel. He likes it because the abs stay under tension throughout, with no rest. It is hard even for people with a strong core, and beginners can manage by rolling out a shorter distance.
- Second: spiderman plank. He likes it because it trains the transverse abdominis, the stabilising core muscle that helps the midsection appear slim and taut. It also trains the whole upper body and is far less dull than holding a standard plank.
- Third: cable crunch. He calls it a step up from the ab wheel for people with a gym available. It is easy and low risk for novices, yet still tough for advanced lifters when form is good and the load goes up.
- Fourth: V-sit twist. He likes it because it targets the obliques, the side abdominal muscles. It has little visible effect while belly fat remains, but when someone is lean, well-developed obliques change the look a lot.
- Fifth: toe touch. He likes it because it is simple, can be done at home and is effective with proper form. Beginners need no added weight; advanced people can hold a 5 lb load or a weighted ball for extra difficulty.
- Wraps up by saying that is his list, and asks viewers to comment with their favourite and suggest other exercises men should do.

The point: Give viewers five recommended ab exercises with a reason for each and a way to scale them for beginners or advanced trainees, then prompt comments.

## Claude, cold, from that brief
What's up guys, Dan Rose here, and I'm gonna give you the five best ab exercises to focus on if you want a six-pack you can actually see.

Number one, the ab wheel. I love this one because your abs are under tension the entire time. There's no rest, no break at the top, nothing. And it's hard, even if you've already got a strong core. If you're a beginner, just don't roll out as far. Shorter distance, you'll be fine.

Number two, the spiderman plank. The reason I like this is it hits your transverse abdominis. That's the stabilizing muscle in your core, and it's what helps your midsection look slim and tight. Plus you're working your whole upper body, and honestly it's way less boring than just sitting there holding a regular plank.

Number three, the cable crunch. So if you've got access to a gym, think of this as the step up from the ab wheel. It's easy to do, it's low risk if you're just starting out, but it's still tough for advanced guys as long as your form is good and you keep adding weight.

Number four, the V-sit twist. This one goes after your obliques, the muscles on the sides of your abs. Now look, if you've still got belly fat, you're not gonna see much from this. But once you're lean, well-developed obliques make a huge difference in how your whole midsection looks.

And number five, the toe touch. I like it because it's simple, you can do it at home, and if you do it with proper form it works. If you're a beginner you don't need any weight at all. If you're more advanced, hold a five pound weight or a weighted ball and it gets a lot harder.

So that's my list, those are my top five. Drop a comment and let me know which one's your favorite, and tell me what other exercises you think guys should be doing.
