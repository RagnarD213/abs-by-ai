## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan, speaking to camera to YouTube viewers (mainly ordinary men who train), in a video about the supplements he personally uses.

Points, in order:
- He has just finished listing the things he swallows after training, and says the supplement list is far from finished.
- Next step: he mixes a drink from supplements that dissolve.
- First ingredient in that drink is Thorne's amino acid product.
- He picked Thorne because AI told him it is the most reliable supplement brand (he has said this before in the video).
- He takes aminos after training for a few reasons, but flags this as something he does not advise the typical man to copy.
- His own AI-assisted research says a protein supplement after training would actually serve him better than aminos.
- He skips it because his stomach is very sensitive: whey protein makes him lose control of his bowels, which he calls a disaster, so whey is ruled out for him regardless of its benefits.
- Most people tolerate whey fine, so they should use whey rather than aminos.

- Aminos are absorbed immediately, whereas protein takes longer to be broken down and absorbed.

The point: Explain why he personally uses Thorne aminos after workouts, while telling most viewers to take whey protein instead unless they cannot digest it or are cutting calories.
