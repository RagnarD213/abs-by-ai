- Claims jump rope beats every other form of cardio available at home, yet the majority of people use poor technique.
- Promises to take the viewer from tripping on the rope to skipping the way a boxer does, within a few minutes.

- Then increase the speed a little at a time until the alternating-foot skip is happening at full speed.

## Claude, cold, from that brief
Jump rope is the best cardio you can do at home, better than anything else, but most people are doing it with really bad form. So I'm gonna take you from tripping over the rope to skipping like a boxer in just a few minutes.

First thing, stop jumping with both feet together. What you want to do instead is step over the rope with alternating feet, one foot and then the other. And the reason is it's way less stress on your joints, and it makes it a lot easier to crank up the cardio intensity without getting caught on the rope.

Now, alternating feet is hard to do at full speed when you're just starting out. So the first time you try it, turn the rope slowly and just step over it, alternating feet. Then pick up the speed a little bit at a time until you're doing that alternating foot skip at full speed.

Once you've got it, do two minutes every morning when you wake up to get your circulation going, or two minutes before your workout as a quick cardio warm-up.

Leave a comment and let me know how learning it this way went for you.
