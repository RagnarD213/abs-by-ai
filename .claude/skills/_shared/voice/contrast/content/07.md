## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan, speaking to camera to his YouTube viewers (mostly men interested in fitness and appearance) in a video about his first spray tan.

Points, in order:
- Opens by finishing a prior thought: the aim of the preceding advice is to look passable while dressed.
- Those with spare cash should pay for a tan done by a professional.
- Poor people should not be buying professional spray tans.
- If someone cannot yet pay for the basics (meal-prep delivery, membership at a gym, gym equipment at home, GLP-1 drugs, TRT), those are the better place to spend first.
- Spend on those basics first; only after building a physique worth showing in photo shoots should the tan come next.
- People whose income leans heavily on how they look should pay for it: on-camera people, influencers, those who constantly meet clients face to face, and those in looks-driven fields such as fashion or hairdressing.
- For them the modest cost pays itself back, since looking better brings in more earnings.
- Extreme case one: wealthy, muscular and always on camera, so the tan is clearly worth it.
- Extreme case two: broke, overweight and unable to pay for a gym, so it likely is not worth it.
- Most viewers fall between these, where an occasional tan is sensible, for example for photo or video shoots or an event such as a wedding.
- That occasional use is his recommendation for the majority.

The point: A professional spray tan is worth paying for only once the fitness basics are covered and the body is in shape, or when income depends on looks; most people should get one just occasionally for shoots or big events.
