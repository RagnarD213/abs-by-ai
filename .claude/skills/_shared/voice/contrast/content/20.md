## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan, speaking to camera, addressing viewers who do their jobs from home.

Points, in order:
- Opens by promising his ten top tips for getting more done when working at home, and says anyone who works at home needs to watch.
- Before the tips, he explains how he learned this: he has worked at home for roughly one year.
- He began roughly half a year ahead of the lockdown, when everyone else was pushed into home working.
- He is thankful for that head start, since it gave him some months to work things out.
- He admits he was bad at it initially and had many days when little got accomplished.

- He acknowledges the pull of a looser schedule and agrees that flexibility is a real benefit of home working.

The point: Establish his credibility as someone who struggled and then figured out home working, promise ten shortcuts, and begin with the first one: keep fixed working hours even though you are free not to.
