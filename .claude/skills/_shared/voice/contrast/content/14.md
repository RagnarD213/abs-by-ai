## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan, speaking to camera to YouTube viewers who want visible abs, mostly men who are not yet lean.

Points, in order:
- Adding ab muscle when already lean: even one millimetre of growth changes appearance dramatically and also improves functional strength.
- For someone carrying fat, extra ab muscle does not help; it makes things worse, because muscle grown under the fat pushes the stomach further outward before the abs can show.
- Example: Chuck Liddell, from the older UFC era. He was a professional athlete with enormous core muscle, which wrestling and boxing at his level demand.
- Despite that, he appeared to have a small beer gut. The cause: he was not lean but did very large amounts of ab work constantly, so the muscle grew underneath the fat layer.
- Instruction: take Dan's word for it, research it further if wanted, but roughly 90% of viewers should skip ab exercises entirely until their abs are already visible.
- He asks how to get lean enough for abs to show, and says it depends on two things.
- Tip three: while abs are not yet visible and some belly fat remains, do vacuum exercises in place of standard ab exercises.
- Standard ab exercises (shown on screen) are things such as toe touches and crunches, movements where the abs contract, which he demonstrates.
- Vacuum exercises (also shown on screen) are entirely different: instead of crunching, the stomach is pulled inward the way a bodybuilder does when posing, which works the transverse abdominis.
- The transverse abdominis is a separate muscle, not the visible six-pack muscle most people have in mind when they say abs.

The point: Do not train abs directly until you are lean enough to see them, because building them under fat makes the belly stick out; in the meantime use stomach vacuum exercises that target the transverse abdominis.
