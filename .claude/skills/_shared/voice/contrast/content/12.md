## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan, speaking directly to YouTube viewers, some of whom belong to his Abs By AI programme.

Points, in order:
- Opens by saying the option just mentioned beats doing nothing at all.
- He has built a tool made specifically to audit a person's supplement stack and give science-backed advice on improving it.
- The advice is not generic; it is tailored to the individual's own goal.
- People who belong to Abs By AI can already use it, via the supplement audit feature, and can run their audit straight away.
- Beginners should instead use ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini: photograph their supplements, explain the context of what they are doing, then request feedback.
- Addressing viewers, he says he has taken them through his whole day of supplements, from the start of the day to the end.
- Each day on waking he begins with Athletic Greens.

The point: Get viewers to have their supplements audited, with his members' tool or, if new, a general AI chatbot, and then begin the walk through his own stack with the one product he kept despite AI advice to cut it.
