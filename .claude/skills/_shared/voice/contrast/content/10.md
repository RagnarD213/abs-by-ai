## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan, speaking to camera to YouTube viewers interested in building muscle without deadlifts.

Points, in order:
- The leg press is a gym machine: you add weight plates, set the safety stop so you cannot descend too far, lower to that stop, then push back up.
- Drive through the heels, the same as in a squat.
- It usually means paying for a gym membership to access the machine.
- He claims no other leg exercise carries as little injury risk as the leg press.
- Reminder: a couple of years of uninterrupted, injury-free leg pressing builds more muscle overall than deadlifting, which builds more at first but brings periodic injuries and setbacks.
- The last exercise he uses for a good-looking back is a fly movement targeting the rear deltoids.
- Two versions: a gym machine (shown on screen), or dumbbells, starting from a bent-over row stance and flying the arms back (also shown on screen).
- With either version, do not simply row the weight backwards; move the arms in a circular arc and contract the back.
- A cue he uses: picture a pencil sitting between the shoulder blades and squeeze it on every rep.
- Benefits: better posture, bigger rear delts, and a better-looking back with low injury risk.

The point: Swap deadlifts for lower-risk exercises such as the leg press and the rear-deltoid fly, because staying uninjured over years builds more muscle, and perform each with the described technique.
