## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan, speaking directly to camera to viewers who are in debt.

Points, in order:
- Two camps exist on repayment order: one says tackle whichever balance carries the steepest rate first; the other says start with the littlest balance.
- Going by rate is the better choice in pure arithmetic terms.
- Dan recommends starting with the littlest balance anyway, because it works better psychologically and emotionally.
- He bases this on experience: recent conversations with many indebted people, and a lot of thought about what would give them momentum to clear what they owe and eventually reach financial independence and wealth.
- His finding: falling into debt is far more an emotional and psychological process than a mathematical one.
- Example: if the balance with the steepest rate is $100,000, clearing it takes ages and you never get the dopamine reward of seeing it fully gone.
- From those conversations, targeting the littlest balance (say, somewhere in the hundreds or low thousands) brings greater benefit over time, because clearing it makes you feel positive about yourself and motivates you to clear further balances.
- Instruction: start with the littlest balance, not necessarily the one with the steepest rate; no need to work out the rates right now; just order them by size, least to greatest, and clear them all.
- Final step: becoming debt-free is mainly about building momentum, especially the positive emotional kind.
- Instruction: whenever a balance is cleared, even a minor one of a couple of hundred dollars, celebrate as a reward.

The point: Clear debts in order of size, starting with the littlest, because the emotional boost of each win keeps you going, and celebrate each one you clear.
