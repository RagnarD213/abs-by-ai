Points, in order:
- A $17 device with a gimmicky infomercial reputation is in fact among the strongest tools available for abdominal training.

- The video promises to explain why viewers should buy one and to demonstrate the correct technique for it.
- Reason one: tension is constant. On-screen footage shows him working through the whole movement, whereas a crunch has rest points at its top and bottom. Longer time under tension is better, so the wheel wins over crunches.
- Reason two: progression is built in. Beginners can benefit, using a modification he will demonstrate; very lean, heavily muscled bodybuilders can benefit too by doing a harder variation, which he will show further on.

- It also works the chest, shoulders and arms to a degree, so it is both a full-body exercise and a complete ab exercise.
- Final advantage: it is inexpensive and compact, costing $17.

The point: Convince viewers that the cheap ab wheel is a serious, superior alternative to crunches and that they should buy and use one.
