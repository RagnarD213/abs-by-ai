## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan, speaking to camera to viewers of his YouTube ab workout video, demonstrating as he talks.

Points, in order:
- Starting position for the current exercise: seated balance with knees lifted off the floor.
- Holding a medicine ball, bounce it off the floor to your left and right in turn.
- Legs swing to one side while the ball bounces there, then swing across for a bounce opposite; both bounces together count as one rep. He shows a second rep.
- For best results, do this move once toe touches are done, so the rectus abdominis is fatigued and the obliques take more of the work.
- The legs must actually shift; staying still with only the ball moving does much less.
- Leg movement across the body is what makes the exercise effective; the ball bounce only serves as a cue for that and as a counterweight against the legs.
- Exercise three is called Spider-Man Planks, and it begins from a plank.
- Plank refresher: rest on the elbows, with hips neither raised high nor sagging; the body stays in one straight line, as the name suggests.
- From the plank, rise to a push-up stance, then lower back down, keeping the body straight throughout and holding tension in the transverse abdominis.
- More advanced viewers can add a stomach vacuum movement while doing it.

The point: Teach viewers correct form for the medicine ball side-bounce move (legs must travel, do it following toe touches to target obliques) and introduce the plank-to-push-up base of Spider-Man Planks with a straight body and engaged deep core.
