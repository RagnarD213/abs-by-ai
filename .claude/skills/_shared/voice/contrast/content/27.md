## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan, a guest on a podcast interview in 2020, speaking to the host and the listeners; he runs an ad agency and previously ran a business called Sixpack.

Points, in order:
- He agrees with the host, then says that landing a copywriting job with top-tier companies, at a salary somewhere around $200k to $300k, is just the start.
- Lesson from the business-to-business model: running his ad agency has let him see inside roughly 20 to 30 Google Ads accounts belonging to top-level firms, and working with such companies teaches a great deal.
- At Sixpack he saw just his one account, so he learned more slowly.
- Seeing what other strong business owners do teaches so much that the learning is probably worth more than the pay.
- Copywriters for these businesses get feedback from clients who understand which copy performs and which fails, so their skills improve quickly.
- If they later launch an offer or an online shop of their own, that background will help them a lot.
- His book is available at the site 15stepsbook.com, free of charge apart from postage; it introduces the fundamentals of his approach.
- People wanting coaching, or wanting him to run their ads, should visit socialresponsemarketing.com, which explains his coaching offerings plus the requirements for having him handle ads; that is where to go to work directly with him.
- He thanks the host for the podcast, calling it a great resource that gathers many experts.
- He adds that he listens to the host's other show, Invest Like a Boss, which gives him access to investing experts he would otherwise never reach, and thanks him for that too.

The point: Working for high-end clients pays off more in accelerated learning than in income, and listeners who want more from Dan should get his book or go to his agency site for coaching or ad management; he closes by thanking the host.
