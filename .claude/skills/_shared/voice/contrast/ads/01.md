## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan Rose, speaking on camera to a viewer who has watched many of his videos but has not yet taken a free copy of his book.

Points, in order:
- Dan greets the viewer again and gives his name.
- His team pushed him to record this video, because the viewer is among a small group who have seen a lot of his videos without claiming the free book.
- He wants the viewer to know the book is not sold only through his own website.
- It is also on Amazon, as a physical copy or an eBook.
- He admits he doesn't see why anyone would pay $19.95 when the book can be had for nothing.
- But he accepts many people simply prefer ordering through Amazon, and Prime members get shipping at no charge.
- He introduces comments from some Amazon buyers of the book.
- He adds that readers have also posted impressive videos describing the results they got from it.
- He tells the viewer to use the link under the video to purchase on Amazon, and says he will see them there.

The point: Get viewers who haven't taken the free book offer to buy the book on Amazon instead, by presenting Amazon as a convenient alternative and backing it with reader reviews and video testimonials.

## Claude, cold, from that brief
Hey, it's Dan Rose again.

So my team actually pushed me to record this video, because you're part of a small group of people who've watched a lot of my videos but still haven't claimed your free copy of my book.

And I wanted to make sure you know something: you don't have to get the book through my website.

It's also on Amazon. You can grab a physical copy or the eBook.

But I get it. A lot of people just prefer to order through Amazon. And if you're a Prime member, you get free shipping.

So here's what some of the people who bought the book on Amazon had to say about it.

And readers have also posted some pretty impressive videos talking about the results they got from the book.

So click the link below this video to buy it on Amazon, and I'll see you there.
