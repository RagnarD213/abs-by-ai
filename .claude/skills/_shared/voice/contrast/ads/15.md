## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Read on camera by a presenter speaking for an institute, addressed to people who have arthritis.

Points, in order:
- Open with a question: among several foods shown, which has a protein capable of worsening arthritis pain?
- Promise the answer within this short video of about three minutes.
- Introduce a set of clips showing what arthritis sufferers experienced after cutting this protein from their diet.
- After the clips, remark briefly that the results are impressive and ask the viewer to agree.
- Tell viewers that to find out how those people turned things around, they should watch a free presentation newly released by the institute's doctors, by clicking the "Watch Now" button.
- Say the presentation reveals more than the name of the protein: the doctors also go through the research that proves the need to cut it out, explained simply enough for anybody to follow.
- Say the doctors will also identify the specific foods to stay away from, since those are the ones carrying the inflammation-causing protein.
- Add that they will spell out what to eat in place of those foods to keep bodily inflammation as low as possible.
- State that the presentation is free only temporarily, and press the viewer to watch immediately while it remains up.
- Tell them not to put it off, and to click "Watch Now" to seize the chance and hear from the doctors first-hand.

The point: Get arthritis sufferers to click "Watch Now" right away and view the institute's free, time-limited doctors' presentation about a pain-worsening food protein, what foods to drop and what to eat instead.
