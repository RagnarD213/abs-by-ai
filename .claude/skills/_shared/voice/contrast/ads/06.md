## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan Rose speaking on camera, in a YouTube ad, to viewers who want to start a business but have no money.

Points, in order:
- He introduces himself as Dan Rose, an entrepreneur whose specialty is building profitable businesses that need no upfront money.
- Example: his present business began with $0. Profit in month one was $11,344, it rose each month after, and last month's profit was $89,341, earned at home with no staff.
- Promise: the video will explain how he did it and how the viewer can launch a $0 business that is also highly profitable in its opening month.
- First lesson: picking the correct type of business is essential. Plenty of businesses work with $1 million or $100,000 to put in, but those models suit a broke person poorly.
- So immediately rule out any business carrying certain costs. Only the fourth item of his list appears here: employees. Payroll is impossible before cash is banked, so anything needing staff is out.
- Conclusion: the majority of business models are unsuitable for someone with $0.
- Good news: one strong model lets you earn quickly without spending anything, and he will now explain it.
- The idea: with nothing to invest, you draw on other businesses' money. The best $0 business is therefore one that sells services to fellow companies.
- A company will happily pay monthly fees of $5,000, then $10,000, and as much as $20,000 for something valuable. With the right know-how, winning clients costs almost nothing. There is no wage bill, no office cost, no ad spend, nor any of the usual overheads owners fret about.
- He ends by voicing the viewer's likely doubt: what could I do at home that a business would value at $10,000 monthly?

The point: Persuade a viewer with no money that the only sensible business to start is a home-based service sold to other businesses, and keep them watching to learn what that service is.

## Claude, cold, from that brief
My name is Dan Rose. I'm an entrepreneur, and my specialty is building profitable businesses that don't require any money up front.

For example, I started my current business with $0. In the first month, I made $11,344 in profit. It's gone up every single month since then, and last month I made $89,341 in profit, working from home, with no employees.

In this video I'm going to explain exactly how I did it, and how you can start your own $0 business that's also highly profitable in its very first month.

So the first thing you need to understand is that choosing the right type of business is critical. There are a lot of businesses that work great if you've got a million dollars to invest, or even $100,000 to invest. But those business models are a terrible fit for you if you're broke.

So right away, you want to cross off any business that comes with certain costs.

And then there's employees. You can't make payroll before you've got money in the bank. So if a business needs staff, it's out.

What that means is most business models just aren't a good fit for you if you're starting with $0.

But here's the good news. There is one great business model that lets you make money fast without spending anything. And I'm going to explain it to you right now.

Here's the idea. When you have nothing to invest, you tap into other businesses' money. So the best $0 business you can start is one that sells services to other companies.

Think about it. A company will happily pay you $5,000 a month, then $10,000 a month, and even up to $20,000 a month, if you're giving them something valuable. And if you know what you're doing, getting those clients costs you almost nothing. You've got no payroll. No office rent. No advertising budget. None of the usual overhead that business owners lie awake worrying about.

Now, I know what you're probably thinking. "Dan, that sounds great, but what could I possibly do from home that a business would value at $10,000 a month?"
