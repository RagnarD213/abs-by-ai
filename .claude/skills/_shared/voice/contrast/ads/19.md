## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Viewers of a YouTube ad for Physio Tru, read on camera by a presenter who is not the writer; aimed at Americans who eat fish for the sake of their hearts.

Points, in order:
- Wild-caught fish vary in quality, because species differ in diet.
- The healthy kind feed on plankton, sea plants and shrimp. That diet gives them a strong Omega-3 content, which can do a great deal for the heart.
- Other wild-caught species are scavengers that comb the seabed, feeding on dead fish and rotten fish eggs. They also eat fish excrement that sinks down there.
- A worse problem than the carcasses and excrement is garbage. People throw huge quantities of trash into the sea, where it either sinks or floats, and scavengers readily eat this poisonous waste.
- Looking at diet makes it easy to see where the toxicity comes from. Fish living on plankton, plants and shrimp are healthy to eat. Scavengers living on trash, excrement and carcasses are toxic and are worse for the heart than pizza.
- The viewer therefore has to learn the four toxic, trash-eating scavenger species in order to avoid them. The way to do that is the "Take Quiz" button below.
- The presenter personally guarantees the viewer will be shocked by the four names.
- One of the four is very low in fat and has been promoted for years as good for the heart, but recent research says it is seriously harming American hearts.
- Another is the wild-caught species Americans eat most. The viewer probably has it several times weekly, and each bite does lasting harm to the heart.
- Repeat the call to action: find out the four to avoid, improve heart health, and press "Take Quiz" straight away to begin.

The point: Explain that a fish's diet determines whether it is healthy or toxic, so that the viewer becomes curious and worried enough to press the quiz button and learn the four species to avoid.
