## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan, speaking on camera in his own YouTube ad, to viewers who want to start a business with no money.

Points, in order:
- Companies cannot survive without customers, and the majority of them do not grasp how advertising online functions.
- If you learn to bring them customers through ad campaigns, they will gladly pay you a share of the revenue those customers generate.
- The speaker says this model is paying off well for him personally, and he is not alone: thousands of entrepreneurs worldwide have reached financial freedom with it while putting hardly any personal cash at risk at the start.
- Many kinds of online ad campaign can be managed on behalf of companies, and he has tried every kind at some stage.
- He chose to specialise in a single type because it earns him much more than the others.
- That type is the one the viewer is watching at this moment: YouTube ads.
- His results managing clients' YouTube campaigns have beaten what he got from Instagram, Facebook or Google search campaigns.
- He gives a single reason: advertiser competition on YouTube is much lower.
- Search, Instagram and Facebook are crowded with well-funded, very capable advertisers you would be up against.
- YouTube has competition too, but most advertisers there run their campaigns badly, which leaves an opening for the viewer to outperform them.

The point: Getting paid a revenue share for sending companies customers through ads is a low-risk business model, and YouTube ads are the best place to do it because the competition there is weak.

## Claude, cold, from that brief
See, every company on the planet needs customers to survive. Without customers, they're dead. And the truth is, most of them have no clue how online advertising actually works.

So if you can learn how to run ad campaigns that bring them customers, they will happily pay you a percentage of the revenue those customers bring in.

This business model is paying off really well for me personally. And I'm not the only one. There are thousands of entrepreneurs all over the world who've used it to reach financial freedom, while risking almost none of their own money to get started.

Now, there are a lot of different kinds of online ad campaigns you can manage for companies. And at one point or another, I've tried all of them.

But I decided to specialize in just one type, because it makes me way more money than any of the others.

And it's the exact type of ad you're watching right now. YouTube ads.

I've gotten better results managing YouTube campaigns for my clients than I ever got with Facebook, Instagram, or Google search campaigns.

And it comes down to one reason: there's way less advertiser competition on YouTube.

Facebook, Instagram, and search are packed with highly skilled advertisers with deep pockets. And those are the people you'd be going up against.

YouTube has competition too. But most of the advertisers on here are running their campaigns badly, which leaves the door wide open for you to come in and beat them.
