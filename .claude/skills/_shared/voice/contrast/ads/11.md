## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan, speaking on camera in a YouTube ad, addressing viewers who want to lose stomach fat and get a six-pack but struggle with motivation.

Points, in order:
- Opens by showing an image and crediting it for his abs, then reveals the image is not a real photo.
- He generated the image at a time when he weighed 200 pounds.
- He set it as his phone's lock screen and saw it daily for over a year.
- He then shows what his body looks like now.
- Promise for this episode: he will explain how he gained unlimited drive to exercise, eat well and do whatever was required to shed his stomach fat and get a six-pack, and he will show viewers how to make their own goal image of themselves with abs at no cost.
- The viewer already knows that regular exercise and clean eating would remove their belly fat; the hard part is finding the drive when life is hectic and stressful.
- Visualising a goal is a strong motivator and also a way of drawing the desired body into one's life.
- For that reason, lots of bodybuilders and people who model fitness wear would, in the past, have their faces photoshopped onto the physique they dreamed of.
- AI has now changed things: anyone can produce an image of themselves having the muscular physique and six-pack they have long wanted.
- Seeing yourself with abs would make you realise how great you would look without the fat on your stomach and how much your life would improve, and would leave you full of drive to actually achieve that body.

The point: Get viewers to keep watching by claiming an AI-generated image of yourself with abs, looked at daily, supplies the motivation needed to lose belly fat, and by promising to show how to make one for free.
