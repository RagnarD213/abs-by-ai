## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Read on camera by a presenter, Dr. Brian Paris, speaking to people who have arthritis.

Points, in order:
- The presenter gives his name, Dr. Brian Paris, and says he has an important question for arthritis sufferers.
- He asks which of several foods shown on screen has a protein capable of worsening arthritis pain.
- He promises the answer within this short video, about three minutes long.
- He introduces on-screen results from arthritis sufferers who cut this protein out of their diet, then remarks that the results are impressive.
- To find out how those people turned things around, viewers should watch a no-cost presentation newly posted on his website, by clicking the "Watch Now" button.
- He built the presentation himself, together with four more doctors and specialists in arthritis. It lays out the natural, science-backed method for tackling arthritis pain that the people shown all used.
- The presentation goes beyond naming the protein to cut out: he and his research team go through the studies proving it must be avoided, in plain language everyone can follow.
- It identifies the specific foods to steer clear of because they carry this inflammatory protein, and specifies what to eat in their place to keep bodily inflammation low.
- The free access is temporary, so he urges viewers to watch immediately, not put it off, and click "Watch Now" to find out the protein that aggravates their arthritis pain.

The point: Get arthritis sufferers to click "Watch Now" right away and view the free presentation, by teasing an unnamed pain-worsening protein in food and promising the answer, the evidence and a diet plan.
