## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Addressed to people past age 65 who suffer from arthritis; read on camera by a presenter who speaks as one of that age group (says 'those of us').

Points, in order:
- Opens by telling arthritis sufferers older than 65 that they should view this brief clip, about two minutes long, immediately.
- Claims a group of leading arthritis doctors and researchers has identified five foods arthritis sufferers must never eat, and that these are particularly damaging for people past 65.
- First call to action: click the "Learn More" button to find out what the five foods are.
- Presenter personally guarantees the viewer will be surprised, since these foods are generally thought of as healthy; says studies show that for older arthritis sufferers they can raise pain and inflammation and cause other bodily problems, and that cutting out these five supposed health foods brings an immediate, noticeable improvement.
- Example: one of them is a vegetable that every senior with arthritis should avoid because it worsens symptoms, for a specific reason that does not affect younger people.
- Asks whether the viewer knows which vegetable; reassures that most seniors do not. Second call to action: click "Learn More" below to find out which one, plus the other foods.
- Says the linked presentation has the doctors reveal five 'superfoods' for arthritis that they advise every patient past 65 to eat, explain the science of why these help, and show easy, tasty ways to start eating them.
- Adds that viewers will learn why pain medication may be worsening some arthritis problems, and will be shown three small lifestyle changes the doctors advise all their 65-plus patients to make to treat symptoms naturally.
- Finally, viewers will hear from real seniors with arthritis who are active and pain-free again after following the advice; these people are living fully at 70-plus, 80-plus and older, and their stories will inspire the viewer to make the recommended diet changes.
- Closes with urgency: the presentation will not stay online long, so watch while it is available; third call to action to click "Learn More" to see the presentation and learn the five foods to avoid.

The point: Get arthritis sufferers past 65 to click "Learn More" and watch a longer presentation, by teasing five supposedly healthy foods they should never eat and promising further diet, medication and lifestyle advice plus testimonials.
