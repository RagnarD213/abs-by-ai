## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan, the author of the book, speaking on camera to YouTube viewers who want to start an internet business and have not yet read his book.

Points, in order:
- He says he is certain his book (title: the 15 Steps) is what a viewer requires for creating a web-based business, and that many thousands of readers have shared their results on the internet agreeing with that.
- He acknowledges he understands why the viewer may be hesitating.
- He says it is not low-quality, ghostwritten padding. It lays out an entire plan for earning income through YouTube advertising, and covering the whole system meant it ended up fairly lengthy.
- The positive news: there is now an audiobook edition on Audible.
- He hired a leading voiceover artist from Los Angeles to narrate it, and that narrator did excellent work. Viewers will enjoy it and it makes the 15 Steps simpler to absorb than before.
- Examples of when to listen: while driving, while working out at a gym, or while doing household chores. No additional time is required, because listening happens alongside tasks the viewer already has to do.
- A single line introduces short testimonials from several listeners about the audio edition (the testimonials themselves are not included).
- He tells the viewer to quit delaying and quit excusing themselves, to quit spending their life on worthless podcasts or YouTube clips that lead nowhere, and to use those hours on a book with the potential to be life-changing.
- Call to action: press the "Listen Now" button to get the Audible edition, and begin right away.

The point: Remove the objection that the book is too long to read by offering it as an audiobook that fits into existing routines, and get the viewer to click through and buy it on Audible immediately.
