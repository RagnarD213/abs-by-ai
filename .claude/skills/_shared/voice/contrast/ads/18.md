## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Read on camera by presenter Jason Hanson, addressing American viewers (implicitly men with wives, children and grandchildren) worried about crime.

Points, in order:
- Open with a question: how would the viewer react if a deranged criminal pointed a firearm at their head?
- Say that someone unprepared could do nothing but freeze in terror while their spouse, kids and grandkids are harmed.
- Promise that a short time spent on this video will teach a technique used by Navy SEALs that takes down a criminal within ten seconds.
- Presenter introduces himself: Jason Hanson, ex-CIA officer, who now teaches Americans to protect themselves against violent offenders.
- Claim the US is currently in a terrible surge of killings and violence; many towns have defunded police, so they can no longer be relied on for rescue; thugs riot, loot and torch buildings without consequence; victims appear on the nightly news.
- Warn that without knowing how to fight off a violent attack, the viewer and their loved ones may be next.
- Ask how to stop criminals from attacking one's family.
- Answer that it is simple: get trained by the top-level instructor who taught Navy SEALs unarmed close-quarters fighting.
- Name him: Paul Vunak.
- Say the SEALs went to him to learn his trademark move, the RAT Technique.
- Say the RAT Technique is known as the best self-protection move there is, because it can take out an attacker within ten seconds, even when the attacker is larger, strikes by surprise, or carries a weapon.

The point: Make the viewer feel personally at risk from violent crime and unable to rely on police, so they keep watching to learn Paul Vunak's RAT Technique as the solution.
