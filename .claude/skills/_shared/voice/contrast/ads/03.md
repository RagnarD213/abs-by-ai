## The content, as a neutral brief
Who: Dan Rose, speaking on camera to a viewer who has seen his videos and visited his site but has not yet ordered his free book.

Points, in order:
- He introduces himself as Dan Rose and tells the viewer that he and his team have been discussing them.
- The team believes the viewer could earn a lot from YouTube marketing; their stats show the viewer has viewed several of their videos and looked at the website, yet has not claimed the free book.
- Cost cannot be the obstacle: the book itself costs nothing and only a small postage fee applies. If the viewer simply forgot to finish ordering, they should return and claim it now.
- Perhaps the book's length puts them off; that is no problem, because an audiobook edition exists that they can hear instead.
- Or perhaps they doubt their own ability to do well at YouTube ads; if so, he says they are completely mistaken.
- He knows this because he had the same inner doubt when launching his first business: he feared failing for lack of money, lack of know-how, lack of connections, and many other reasons.
- He says that doubting inner voice is misleading them; they are capable of earning online via YouTube ads.
- All they need is one small initial step toward that goal: getting the free book.

The point: Remove each likely objection (cost, book length, self-doubt) and push the hesitant viewer to go back and order the free book now.

## Claude, cold, from that brief
Hey, it's Dan Rose here, and I've got to tell you something. My team and I have been talking about you.

Seriously. We think you could make a lot of money with YouTube marketing. And here's the thing: we can see in our stats that you've watched a bunch of our videos. You've checked out our website. But for some reason, you still haven't grabbed your free copy of my book.

And I'm trying to figure out why.

It can't be the money. The book is free. All you do is cover a small postage fee, and that's it. So if you just got busy and forgot to finish your order, no big deal. Go back right now and claim your copy.

Or maybe you looked at it and thought, "Man, that's a long book. I'm never going to read all that." No problem. There's an audiobook version too, so you can just listen to it instead.

Or maybe it's something else. Maybe you don't really believe that you could be good at YouTube ads.

And if that's what's going on, I've got to tell you: you are dead wrong.

How do I know? Because I had that exact same voice in my head when I started my first business. I was scared I was going to fail. I didn't have enough money. I didn't know what I was doing. I didn't have the right connections. And I had about a hundred other reasons why it wasn't going to work for me.

That voice was lying to me, and it's lying to you right now. You can make money online with YouTube ads.

All you need is to take one small first step toward it. And that step is getting your free book.
