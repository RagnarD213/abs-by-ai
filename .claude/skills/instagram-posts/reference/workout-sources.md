# Steal My Workout: where Dan states each routine (batch 1, workout-02 to workout-11)

Every exercise, interval, set and rep on these posts comes from Dan's own words. `ROLL/` = the 10/15 shoot folder
`/Volumes/Extreme/10:15 dan rose fitness shoot - green screen, outdoor shorts, outdoor b roll, studio backdrop/`;
each `C####.roll.md` holds the full Whisper transcript of the roll as one paragraph on **line 18**. `SPA/` =
`sixpackabs/articles/` (articles written from the published video's own words; front matter `id` = YouTube id).
`ZCV/` = `Zeeshan Content Videos/` (the published final and its SRT).

General rules used across posts:
- Gym rep scheme (one warm-up set of 12 to 15, then two working sets of 6 to 10): `ROLL/C1776.roll.md` line 18
  ("I start with 1 light warm up set of 12 to 15 reps, and then I move into 2 working sets of 6 to 10 reps") and
  `Docs/memory/dan-personal-facts-for-scripts.md` line 21.
- Home workouts on a timer, Gymboss: memory line 21; `ROLL/C1777.roll.md` line 18 ("I use a gym boss for every home
  workout I do").
- "Spiderman plank" means, in Dan's words, forearm plank up to a push-up position and back down
  (`SPA/KThvfTo4xMo.md` lines 13 to 16; `SPA/Sv5wZha_a8c.md` lines 53 to 55; `ROLL/C1765.roll.md` line 18,
  "Get in a plank and then go to a push-up position on one side, then the other"). The stills show that move.
