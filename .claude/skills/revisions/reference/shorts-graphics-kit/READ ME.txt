SOFT BLUE LIGHT GRAPHICS FOR SHORT FORM VIDEOS

These replace the title bar and the chips in every short form video. This is the locked graphic style from now on.

HOW TO USE THEM
1. Each video has its own folder. Every .mov is 1080x1920, 29.97 fps, with a transparent background (Apple Animation codec with alpha).
2. Put each .mov on the top video track, lined up with the top left corner of the frame, at 100% scale. Do not move or resize it. It is already placed above my head.
3. The file name gives the time it starts in the cut you sent me. Example: g04_0m10_3s_keypoint.mov starts at 0:10.3. Each file already runs the right length, with its own animation in and out.
4. Take your own title bar and chips off. Captions stay.
5. The "stills" folder has a PNG of each graphic, in case you need to hold one longer.
6. The "0 Labels" folder has the two labels:
   label_AI-GENERATED: on every AI picture and AI clip, for the whole time it is on screen.
   label_REAL-PICTURE: on real photo shoot pictures of me.
   Use the "top" version when no top graphic is on screen, and the "below-top-graphic" version when one is. If a label lands on a face or on abs, use the "chip-only" PNG and move it to a clear corner.
7. If a start time moves after your changes, keep each graphic on the same words.
