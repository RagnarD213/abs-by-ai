Handheld phone video, warm flat light in a casual restaurant at lunchtime, the camera stays where it is with only slight handheld shake. One continuous shot at natural speed. The seated man closes the big dark menu, lowers it and lays it open flat on the table in front of him. With his left hand he picks up his own phone from the table, the only phone in the scene, moves it to his right hand and looks at its screen. Then he points his left index finger at one line on the open menu, relaxed and decided. The waiter stays standing in the same spot beside the table the whole time, holding his small notepad in one hand and a pen in the other; he writes on the notepad and nods. The waiter's hands never leave the notepad and never reach toward the table. Diners in the background stay where they are.
