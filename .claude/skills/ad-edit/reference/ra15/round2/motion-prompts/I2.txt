Handheld phone video, flat daylight in a plain home kitchen, slight camera shake, the camera stays where it is. The man pulls the tall white refrigerator door open wide with his right hand and steps half a pace toward it. The inside of the fridge is already packed, shelf after shelf, with identical clear meal-prep containers of plain grilled chicken and broccoli; the containers sit still on their shelves the whole time. He looks in, his shoulders drop, and his face goes flat and defeated. Natural speed, one continuous shot.
