Handheld phone video, one continuous shot, the camera stays still. An ordinary man sits on a grey couch in a living room in flat overcast daylight. He holds a black phone up in his right hand toward the camera, screen facing the lens. The phone screen shows a still photograph, frozen like a printed picture: the man in the photo never moves, never changes pose and never changes expression. He moves the phone a little closer to the camera and tilts it slightly toward the lens, then holds it steady and settles into a pleased half smile, looking straight into the camera. His mouth stays closed; he does not talk. His left hand rests on his knee and stays there. Exactly one person. Nothing appears or disappears. Ordinary home video realism.
