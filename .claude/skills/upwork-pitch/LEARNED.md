## Rules for proposals

- Approved proposal format (Dan, 2026-11-01, batch upwork-1): job-specific opening with details only from the post; one $5 trial (Upwork minimum) inside a full-price bid, steps adding up to the bid; why $5 (new to Upwork, first ten clients only); who Dan is; a 15 minute Upwork video call; "Here's what you asked for" answering each item in the post; then "Here's a summary of my accomplishments and why this trial ad is worth your time", most relevant proof first. Approved examples: `upw-2107180952645930829`, `upw-2114834950612066141` on the pipeline page.
- Job with a required first word: use it alone, as "Funnel. I'm confident..." (Dan, 2026-11-01). Timelines run from "after we've filmed it or I have your footage". Unknown spend: quote views only.
- Proofread every proposal before Dan sees it, starting with the first line: read it as the client would. A word a client requires (such as a set first word) must sit inside a natural sentence, never alone, and is flagged to Dan with the line from the post. (upwork-1, 2026-11-01)
- A $5 first step is only a suggestion in the proposal: Upwork says clients set the milestones when they send the offer. Check the offer shows $5 first before Dan accepts it. (upwork-1, 2026-11-01)
- Upwork proposals use Dan's outreach template (`TEMPLATE-outreach-email.md`), lightly customized per client (Dan, 2026-10-30, batch upwork-1). Keep his wording, keep any screening answers and the price in a block at the end.
- On Upwork, swap Calendly and "respond to this email" for a reply and an Upwork video call. Contact outside Upwork before a contract is against its Terms of Service. No mailing address footer.

## Rules for outreach emails

- Never open by faulting the company's ads. Say something true and complimentary first, then frame an opportunity to improve. A teardown reads as an insult from a stranger. (outreach-1, 2026-10-30)
- Copy alone will not sell. Dan: "I don't think people are just going to pay for copy only." Lead with video the prospect can watch. (outreach-1, 2026-10-30)
- Review-derived lines are not automatically hooks. They read like testimonials. Hooks must clear Dan's bar before they go on a page. (outreach-1, 2026-10-30)
- Plan what is being sent and get Dan's agreement on one example before drafting a batch. Dan: "Let's plan out what we should be sending before we just start writing up these drafts." (outreach-1, 2026-10-30)
- Assume the inbox is flooded: "There are just so many of these emails that go out." A templated email with swapped-in details is not enough. (outreach-1, 2026-10-30)

## Rules for picking jobs and prospects

- Never judge a brand's ads from Meta Ad Library captions. The same caption can sit on 50 different videos. Download or watch the videos and transcribe the openings first. Built With Science: 52 ads shared one caption, all different videos. (2026-10-30)
- "Started running" dates are not how long a creative has lived: brands relaunch winners in new ads. The video link's `asset_age_days` shows the file's real age. Old files kept in rotation are the likely winners, and two with the same body and different openings show a hook test. (2026-10-30)
