A single continuous comedy shot in a bright office in the morning, filmed on a locked-off tripod camera. No camera movement, no zoom, no cuts. The shot begins at natural speed: the cheerful man in the light blue shirt takes a big happy bite of a pink frosted donut and chews, wide awake. Then time speeds up into a fast time-lapse for a moment: his hands blur as he grabs donut after donut and the muffins out of the bakery box and eats them, the box empties down to crumbs, and the minute hand of the wall clock sweeps once clockwise around the dial from nine o'clock to ten o'clock. Then time returns to natural speed: his chewing slows, his eyelids droop, he yawns, and his head sinks down onto his keyboard until he is fast asleep face down on it, one cheek squashed against the keys, mouth open, one arm hanging off the desk with a half-eaten muffin still in his hand. The room, the plants and the white mug do not change. He has exactly two arms and five fingers on each hand.
