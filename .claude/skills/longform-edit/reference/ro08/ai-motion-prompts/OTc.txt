Time-lapse footage of a home office, shot on a locked-off tripod camera: two hours pass in four seconds. No camera movement, no zoom, no cuts. The chair at the desk is empty at first. The hands of the wall clock sweep steadily forward, clockwise, from twelve o'clock to two o'clock. The lean, muscular man in the navy polo shirt walks back into the frame, sits down in the chair and works at the laptop in fast, jittery time-lapse motion, pouring the last of the black coffee from the glass pot into his white mug, so the pot is almost empty. The light turns to warm afternoon light coming from the right. At the end he pushes the laptop off to the side, half closed, sets a large glass bowl of fresh colourful salad with chicken on the desk in front of him and picks up a fork, looking down at the salad with a happy, relieved smile. The clock hands only ever move clockwise.
