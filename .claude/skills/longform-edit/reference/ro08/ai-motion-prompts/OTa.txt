Time-lapse footage of a home office, shot on a locked-off tripod camera: two hours pass in four seconds. No camera movement, no zoom, no cuts. The lean, muscular man in the navy polo shirt works at his laptop in fast, jittery time-lapse motion: typing quickly, glancing at the screen, taking quick sips of black coffee from the white mug. The hands of the wall clock sweep steadily forward, clockwise, from eight o'clock to ten o'clock. The morning sunlight from the window on the left grows brighter and climbs higher. At the end he picks up the glass coffee pot with his right hand and pours black coffee into his mug; the coffee in the pot has gone down to about two thirds full. The clock hands only ever move clockwise. Nothing else in the room changes.
