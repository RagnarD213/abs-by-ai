Time-lapse footage of a home office, shot on a locked-off tripod camera: two hours pass in four seconds. No camera movement, no zoom, no cuts. The lean, muscular man in the navy polo shirt sets the glass coffee pot down and works at his laptop in fast, jittery time-lapse motion, typing quickly and sipping black coffee from the white mug. The hands of the wall clock sweep steadily forward, clockwise, from ten o'clock to twelve o'clock. The coffee in the glass pot goes down to about one third full. The sunlight changes from morning sun to bright, flat midday light. Near the end he pushes his chair back, stands up and walks out of the frame, leaving the chair empty and the laptop open on the desk. The clock hands only ever move clockwise. Nothing else in the room changes.
