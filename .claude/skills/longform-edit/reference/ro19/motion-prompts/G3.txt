A single continuous comedy shot on a sunny backyard pool deck, filmed on a locked-off tripod camera. No camera movement, no zoom, no cuts. A deeply tanned man in navy swim shorts stands facing the camera, grinning proudly, giving a thumbs up with his right hand and holding up a brown tanning mitt on his left hand like a trophy. He holds the proud pose for a moment, then turns around in one smooth step, away from the camera, and starts to walk toward the pool, glancing happily back over his shoulder, still pleased with himself. As he turns, his back comes into view: his shoulders, arms and lower back are the same deep golden tan, but in the middle of his back, between the shoulder blades, is one big stark pale untanned patch with streaky brown fingertip smears around its edge, the spot his mitt could not reach. The pale patch is already there when his back first shows and it does not change or fade in. Funny and harmless, real-time speed. He has exactly two arms and five fingers on each hand. Nobody else is in the shot. The pool water keeps rippling gently.
