Static locked-off camera, no camera movement, no zoom. Real footage on a bright kitchen counter: an old brass balance scale with a double cheeseburger on the left pan and an empty right pan. A hand holds a plain, perfectly clear glass jug of trail mix (cashews, almonds, dark raisins and white yogurt-covered raisins) above the right pan. The jug is completely plain clear glass with no markings, no printing, no lines and no numbers at any moment. The hand tips the jug and pours all the trail mix onto the right pan, where it piles up. Only after the trail mix has landed, the right pan sinks and the left pan with the burger rises as the beam tips smoothly and settles. The burger stays on its pan. The hand brings the empty jug back up.
