Locked-off camera on a tripod: the camera does not move, pan or zoom. Photorealistic, natural real-time motion, like a shot from a TV commercial. Nothing appears or disappears by itself. No text, no captions, no logos.
Evening in a cosy living room lit by the blue glow of a TV off screen. A heavy-set man in a grey hoodie sits slumped deep in the sofa, eyes fixed on the TV, completely absorbed. His right hand is down inside the open bag of trail mix on his lap; he rummages, takes out a white yogurt raisin and a few nuts between his fingers, and without looking away from the TV brings them up and pops them into his mouth, chewing. The bag stays on his lap and the trail mix inside it stays the same. His left hand rests on the sofa.
