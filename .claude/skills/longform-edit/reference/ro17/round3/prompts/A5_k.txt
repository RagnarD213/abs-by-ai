Static locked-off camera, no camera movement, no zoom. Real footage, close-up over a sunny kitchen counter, a big glass bowl of green grapes on the left that never changes. A right hand holds a small steel measuring cup of dark brown raisins, tipped over an open left palm. The hand holding the cup does not move. Dark, wrinkled, solid raisins slide out of the cup's lip one or two at a time and drop straight down into the open palm, where they pile up; the cup slowly empties as the pile grows by exactly the raisins that fell. Every raisin stays a dark brown wrinkled raisin the whole time, the same size and colour in the cup, in the air and in the palm. Raisins that are already in the palm stay where they are.
