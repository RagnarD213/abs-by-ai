Locked-off camera on a tripod: the camera does not move, pan or zoom. Photorealistic, natural real-time motion, like a frame-accurate shot from a TV commercial. Nothing appears or disappears by itself; every object that moves is moved by a hand. No text, no captions, no logos.
A food-packing room. A worker in a white coat and hairnet holds a metal scoop of colourful candy-coated chocolates tipped over a glass jar of nuts and raisins with a green leaf sticker on it; his other hand steadies the jar. The candy pours from the scoop into the jar in a short stream and settles as a layer on top of the nuts. The scoop stays the same size in the same hand, ending empty just above the jar. The sticker and the jar do not change. Then he lifts his head, looks into the camera, grins and gives a sly wink.
