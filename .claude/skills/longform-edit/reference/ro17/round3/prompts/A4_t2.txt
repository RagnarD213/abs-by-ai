A food-packing room. A worker in a white coat and hairnet holds a long metal scoop full of colourful candy-coated chocolates, tipped over a glass jar of nuts and raisins with a green leaf sticker; his other gloved hand steadies the jar. The candy pours out of the scoop in a short stream and settles as a layer on top of the nuts. He keeps holding the SAME long metal scoop in his right hand, empty, just above the jar the whole time: he never puts it down and it never changes shape. As soon as the pour ends he lifts his head, looks straight into the camera with a big sly grin and gives a quick wink. The jar and its sticker never change.
