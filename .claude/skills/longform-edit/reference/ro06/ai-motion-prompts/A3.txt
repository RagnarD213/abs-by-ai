A single continuous slapstick comedy shot in a garage home gym, filmed on a locked-off tripod camera. No camera movement, no zoom, no cuts. The heavyset man in the grey t-shirt whines with his eyes squeezed shut and his palms turned up. Almost at once the muscular trainer in the black tank top whips his raised left hand forward in one fast, hard, open-palm slap that lands flat on the heavyset man's cheek. The hand does not stop: it carries straight on past his face and sweeps down across the trainer's own body toward the camera. The heavyset man's head snaps round toward the camera from the hit, his cheek and jowls ripple, his hair flies, his hands jerk up and he rocks back on his heels. Real-time speed, a real fast slap with motion blur on the hand, realistic physics. No blood, no injury. The trainer's right arm stays down by his side. Each man has exactly two arms and five fingers on each hand.
