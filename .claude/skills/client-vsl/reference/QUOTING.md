## Using it for the next quote
- With this skill's kit and gates, plan **about 2 hours of wall time per 5 minutes of new film** for the first full
  build (Joint Vigor measured about 1.8 h), plus **one-time setup of about 8 h** per new client (key calibration, set,
  opening, pages), plus **two review rounds of about 6 h each** if the gates in GATES.md run before the first review.
  Joint Vigor's 30 h of review rounds came mostly from defect classes those gates now catch.
- Credits: about 25 Flow credits per minute of finished film at Joint Vigor's AI density (mostly Veo takes for story
  scenes and testimonials). Stills and stock cost nothing.
- Add a day of padding to any promised date; tell Dan the same day a block's measured time puts the date at risk.
- The cut version, each upsell version and the vertical are extra renders on the same edit; quote them once the full
  film's actual hours are known (not yet measured on Joint Vigor).
