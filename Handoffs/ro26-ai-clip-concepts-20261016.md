## Working Out

- **W1 — Beat the Snooze Button:** An alarm rings; a man reaches past it for his shoes and starts a five-minute workout.
- **W2 — Five-Minute Staircase:** Small exercise blocks become a staircase marked 5, 10 and 15 minutes as a man climbs.
- **W3 — Same Time Tomorrow:** The same man starts his workout as seven different sunrises pass behind him.
- **W4 — The First Step:** A dusty treadmill wakes up as a hesitant walker finds a steady rhythm.
- **W5 — The Couch Lets Go:** A couch comically tries to hold a man back; he breaks free and picks up his dumbbells.
- **W6 — A Lifelong Habit:** A teenage football player grips his helmet; a match cut reveals the adult gripping a dumbbell.
- **W7 — Water the Habit:** A man waters a tiny plant beside his weights each morning until it grows tall.
- **W8 — Start Again Today:** A man closes a notebook of abandoned workout plans, opens a fresh page and does his first rep.
- **W9 — Five Minutes Anywhere:** An ordinary living room becomes a compact workout space as a man starts a five-minute circuit.
- **W10 — Light the Path:** One small step lights up a long running track, and a man starts moving along it.

Suggested starting choice: W5.

## Martial Arts

- **M1 — White Belt to Black Belt:** Hands tie a white belt; matching shots across years of training end with those hands tying a black belt.
- **M2 — Tap, Learn, Repeat:** A controlled jiu-jitsu drill ends in a tap, a fist bump and a calm reset.
- **M3 — Calm in the Meeting:** During a tense meeting, a man recalls his training, takes one breath and responds calmly.
- **M4 — Stop Fighting the Knot:** A man struggles against tangled ropes, slows down, finds the loose end and steps free.
- **M5 — Redirect the Pressure:** A training partner pushes forward; the student pivots and redirects the pressure without losing balance.
- **M6 — Think from a Bad Position:** A student loses position, pauses instead of panicking and works into a controlled reset.
- **M7 — Step onto the Mat:** A nervous beginner changes into training gear, steps onto the mat and greets his partner.
- **M8 — Become the Teacher:** A black belt adjusts a beginner’s grip, then patiently practices the movement alongside him.
- **M9 — The Argument Deflates:** Two people argue with exaggerated storm clouds overhead; one stays calm, and the storm dissipates.
- **M10 — Calm inside the Storm:** Thunder rattles a gym window while two partners continue a controlled training drill.

Suggested starting choice: M3.
