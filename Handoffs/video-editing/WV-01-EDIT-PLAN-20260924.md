## 3. The viewer's path through the sale

By the end of each stage, the viewer should be able to answer one question:

1. **Opening:** Can this help me get real abs, and why should I keep watching?
2. **Founder story:** Does this person understand why someone like me struggles?
3. **Mechanism:** What changed beyond getting another generic workout plan?
4. **Five examples:** What does that actually do for my motivation, training, food and recovery?
5. **App reveal:** Can I get this help without learning prompting or managing several AI tools?
6. **Walkthrough:** What will I actually receive and use?
7. **Offer:** What do I do now, what does it cost, and what happens next?
8. **Close:** Is trying this worth doing today?

The edit should move the viewer through those questions. A beautiful insert that does not help the current question is lower priority than a clear demonstration that does.

Do not remove every repetition. Distinguish three things: a failed take, an accidental repeated sentence, and deliberate selling reinforcement. Remove the first two. Keep the third when it adds proof, makes a benefit personal, answers an objection or advances the close. The recorded founder story and concrete examples matter more than achieving an arbitrary short runtime.

## 6. Editing style specification

### Performance and cutting

Choose the strongest delivery of each thought. A clean sentence with conviction is better than a stitched sentence assembled from individually perfect words. Preserve natural emphasis, small pauses before admissions and the emotional close. Remove production talk, restarts, dead air and duplicated phrases.

Use fixed NEAR and FAR compositions, anchored to the hairline with comfortable headroom. Cuts between framing sizes should land on a thought change, emphasis or repair. Horizontal footage does not track, drift or slowly zoom. Do not use a new crop merely because a timer elapsed.

Starting rhythm: Dan may hold for a complete 6-15 second thought; a specific illustration may hold 3-7 seconds; an app action and its result may need 7-12 seconds. These are planning ranges, not pass/fail rules or measurements of the competitor. Vary them around comprehension and emotion. A visual change must reveal something or strengthen the argument.

### Graphics

Use the accepted Muhammad-inspired components from the Codex recipe registry: bold readable type, clean hierarchy, restrained blue/white accents, consistent rounded cards and concise motion. Rebuild with the accepted components rather than extracting finished graphic pixels from Muhammad's export.

Use four reusable graphic families only: benefit/chapter label, week/support diagram, real app demonstration frame, and offer/CTA card. Photo disclosure chips are a separate required layer. The entire film should feel like one presentation.

Write one idea per screen. A headline normally contains two to six words. Use large type suitable for a 390-pixel-wide phone view. At a 1920-pixel master width, a 96-pixel headline displays at roughly 20 pixels when the entire video is 390 pixels wide. Test the actual rendered result, not the editing monitor.

Do not let headline text, disclosure chips and app controls compete for the same space. For app demonstrations, crop to the relevant action or result. A full tiny phone UI inside a wide frame is insufficient proof if the viewer cannot read it.

### Captions and website rules

The explicitly requested organic Codex adapter delivers SRT subtitles without running burned captions. Use that as this plan's default, plus selective burned emphasis text, required photo/AI labels and readable app-result labels. V Shred's more persistent text is an observed reference, not an instruction to override Dan's chosen workflow.

The website skill contains a legacy burned-caption expectation. Record this format decision in the next task's manifest before build. Run the current website delivery gate and document the applicable caption mode honestly. Do not fabricate a caption pass or silently change thresholds. If the current gate cannot express the chosen mode, resolve its configuration before rendering a full film. This is a technical compatibility check, not a request to rewrite the creative brief.

### Sound and music

Dan's voice is the main soundtrack. Measure the lav on every used source. Follow `pick_lav.py`, `voice_chain.py` and `audio_gate.py`; do not reuse an old microphone map blindly. Use gentle dereverb if the measured room requires it. Do not hide poor speech clarity with music.

If music helps, use one restrained instrumental bed, with a subtle lift into the dream/close. No whooshes, swipe sounds, impact hits on every text change or abrupt track changes. The quiet personal admission and nightmare passage can be dry voice. The exact final mix must pass the shared audio requirements. The website reference's target is approximately -14 LUFS with true peak no higher than -2.5 dBTP; the current shared gate is authoritative.

### Color and source quality

The September 23 Sony FX30 footage is S-Cinetone Rec.709, 4K, 29.97 fps. Do not apply the older log LUT. The footage report identifies dark, soft material, glasses reflections, a visible lav and distracting edge objects. Grade for clear skin and readable eyes while preserving texture. Avoid aggressive sharpening, crushed blacks and orange skin.

Compare new-source grade stills against approved reference stills decoded consistently as BT.709. Test both framing sizes and representative head movement. Protect hair throughout. A polished sample is not permission to assume the full film is safe; check the final delivered frames and joins.
