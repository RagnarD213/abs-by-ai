### B. Eat a Salad First to Eat Less

Type: food rule. Source: 5:34.932 - 6:21.891. Expected runtime: 47 seconds.

One meal-order tactic, the study, an explanation and alternatives.

Opening: Start every meal with a big serving of something low in calories.

First five seconds / visual plan: Open on the finished salad; show salad before the main meal and retain the study visual in a portrait layout.

Verbatim retained source transcript (existing word sidecar, not a fresh listening verification):

> Start every meal with a big serving of something low in calories. Researchers at Penn State tested this. They gave people a big, low calorie salad before their meal, and then they let them eat as much of the main course as they wanted. Those people ate about 12% fewer calories for the whole meal, even counting the salad. And they didn't feel any less full. Let me explain to you why that works. Your stomach gets full based on how much food is in it, and it has no idea how many calories that food has. So if you fill it up with 3 cups of salad for 100 calories, there's just less room left for the pasta. That's the reason why I break my fast with a salad. You can also do this with vegetables or a broth based soup. Eat that first, then eat your real meal. You'll eat less and you won't feel like you're on a diet at all.

### C. Stop Drinking Your Calories

Type: food rule. Source: 7:03.272 - 7:04.991 + 7:09.212 - 7:56.811. Expected runtime: 50 seconds.

The jelly-beans-versus-soda comparison leads to specific drink swaps.

Opening: Stop drinking liquid calories.

Verbatim retained source transcript (existing word sidecar, not a fresh listening verification):

> Stop drinking liquid calories.

> Researchers at Purdue gave people 450 extra calories per day. For four weeks, they got it as jelly beans. And for another four weeks, they got it as soda. When they ate the jelly beans, they ate less food the rest of the day to make up for it. And they didn't gain weight. When they drank the soda, they didn't eat any less at all. They just added it on top of everything else. And they gained weight. So your body doesn't count liquid calories. That means soda, juice, sweet tea, the coffee drinks at Starbucks, and beer. A lot of guys are drinking 500 to 1,000 calories a day. And none of it is filling them up. If it has calories, don't drink it. Drink water, black coffee, and zero calorie drinks. For a lot of you guys, this one change is enough to start losing your belly fat all by itself.
