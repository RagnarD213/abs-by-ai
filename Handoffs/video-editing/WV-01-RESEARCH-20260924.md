## Decisions carried into the editing plan

1. Keep Dan as the principal salesperson. Use graphics and demonstrations to strengthen his point.
2. Get real-result proof into the opening, then preserve the admission that makes the story relatable.
3. Make each of the five benefits visually different because each solves a different viewer problem.
4. Preserve B's three-hours-versus-165-hours argument and connect its six pickups to the shared body.
5. Use real product interaction where belief depends on seeing the app do the job.
6. Show the offer clearly and let the emotional close finish. Do not add a free photo-generation detour.
7. Keep A and B as complete message approaches with consistent production and offer conditions.
8. Judge the eventual traffic test on cost per trial and cost per paying customer. Retention helps diagnose the video; it does not establish profit.
