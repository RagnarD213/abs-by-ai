## What both outlines share

**The big idea (ours, not theirs):** the "healthy" diet men were sold, low-fat, egg whites, sugar dressed up as health food, starves the body of what it builds testosterone from. Fix the food first; then give the body the one thing food can't (tongkat ali) and the mineral most men run short on (zinc).

- TestoGreens' idea is estrogen: an enzyme "steals" testosterone. V Shred's is chemicals: pesticides, plastics, cortisol, a "male hormone matrix." **Ours is the diet itself**, which is Dan's home turf as the fat-loss and abs guy.
- **Name for the mechanism (pick one):** "Starved T" / "the Starved Testosterone Diet" / "the Lean Diet Trap." Something we can say every few minutes the way they repeat "the thief" and "the matrix."

**Food claims we can stand behind (every food on screen gets its study card):**

| Food | What the study found | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Low-fat diets | Testosterone 10 to 15% lower on low-fat than higher-fat diets (6 studies, 206 men) | Whittaker & Wu 2021, J Steroid Biochem Mol Biol |
| Egg whites instead of whole eggs | 30 lifting men, 12 weeks: the whole-egg group had a bigger testosterone rise and lost more body fat than the egg-white group | Bagheri 2021, J Strength Cond Res |
| A big sugar hit (75 g) | Testosterone dropped about 25% and stayed down at 2 hours, in 74 men | Caronia 2013, Clinical Endocrinology |
| Licorice | 7 g a day dropped testosterone in healthy young men within a week (26% in the larger follow-up) | Armanini 1999 NEJM; 2003 follow-up |
| Zinc | Zinc-restricted young men: testosterone fell about 75% over 20 weeks. Zinc-short older men given zinc: testosterone roughly doubled | Prasad 1996, Nutrition |

**What 75 g of sugar looks like:** a 16 oz orange juice (about 45 g) plus a cup of granola with flavored yogurt (about 35 g), the classic "healthy breakfast." Or two 20 oz sports drinks.

**Ingredient claims we can stand behind:**
- **Tongkat ali:** 105 men 50 to 70 with low testosterone, 12 weeks, testosterone up vs placebo (Physta extract, 100 to 200 mg; PubMed 34262417). Stressed adults, 200 mg for 4 weeks: cortisol down 16%, testosterone up 37% (Talbott 2013). Meta-analysis: improves total testosterone in men (2022, PMC9415500).
- **Zinc:** Prasad above.
- **Maca:** sold on drive. The research supports desire, not testosterone, so say "drive."
