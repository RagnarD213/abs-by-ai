# 4. Turn the VSL into a mobile sales page
Owner: Claude Opus 5.5 high, with Dan reviewing the words and phone flow. Inputs: approved VSL and offer brief. Output: text-page.md plus a private, phone-first preview or clearly labeled wireframe. Read Docs/DESIGN_QUALITY_LESSONS.md before design. Do not replace the live store or send traffic as part of planning.

Write a standalone text version a visitor can understand without playing the video. Start with the same question as the approved lead. First phone screen at 390px: identify the product and why the reader should continue. Then give the useful explanation, supporting proof with captions, actual bottle/tablet and label, who it suits, pack options and total price, shipping, actual guarantee conditions, objections and purchase link. Keep subscription terms explicit if offered. Do not paste a transcript or invent testimonials. An ad that invites viewers to watch must arrive at the VSL; provide the text route without making customers hunt for it.

Make a mapping from each material VSL assertion and term to its page section and to checkout. Show the phone version first, followed by 1440px desktop. Verify the current product links including selected packs, mobile CTA and total at checkout. Distinguish preview from published or tested. Finish when Dan can read the page on a phone and the VSL, text and actual checkout make the same offer.
