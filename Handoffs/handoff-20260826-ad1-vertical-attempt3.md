## THE REVISIONS

Numbered so Dan can refer to them. **1–4 are the ones that decide whether this passes.**

### 1. The captions are broken. **[diagnosed — fix is exact]**

Dan: *"Some words are displaying on a different line than others."* He is right, and the
cause is a bug I fixed in `vlib.draw_type` during attempt 2 and **failed to fix in the
identical code in `captions.py`**.

`captions.py` draws each WORD separately with `anchor="lt"`. PIL's `t` anchor is the
ascender line **of the string it is given** — so for a single word it is that word's own
tallest glyph. A word with an ascender ("single", "day", "for") sits high; a word with
none ("more", "you", "was", "your") is pushed DOWN by the ascender-to-x-height gap. That
is exactly the stagger in his screenshot.

Two more bugs in the same eight lines:

* the drop shadow is drawn from the WHOLE string at `"lt"`, so it is laid out on a
  different baseline from the words it is meant to sit under;
* `cx += text_size(ww + ' ', F)[0]` advances by the INK width — `getbbox` ignores a
  trailing space — so the words progressively crowd together and the line drifts left of
  its own shadow.

**Fix:** draw every word AND the shadow with `anchor="ls"` at `CAP_Y + F.getmetrics()[0]`,
and advance with `F.getlength(ww + ' ')` (the real advance width), not `text_size`.
Then render one caption PNG at full 1080 and LOOK at it before rebuilding the whole track.
