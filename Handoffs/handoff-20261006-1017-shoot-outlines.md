## The format (copy Dan's, make it tighter)

Dan's two outlines ("I'm 41. I wasted my life." and "How To Achieve A Life Better Than 99% Of Men") set the format.
He dictated them in Wispr Flow, so they ramble. Keep his structure, cut the rambling:

- **Bold Title Case title**, then real nested bullets (Google Docs bullets with indent levels, never flat dashes).
- **Intro** with 2 or 3 sub-bullets: the strongest first-person claim (the hook), "In today's video, I want to show
  you...", and "Watch this video if you want...".
- **Body sections** as top-level bullets with short labels. Sub-bullets are first-person lines or plain directions
  ("Explain how...", "Tell them...").
- **An "Action step" sub-bullet at the end of each section.** Dan likes every section to end on what the viewer does.
- **Graphics and footage cues in bracketed caps** as their own nested bullet: `[SHOW FULL SCREEN GRAPHIC ...]`,
  `[AI GENERATED IMAGE OF ME AT 25% BODY FAT]`, `[PLAY CLIP: ...]`.
- **Evidence filled in, not left as placeholders.** Dan's own outlines say `[CLAUDE - INSERT BRIEF EVIDENCE ...]`; ours
  carry the actual study: what it found as one big plain number, plus the link. Real studies only.
- **A review section** ("Review the N ... and give one action step for each [FULL SCREEN GRAPHIC]") where the video is a
  list, then **Wrap up** (subscribe, or tease the next video when two videos pair).
- **Tight:** one sentence per bullet, ideally under 25 words. Long-form outlines about 250 to 450 words. Shorts about 80
  to 150 words with a hook bullet, at most three beats, and a bottom line; they must fit 176 to 185 spoken words later.
- **Workout outlines** add an equipment line at the top, the timing (Dan's Gymboss method: 30 seconds work, 30 rest,
  unless the workout needs another split), each exercise with 2 or 3 form cues as sub-bullets, a beginner scaling
  note, and `[SHOW TIMER ON SCREEN]` cues.
- **Reaction outlines:** each beat is `[PLAY CLIP: creator, video title, URL, mm:ss to mm:ss]` (10 to 30 seconds), then
  "My take" sub-bullets, then an action step. Only real clips with checked URLs and timestamps. If one cannot be
  found, write `[CLAUDE - FIND CLIP: what it should show]` instead of guessing.
- No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere (`grep -c` must be 0). Titles are Dan's picks below; sharpen wording only if
  it keeps the idea.
