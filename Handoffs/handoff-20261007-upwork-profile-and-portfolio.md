Round 1: experience and positioning.
- What work has he done for SixPackAbs.com and clients? Dates, brands, responsibilities, strongest results.
- Which jobs does he want to sell, and which does he dislike or want to exclude?
- Does he prefer fitness brands, coaches, agencies, or a wider adjacent market?
- What work did he personally script, direct, film, edit, or supervise?

Round 2: evidence and assets.
- Ask for links/files to his strongest client and SixPackAbs.com ads and the relevant Abs By AI examples.
- Ask what made each successful, what evidence is available, and what can be shown publicly.
- Identify client quotations available for accurate use in case studies, without promising an Upwork testimonial-request feature.
- Ask which portrait and introduction-video approach he prefers. Existing headshots can be reviewed before proposing new photography.

Round 3: commercial fit.
- Weekly capacity, preferred project size, availability, starting rate, turnaround and revision boundaries.
- What production can the present workflow deliver reliably? Treat historical asset quality and present execution capability as different questions.
