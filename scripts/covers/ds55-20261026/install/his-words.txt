If I only had 5 minutes to work out at home, these are the 3 exercises I do. Together, they hit almost every muscle that you have. Number 1, body weight squats. These work your legs and your glutes, the biggest muscles in your body. Sit back like there's a chair behind you, then stand up tall. Number two, push-ups. They work your chest, shoulders, and arms. Keep your body in a straight line. Bring your chest all the way down. If that's too hard, do it from your knees. Number three, Spider-Man Planks. They work your abs and the deep muscle that pulls your waist in. Get in a plank and then go to a push-up position on one side, then the other, and then switch sides for your next set. Do each one for thirty seconds, rest thirty seconds, then cycle through all three until five minutes are up. Five minutes every day, beats an hour once a week. Start with 5 minutes every morning.
