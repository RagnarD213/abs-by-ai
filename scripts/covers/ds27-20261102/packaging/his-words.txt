If you're too busy to work out, cancel your gym membership. You'll get in shape faster at home, and it'll cost you less than two months at the gym. Listen, the workout isn't what's eating your time. The commute is. You pack a bag, drive, park, change, workout, and drive back. If you're doing a short 30-minute workout, driving 15 minutes each way doubles your workout time. At home, there's no commute. You roll out of bed and start. And you only need six things. a yoga mat, push-up handles, light dumbbells, a kettlebell, an ab wheel, and a jump rope. All of it costs $100 and you're seeing it on screen right now. I'll keep it real with you. I pay for a gym myself because I train 7 days a week and the drive is already a habit. But if time is what's stopping you, start with short daily workouts at home. Once working out every day is a habit, you can always join a gym later.
