Hamstring curls are the key to building well-balanced aesthetic legs. Here's how to do them.

First, line your knees up with the hinge of the machine. Then set the range so you feel a stretch in your hamstrings at the bottom, without letting your legs hyperextend. Pull your toes towards you on every rep. Curl the weight as far as it goes, squeeze for a second at the top, then lower it slowly. Start with one really easy warm-up set of 10 to 15 reps. Then do two working sets of 8 to 12 reps. Every working rep should be strict. Do as much weight as you can for as many reps as you can, and stop once you can't do another clean rep. Take two to three seconds on the way down, then explode up.
