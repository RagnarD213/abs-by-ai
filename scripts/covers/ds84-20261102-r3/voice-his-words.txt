hamstring curls are the key to building well-balanced aesthetic legs. Here's how to do them. First, line your knees up with the hinge of the machine. Second, set the range of motion so that way you start with the stretch. You wanna feel a stretch in your hamstrings at the bottom, but not so much that your legs hyper extend. Curl your toes towards you to flex your hamstrings. Pulling your toes towards you is what you want on every rep. Then curl the weight as far as it goes and then squeeze for a second at the top. Then lower it slowly. For your sets, start with one really easy warm-up set of 10 to 15 reps. Then do two working sets of eight to 12 reps. Every working rep should be strict. Do as much weight as you can for as many reps as you can and then stop once you can't do another clean rep. Take two to three seconds on the way down and then explode up.
