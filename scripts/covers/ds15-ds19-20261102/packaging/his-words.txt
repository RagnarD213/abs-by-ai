Does jujitsu get you abs? I'm a Brazilian jujitsu black belt who's been training for 22 years, and I've had a lot of people asking me if jujitsu is a good way to get abs. Jujitsu does give you a great ab workout, both with the conditioning exercises we do and with wrestling live rounds, but it might be the hardest possible way to get abs. Ab training is a great bonus if you love jujitsu, but if you just want abs and you don't care about wrestling, gym ab exercises do the job faster. Also, remember that nutrition is 80% of getting abs whether you do jujitsu or not. You can't out train a bad diet, not even with jujitsu training. So if you want to get abs, you should be mainly focused on how you're eating, not how you're training. So if you want abs, focus on your nutrition. But if you love jujitsu, burning extra calories and getting in a great ab workout is a good bonus to the sport.

Battle ropes are an incredible total body cardio workout. Here's how to use them. Loop the ropes around a pillar or tree. Stand at the end of the ropes and get in your starting stance. Have your feet shoulder width apart and a slight bend in your legs and grab the rope with both hands. Do alternating waves. If you want a less intense workout, focus on your upper body. Hold one end of the rope in each hand. Move your arms up and down in an alternating whip-like motion to send continuous waves towards the anchor. If you want a more intense total body workout, try two arm slams. Lift both arms overhead and slam the ropes down onto the floor using your entire body. I love battle ropes for a great home cardio workout. You can set them up anywhere and you can get in a great cardio workout in a matter of minutes.
