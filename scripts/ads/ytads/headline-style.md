# Headline style — placeholder until Phase 0

This file is rewritten from the real ads in the three Demand Gen campaigns (Phase 0 of the
handoff). Until then the generator falls back to these conservative shapes:

- Plain sentence case, no hype, 3–6 words for a headline.
- The headline names what the video shows: an exercise, a habit, a tool, a mistake.
- "AI" is the product's word; "Abs by AI" is the brand.
- No promise about the viewer's body, ever.
