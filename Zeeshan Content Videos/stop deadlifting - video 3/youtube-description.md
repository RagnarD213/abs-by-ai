Deadlifts are the fastest way to pack on muscle. That was never the question. The problem is the risk: looking back at my own training, deadlifts caused more of my injuries than every other lift combined, and an injury costs you months of progress, not days. Past 40 you heal slower, and one bad injury is how most guys end up quitting the gym for good. Deadlifts also build a thick powerlifter body, not the lean, aesthetic look most of us are after.

What I do instead: T-bar rows or barbell rows with a wide grip, wide-grip lat pulldowns for back width, leg presses with the safety stops set, and rear delt flys for posture and an aesthetic back. Nearly the same results, with almost none of the risk.
