GUIDELINE 1.1 - PLEASE RECONSIDER: THIS IS A FITNESS COACHING APP, NOT A BODY EDITOR. We support Apple's action against apps that alter photos of OTHER people - the harassment and body-shaming vector. Abs by AI is not that: (1) SELF-ONLY BY DESIGN - a user visualizes their own fitness goal from their own photo; there is no way to browse, receive or alter anyone else's image, and generated images stay private to the account that made them. The pipeline hard-refuses photos of apparent minors and moderates inputs and outputs. (2) EXPLICIT CONSENT - a screen names every AI provider before any photo is sent; declining cancels the action. (3) LABELED AS ILLUSTRATIVE AI - every image is presented as an AI-generated goal image, "illustrative goals, not guarantees", never a claim about the user's body. (4) THE PRODUCT IS COACHING - AI Trainer, AI Nutritionist, macro tracking, sleep coaching, supplement review, progress logging, daily brief. The goal image is step one of a program, not the product. This version's metadata was revised so that is unmistakable.

WHAT IT DOES / AUDIENCE: a fitness motivation and planning app for adults, primarily men aged 25-55, who want to lose body fat. The user uploads a photo, the app generates an AI image of how they could look at their goal, then builds the plan to get there. A printed poster or canvas of the goal image can also be ordered.

MAIN FLOW: home > Add your photo > choose options > Generate my goal image. Then "Continue to my hub" for AI Trainer, AI Nutritionist, Macro Tracker, Sleep Coach, Supplement Audit, Daily Brief, Progress Log. No sample files or setup needed.

ACCOUNT DELETION: Member Hub > Account > Delete my account. Permanent and immediate.

DEVICES TESTED: physical iPhone 17 Pro on iOS 26 via TestFlight; iOS 26.5 simulator across iPhone 17 Pro/Pro Max/17e/Air and iPad Pro 13-inch, iPad Air 11-inch, iPad mini.

EXTERNAL SERVICES (all processors for us; no data broker or ad network receives user data): Anthropic (Claude) checks the photo and writes the plans; Google (Gemini), and Replicate running ByteDance Seedream and Black Forest Labs FLUX, generate the image; Apple StoreKit with RevenueCat handles subscriptions and server-side entitlements; Stripe takes payment for printed goods only; Printify ships them; Resend sends account email; PostHog is analytics; Railway hosts our backend and database in the US.
